## Commander-first plan

Anhelo grants casualty 2 to the first instant or sorcery spell cast each turn. The deck needs a creature with power 2 or greater to sacrifice, then maximizes spell-copy triggers, magecraft, copied removal, token production and graveyard reuse. Its main finishers are copied high-impact spells and large token boards; its conditional Reiterate/Mana Geyser/Ral Storm Conduit line remains dependent on sufficient mana and pieces, not an unconditional claim.

## Changes

The two previous-cycle changes were re-evaluated and retained:
- **Deekah, Fractal Theorist for Talrand, Sky Summoner:** Deekah triggers on casting or copying instant/sorcery spells, so Anhelo's copy also makes a Fractal. Its tokens scale with spell mana value; the trade-off is a five-mana engine and non-flying tokens.
- **Make an Example for Talrand's Invocation:** copied Make an Example forces each opponent to split their current creatures into piles, then you choose which pile they sacrifice. This is a higher-impact copied removal effect than four-mana token production, though it does not build your own board.

Three additional changes were selected:
- **Dogged Detective for Ghoulish Procession:** a 2-power casualty body that surveils two on entry and can return from the graveyard to hand when an opponent draws their second card each turn. Ghoulish Procession was a reliable once-per-turn token engine, but the deck already has several token makers; Detective adds a reusable power-2 body and graveyard setup.
- **Startle for Preordain:** Startle costs {1}{U}, draws a card and creates a 2/2 Zombie with decayed, while giving a creature -2/-0. When copied by Anhelo, it draws two cards and creates two Zombies, increasing casualty fodder. The trade-off is losing Preordain's one-mana scry-two selection and paying one extra mana.
- **Waste Management for Maestros Charm:** this instant can exile up to two cards from a graveyard and creates a 2/2 Rogue for each creature card exiled. If kicked, it instead exiles a target player's graveyard. The official ruling confirms a copy of a kicked spell is also kicked, so Anhelo can exile two graveyards and create a large Rogue board. The trade-off is losing Maestros Charm's flexible selection/recursion/removal modes and relying on graveyard targets.

## Casualty and rules verification

- Anhelo's casualty 2 applies to the first instant or sorcery you cast each turn, including opponents' turns. It requires sacrificing a creature with power 2 or greater as an additional cost; a sacrificed creature cannot pay two separate additional costs for both Anhelo's casualty and a spell's own casualty.
- Dogged Detective is a 2/1. Its ETB surveils two; if it is in your graveyard when an opponent draws their second card in a turn, it can return to hand. It does not need to be in the graveyard for that opponent's first draw.
- Startle creates a 2/2 Zombie with decayed and draws a card. The copied spell can choose a new target, and both copies resolve independently; the decayed tokens are useful casualty fodder but cannot block and are sacrificed after attacking.
- Waste Management's kicker cost is {3}{B} in addition to {2}{B}. If kicked, its copy is also kicked under the official ruling. Each kicked resolution can exile a different player's graveyard and creates a 2/2 Rogue for each creature card exiled.
- Deekah's magecraft trigger occurs when an instant/sorcery is cast or copied; the copy from Anhelo can create another Fractal. Talrand's ability triggered on casting, not copying, which is why Deekah is preferred.
- Make an Example copied by Anhelo resolves separately; each opponent makes a new split of creatures still controlled at that resolution, and you choose a pile for them to sacrifice.
- The previous conditional Reiterate/Mana Geyser/Ral Storm Conduit line remains conditional on sufficient mana and the needed pieces; no unconditional infinite-combo claim is made.

## Candidate review

The full candidate ledger records 30+ commander-specific options, including Dogged Detective, Sinister Concierge, Squee, the Immortal, Bloodsoaked Champion, Startle, Rotten Reunion, Waste Management, Skyclave Shade, Timeline Culler, Maestros Confluence, Mystic Confluence, Exsanguinate, Metallurgic Summonings, River's Rebuke, Brass's Bounty, Drawn from Dreams, Tragic Slip, Cruel Ultimatum, Fact or Fiction, Ponder, Clone Legion, Dread Summons, Syphon Mind, Pirate's Pillage, Resonating Lute, Bedevil, Jaya's Immolating Inferno and others.

- **Dogged Detective accepted:** low-cost power-2 body, surveil two, and conditional graveyard-to-hand recursion supports repeated casualty turns.
- **Sinister Concierge rejected:** its death effect suspends itself and a target creature for three upkeeps, so removal is delayed and the creature returns; the current deck has sufficient fodder and more direct answers.
- **Squee, the Immortal rejected:** reliable recurring body, but casting it from the graveyard costs three mana; Dogged Detective's return can be free when its trigger condition occurs.
- **Bloodsoaked Champion rejected:** repeatable casualty fodder after attacking, but requires a combat step and {1}{B}; current power-2 bodies and token engines are sufficient after adding Dogged Detective.
- **Rotten Reunion rejected:** efficient graveyard hate plus one decayed Zombie, but Startle adds a card draw and token, while Waste Management has a higher ceiling as a copied graveyard-hate/token spell.
- **Maestros Confluence rejected:** six mana is expensive and its modal effects overlap the existing removal/recursion suite.
- **Exsanguinate / Cruel Ultimatum rejected:** strong top-end spells but overlap Torment of Hailfire, Crackle with Power and the existing copied-spell finishers.
- **Metallurgic Summonings / Clone Legion rejected:** high mana-value token/value engines are slower or more redundant than the existing Deekah, Poppet Stitcher, Judith, Baral and Kari Zev, Lagomos and Army of the Damned package.
- **Skyclave Shade / Timeline Culler rejected:** repeatable graveyard bodies are useful, but Dogged Detective and Bloodghast already support graveyard-to-hand/battlefield access, and the selected new creature is better tied to casualty.
- **Waste Management accepted:** flexible low-cost graveyard hate at base cost; when kicked and copied, it can clear two graveyards and create a large board. This is a high-ceiling option, not a claim that seven mana is always efficient.
