## Commander-first plan

Admiral Brass, Unsinkable mills four cards on entry, then at the beginning of combat on your turn may return a Pirate creature card from your graveyard with a finality counter, base 4/4 and haste. The deck must stock the graveyard, choose Pirates with high-impact ETBs/attack abilities, exploit the temporary 4/4 haste body, and mitigate the finality counter so valuable Pirates can return to the graveyard instead of being exiled. Roaming Throne naming Pirate can double Admiral's combat trigger and other Pirate triggers.

## Five additions and five cuts

| Add | Cut | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Power Conduit** | Dire Fleet Daredevil | Removes a finality counter as part of its activation cost, then adds a +1/+1 counter to a creature or charge counter to an artifact. This directly enables reuse of commander-reanimated Pirates; Daredevil is useful but incidental opponent-graveyard value. |
| **Nesting Grounds** | Crumbling Necropolis | Utility land can move a finality counter off a valuable reanimated Pirate onto an expendable permanent. The tapped tri-land's colored fixing is lost, but the deck retains 34 lands and strong UBR fixing. Activation is sorcery-only. |
| **Kitesail Larcenist** | Amphin Mutineer | Three-mana flying Pirate that can turn up to one artifact/creature per player into a Treasure while it remains in play. It is cheaper and can disrupt multiple players; opponents may sacrifice Treasures for mana, so this is temporary conversion, not unconditional exile. |
| **Timestream Navigator** | The Indomitable | A two-mana Pirate that can be reanimated with haste and, with the City's Blessing and four mana, activate its extra-turn ability. The Indomitable's recurring draw body is strong, but its draw is mandatory and it is not a Pirate; this list already has several draw engines. |
| **Dire Fleet Ravager** | Angrath's Marauders | Five-mana Pirate with menace/deathtouch and an ETB that makes each player lose one-third of their life, rounded up. It is an immediate reanimation payoff and Roaming Throne can double the ETB trigger. It is symmetric and can dangerously reduce your own life, but is more directly aligned with repeatable ETB reanimation than a seven-mana damage doubler. |

## Finality-counter engine and rules

- Power Conduit can remove the finality counter as an activation cost and put a +1/+1 counter on that same creature, allowing it to go to the graveyard normally if it later dies.
- Nesting Grounds can move the finality counter from a permanent you control onto a second target permanent; the second target need not be controlled by you. It is sorcery-speed and costs {1} plus tapping.
- Finality counters apply to any permanent. If the counter remains on a reanimated Pirate when it would die, it is exiled instead of going to the graveyard.
- Roaming Throne naming Pirate can double Admiral Brass's beginning-of-combat trigger. To reanimate two different Pirates, the two triggers need different valid graveyard targets; a second trigger targeting the same card becomes illegal after the first moves it.
- Roaming Throne can also double Dire Fleet Ravager's ETB and Skeleton Crew's triggered ability. Ravager's two life-loss triggers resolve separately: from 40 life, a player loses 14 then 9 (40→26→17). This includes you; do not use it when your own life total cannot absorb the loss.
- Skeleton Crew triggers when one or more creature cards leave your graveyard and creates a 2/2 Skeleton Pirate token. If Roaming Throne names Pirate, its trigger can also be doubled.
- Timestream Navigator costs {2}{U}{U}, taps and goes to the bottom of its owner's library to take an extra turn; it requires the City's Blessing. It is not an infinite loop by itself because it leaves the battlefield for the library and must be milled/discarded again to be reanimated.
- Kitesail Larcenist's selected permanents become only Treasure artifacts and lose other abilities while Larcenist remains on the battlefield. If it leaves before the ETB ability resolves, the effect does nothing.

## Candidate review

The full-list matrix covers all 97 baseline names plus the five new candidates. Candidate decisions include Power Conduit, Nesting Grounds, Kitesail Larcenist, Timestream Navigator, Dire Fleet Ravager, The Indomitable, Angrath's Marauders, The Grim Captain's Locker, Storm Fleet Negotiator, Port of Karfell, Nephalia Drownyard, King Narfi's Betrayal, Vanquisher's Banner, Bident of Thassa, Arm-Mounted Anchor, Captivating Crew, Blood Money, Prismari Command, Icon of Ancestry, Spectral Sailor, Mary Read and Anne Bonny, Daring Saboteur, Evacuation, Rakdos Charm, Azure Fleet Admiral, Lethal Scheme, Departed Deckhand, Dire Fleet Neckbreaker, Soul Diviner, Pirated Copy and Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider.

- **Timestream Navigator over The Indomitable:** higher commander-specific ceiling and Pirate density, but needs City Blessing plus four mana, consumes its own attack, and is not repeatable without returning it to the graveyard.
- **Dire Fleet Ravager over Angrath's Marauders:** its ETB is immediately useful on reanimation and can be doubled by Roaming Throne; the trade-off is symmetric life loss. Angrath's Marauders remains a powerful damage doubler, but is a higher-cost static payoff and less synergistic with finality-counter reuse.
- **The Grim Captain's Locker rejected:** four-mana artifact with slow tap/sorcery-speed setup; existing reanimation, self-mill and finality-counter tools are more efficient.
- **Storm Fleet Negotiator rejected:** useful flying Pirate value, but its attack/parley package gives opponents cards and overlaps existing Treasure/card-advantage density.
- **Port of Karfell / Nephalia Drownyard rejected:** tapped or colorless land slots with expensive, slow graveyard setup; Nesting Grounds has more direct synergy with the commander’s finality counter.
- **Vanquisher's Banner / Icon of Ancestry rejected:** tribal draw/anthem effects are plausible, but the deck already has Kindred Discovery, Black Market Connections, Skeleton Crew and Shared Animosity.
- **Bident of Thassa / Arm-Mounted Anchor rejected:** combat draw is already abundant; these do not improve the finality-counter/reanimation engine as much as the selected changes.
- **Captivating Crew rejected:** repeatable temporary theft is on-plan but four mana to cast plus four mana per activation is inefficient.
- **Blood Money / Evacuation / Lethal Scheme rejected:** expensive or redundant removal; the current list retains Living Death, Toxic Deluge, Cyclonic Rift, Chaos Warp and targeted interaction.
- **Mary Read and Anne Bonny / Spectral Sailor / Soul Diviner rejected:** useful loot/draw bodies, but the deck has sufficient card access and wants slots that improve high-impact reanimation or finality-counter reuse.
- **Dire Fleet Neckbreaker rejected:** powerful combat anthem, but Shared Animosity, Corsair Captain and Skeleton Crew already scale the board; Ravager/Navigator improve the distinctive reanimation plan.
- **Dire Fleet Daredevil rejected:** first-strike Pirate with opponent-graveyard value, but its ETB is less impactful than Power Conduit's repeatable counter removal.
- **Amphin Mutineer rejected for Kitesail Larcenist:** Mutineer permanently exiles one non-Salamander creature but gives a 4/3 token; Larcenist is cheaper, flying, and can disable multiple permanents, though opponents can sacrifice the Treasures for mana.
