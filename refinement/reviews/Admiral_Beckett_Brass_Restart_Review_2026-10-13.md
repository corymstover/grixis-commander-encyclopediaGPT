## Commander-first plan

Admiral Beckett Brass rewards combat damage to one opponent from three or more Pirates in the same turn, then steals a nonland permanent at the beginning of the end step. The deck therefore prioritizes Pirate density, evasion and blocker manipulation, anthems/combat scaling, combat-damage draw and Treasure, and protection for the critical attack. Don Andres and Roaming Throne provide a theft/trigger-multiplication secondary engine.

## Final changes — three additions and three cuts

| Add | Cut | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The Indomitable** | Bident of Thassa | A 6/6 trampler that draws on any creature combat damage and can be cast from the graveyard when three tapped Pirates/Vehicles are controlled. This adds resilience. Mandatory draw and loss of Bident's optional draw/force-attacks mode are real trade-offs. |
| **Open into Wonder** | Daring Saboteur | At X=3, it costs five mana, makes three chosen creatures unblockable and draws for each that hits. Target three Pirates to directly satisfy the commander condition. It is a one-shot finisher; Saboteur is cheaper, but its single-creature unblockable activation costs three mana. |
| **Admiral's Order** | Counterspell | Raid can reduce it to one blue mana after attacking, allowing a hard counter during the combat step when the three-Pirate condition is at risk. It costs three mana on opponents' turns versus Counterspell's two; Mana Drain and free protection remain. |

## Full-list reassessment

All 97 baseline unique names were reviewed by role, with three additions and three cuts recorded in the full-list matrix. The retained list keeps 35 lands, high Pirate density, tribal cost reduction, Treasure engines, combat-damage draw, evasion, theft, anthem effects and stack protection. No land changes were justified.

Zephyr Singer was reconsidered rather than carried forward automatically. Its persistent flying counters are useful, but convoke taps the creatures that pay for it, so those creatures normally cannot attack that turn. Cover of Darkness already provides broad repeatable fear, while Open into Wonder directly creates a same-turn three-Pirate connection plus card draw. The latter was selected for its more direct commander payoff despite its five-mana cost and one-shot nature.

## Candidate decisions

The candidate ledger records accepted and rejected alternatives beyond the inherited shortlist. Rankings and EDHREC are discovery signals, not automatic-add rules.

- **The Indomitable over Bident:** recursion and the 6/6 trample body add resilience. Mandatory draw can create deck-out risk, and Bident's optional draw/force-attacks mode is a real loss.
- **Open into Wonder over Daring Saboteur:** five mana at X=3 guarantees three selected Pirates cannot be blocked and draws for each that hits. Cover of Darkness remains; redundancy is intentional because fear can still be blocked by black/artifact creatures.
- **Admiral's Order over Counterspell:** raid makes it a one-blue hard counter after attacking, specifically protecting the combat threshold. It is worse off-turn at three mana, but the list retains Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship and Deflecting Swat.
- **Zephyr Singer rejected for this revision:** persistent evasion requires setup/convoke and is less direct than Open into Wonder for a decisive same-turn trigger.
- **Lookout's Dispersal rejected:** efficient with a Pirate but only counters unless its controller pays four; Admiral's Order is a hard counter for one blue in the key attack turn.
- **Fell Flagship rejected:** overlaps existing anthem effects and requires crew.
- **Gemcutter Buccaneer rejected:** the list already has Treasure engines from Black Market Connections, Captain Lannery Storm, Malcolm, Edward Kenway and Storm the Vault.
- **Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider rejected:** incremental Treasure-based counters do not solve evasion/threshold as directly as Open into Wonder.
- **Dire Fleet Daredevil and Kitesail Freebooter rejected:** useful Pirate utility, but interaction and evasion density are already adequate; the chosen finisher has greater commander-specific impact.

Roaming Throne naming Pirate can double Admiral's end-step trigger; Forerunner of the Coalition supplies Pirate tutoring and drain; Shared Animosity scales wide attacks; Raise the Palisade can be a tribal-sided reset; Port Razer adds combat pressure; Don Andres rewards stolen permanents; Admiral Brass, Unsinkable supplies Pirate reanimation.

## Rules, legality and integrity

- Admiral Beckett Brass checks whether a player was dealt combat damage by three or more Pirates earlier in the turn; the commander need not have been present when the damage occurred.
- Open into Wonder at X=3 costs five mana ({3}{U}{U}); target three Pirates that can hit the same player. It grants unblockability and a draw trigger for each that connects.
- Admiral's Order's raid alternative cost is available after you attacked that turn; it is not one mana on opponents' turns.
- The Indomitable's graveyard permission requires three or more tapped Pirates and/or Vehicles; it does not waive its mana cost or normal timing. Its combat-damage draw is mandatory.
- Multiple draw engines can create large trigger stacks; The Indomitable's mandatory draw can cause deck-out risk if the library is low.
- The prior Scryfall refresh covered the 97 baseline names. Open into Wonder and Admiral's Order were separately checked for current Oracle text, color identity and Commander legality. All final cards fit UBR.
- Official Commander banned-list checkpoint reviewed 2026-10-13; no final-list card is banned and no project exception is used.
