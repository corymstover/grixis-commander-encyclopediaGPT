## Commander-first plan

Ashnod's defining engine copies activated abilities you activate when one or more permanents are sacrificed, except mana abilities. This deck therefore values activated abilities with sacrifice costs, artifact/creature tokens to pay those costs, Rings of Brighthearth and other copy effects, and payoffs for artifact entry/death. The ability copy does not pay the original activation cost again. Mana abilities such as Ashnod's Altar and Krark-Clan Ironworks are not copied by Ashnod's triggered ability, so those outlets remain mana engines rather than double-mana activations from the commander itself.

## Changes

**Retained from prior review:**
- **Oni-Cult Anvil for Commander's Sphere.** Anvil supplies a repeatable artifact-sacrifice ability and a once-per-turn drain/life gain plus Construct token when activated during your turn. Ashnod can copy its activated ability when the artifact sacrifice is paid. Commander's Sphere was generic fixing/draw and did not exploit the commander's defining ability.
- **Stormclaw Rager for Dockside Chef.** Rager's activated ability sacrifices another artifact or creature to draw a card and put a +1/+1 counter on itself. Ashnod copies that nonmana activated ability, so one sacrifice can draw two cards and add two counters. This is more direct commander synergy than the Chef's slower sacrifice-to-draw activation.

**Three new changes:**
- **Legion Extruder for Pyrite Spellbomb.** Extruder turns an artifact sacrifice into damage to each opponent and a 3/1 red Phyrexian Horror token with trample and haste; Ashnod copies the activated ability, so one artifact sacrifice can produce two damage resolutions and two temporary creature tokens. The tokens can be used for other sacrifice abilities before the end step. The trade-off is losing Spellbomb's one-mana, flexible 2-damage or draw mode; the deck retains substantial removal and draw.
- **Weapons Manufacturing for Farid, Enterprising Salvager.** Whenever a nontoken artifact you control enters, Manufacturing creates a Munitions artifact token that deals 2 damage to any target when it leaves the battlefield. The deck casts and recurs many nontoken artifacts and has numerous sacrifice outlets, so this creates scalable artifact fodder and damage without requiring a separate activation each time. The trade-off is losing Farid's repeatable Junk-token activation and artifact-sacrifice counters; the deck retains multiple other sacrifice outlets.
- **Burnished Hart for Wayfarer's Bauble.** Hart's activated ability sacrifices it to put two basic lands onto the battlefield tapped. Ashnod copies the ability, so one activation can fetch four basics rather than Bauble's two. Hart is also an artifact creature that can be recurred by the deck's artifact recursion package. The trade-off is the higher total mana investment and slower setup.

## Rules and interaction verification

- Ashnod's ability copies activated abilities that you activate when you sacrifice one or more permanents; it does not copy mana abilities. The copy does not require paying the original ability's sacrifice cost again.
- Oni-Cult Anvil's activated ability is copyable when you sacrifice an artifact to pay its cost. Its separate triggered ability is limited to once each turn; copying the activated ability does not create extra Anvil triggers beyond that limit.
- Stormclaw Rager's sacrifice activation can be copied, so the original and copy each draw a card and put a +1/+1 counter on Rager. Only one creature/artifact is sacrificed to pay the original cost.
- Legion Extruder's artifact-sacrifice activation is copyable by Ashnod; the copied ability resolves independently and can create another Horror token and damage resolution. The token's delayed sacrifice at the beginning of the next end step does not prevent using it earlier for other abilities.
- Weapons Manufacturing triggers only when a nontoken artifact enters. The Munitions tokens it creates are tokens and do not cause a self-trigger loop. Each Munitions token deals 2 damage to any target when it leaves the battlefield, including when sacrificed.
- Burnished Hart's activated ability searches for up to two basic lands. Ashnod's copy can resolve separately and search for up to two more basics, provided enough legal basics remain in the library.
- Existing engines remain important: Rings of Brighthearth can copy an activated ability for {2}; Altar of Dementia, Blasting Station, Arcbound Ravager, Executioner's Capsule, Pyrite-independent Skullbombs, Grinding Station, Repurposing Bay, Time Sieve and Clan Crafter all have activated abilities or sacrifice costs that must be evaluated according to their exact text. Ashnod does not copy an ability merely because a permanent dies; the relevant activated ability must have been activated and the sacrifice event must trigger Ashnod.

## Candidate review

The full-list matrix covers all 95 frozen baseline names plus five additions. The candidate ledger reviews 30+ commander-specific options, including Trading Post, Surgical Skullbomb, Moonsilver Key, Dragonspark Reactor, Hedron Archive, Eloise, Nephalia Sleuth, Ruthless Knave, Marionette Master, Imskir Iron-Eater, Burnished Hart, Soul-Guide Lantern, Doomed Necromancer, Sage of Lat-Nam, Conjurer's Bauble, Ichor Wellspring, Mindslaver, Daretti, Scrap Savant and Legion Extruder.

- **Legion Extruder accepted:** efficient activated artifact-sacrifice payoff; the copied ability adds damage and two 3/1 temporary bodies, improving both pressure and fodder.
- **Weapons Manufacturing accepted:** passive token generation from nontoken artifact entries scales with the deck's high artifact density; Munitions tokens convert sacrifice into damage.
- **Burnished Hart accepted:** its sacrifice ability is unusually efficient with Ashnod because one activation yields four basic lands; the deck's recursion package can reuse it.
- **Trading Post rejected:** flexible and very compatible with Ashnod, but four mana and tap-limited activations are slower than the selected engines; Farid and existing sacrifice-to-draw/recursion tools already cover many of its modes.
- **Surgical Skullbomb rejected — color identity:** its white activated ability gives it white color identity, so it is not eligible for this Grixis deck despite its attractive graveyard-hate/token mode.
- **Moonsilver Key rejected:** Ashnod could copy its tutor activation, but Kuldotha Forgemaster, Inventors' Fair, Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor and Wayfarer's/Burnished Hart already cover artifact/land access; the final version prioritizes the Hart's larger ramp payoff.
- **Dragonspark Reactor rejected:** strong scalable copied damage, but overlaps the selected Legion Extruder and existing Mayhem Devil, Marionette Apprentice, Disciple of the Vault, Reckless Fireweaver and Walking Ballista; its {4} activation is slower.
- **Hedron Archive rejected:** the copied sacrifice activation could draw four cards, but its four-mana setup is slower than Mind Stone and the existing draw-on-sacrifice package.
- **Sage of Lat-Nam rejected:** its artifact-sacrifice draw ability is on-plan, but Stormclaw Rager, Clan Crafter, Mind Stone, Mnemonic Sphere and Skullbombs already provide draw from sacrificed permanents.
- **Conjurer's Bauble rejected:** can recycle a card and draw when sacrificed, but the deck already has several low-cost Bauble/Skullbomb/Sphere effects and more impactful activated abilities.
- **Doomed Necromancer rejected:** Ashnod can copy its sacrifice-to-reanimate ability, but the deck already has extensive artifact recursion and the creature needs to untap before use.
- **Ruthless Knave rejected:** one creature sacrifice can produce four Treasures with Ashnod, but the deck already has substantial mana generation and prioritizes artifact-based sacrifice engines.
- **Ichor Wellspring rejected for this revision:** good draw on entry/death, but its effect is not itself an activated ability to copy and the list already has strong artifact-sacrifice draw engines.
- **Missy / Imskir Iron-Eater rejected:** powerful death/ETB payoffs, but the deck already has dense artifact entry/death payoffs; neither directly improves the activated-ability-copy plan more than the selected additions.
- **Mindslaver rejected:** a copied sacrifice-to-control-turn ability can be powerful, but its high mana burden and potential lock pattern exceed the deck's intended realistic ceiling; the current plan can close games without leaning on repeated turn-control locks.
