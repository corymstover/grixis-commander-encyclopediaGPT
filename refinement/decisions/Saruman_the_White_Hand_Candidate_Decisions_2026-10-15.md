## Material challengers rejected

- **Fling:** sacrificing the Army is an additional cost, so the Army is gone before Saruman's cast trigger can enlarge it. Soul's Fire keeps the Army and uses its larger post-trigger power.
- **Essence Harvest:** useful Army payoff but sorcery-speed and player-only; Soul's Fire is materially more flexible.
- **Temporal Trespass:** excellent MV 11/delve interaction, but the {U}{U}{U} floor and graveyard competition with Dig Through Time, Treasure Cruise, Mizzix's Mastery, and Summons of Saruman make it less reliable than the selected package.
- **Storm of Saruman:** flavorful and strong, but the copied spell is not cast and therefore does not itself trigger Saruman. A six-mana setup enchantment loses to the direct discounted-high-MV plan.
- **Shark Typhoon:** powerful parallel payoff, but at six mana it shifts the deck toward generic spellslinger tokens rather than improving the Orc Army engine directly.
- **Uncover the Moon-Letters:** attractive 2026 draw engine, but it scales with mana actually spent. That is specifically anti-synergistic with the deck's strongest Saruman exploit: high mana value cast for reduced/alternate costs.
- **Dreadhorde Invasion / March from the Black Gate:** incremental amass engines are slower and less explosive than triggering Saruman repeatedly through spells.
- **Corsairs of Umbar / Great Goblin, Foul-Hearted / Saruman the White / Warg Rider / Gandalf, Goblins' Bane:** additional creatures dilute the noncreature-spell density that actually drives the commander.
- **Dark Ritual:** strong acceleration, but black-only burst does not reliably solve a four-color-symbol commander cost of {1}{U}{B}{R}; the retained rocks and fixing are more stable.
- **Mana Sculpt:** good Wizard-aware counterspell, but redundant beside Mana Drain and Counterspell and does not outperform the retained interaction package.
- **The Red Terror / commander-independent All Will Be One loops:** rejected because forcing compact generic combo lines would raise homogenization without improving Saruman's own amass identity.

## Rules-critical conclusions

- Cost reductions and alternate costs do **not** change mana value. Volcanic Salvo therefore gives Saruman X=12 even if cast for far less than twelve mana; Treasure Cruise remains MV 8 with delve; Force of Will remains MV 5 under its alternate cost.
- For spells with X in their mana cost, chosen X contributes to mana value while the spell is on the stack. This matters for Assault on Osgiliath, Summons of Saruman, and Shatterskull Smashing.
- Soul's Fire and Chandra's Ignition are cast first; Saruman's triggered ability goes on top and resolves first, so the Army is larger when the damage spell resolves.
- Harmonic Prodigy and Wizard's Staff each cause Saruman's triggered ability to trigger an additional time. Veyran does the same when the triggering event is casting/copying an instant or sorcery. These effects are additive, not replacement choices.
- Mauhúr modifies each applicable counter-placement event by adding one additional +1/+1 counter, not one extra counter for each counter that would have been placed.
- The Great Goblin can trigger from each separate applicable counter-placement event. All Will Be One sees the actual number of counters put on the permanent after replacement effects.
- Widespread Brutality's Saruman cast trigger resolves before the spell; Widespread then amasses 2 and the Army deals damage equal to its power to each non-Army creature. The Army does not damage itself.
- Mizzix's Mastery copies cards in exile and permits casting those copies; the copied cards actually cast as noncreature spells trigger Saruman.
- MDFC land backs are played as lands and do not trigger Saruman; their front faces, when cast, are noncreature spells and do.
