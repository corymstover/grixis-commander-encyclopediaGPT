## Accepted and implemented

| Addition | Cut | Commander-specific rationale | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deekah, Fractal Theorist | Talrand, Sky Summoner | Deekah’s Magecraft triggers from both the cast and Anhelo’s casualty copy, making two mana-value-sized Fractals for the first casualty-2 spell. Those tokens can become later casualty fodder. | Five mana rather than four; Fractals have no flying. Talrand’s repeated flying Drake production is valuable and is deliberately surrendered for direct copy-synergy. |
| Make an Example | Talrand’s Invocation | Copyable non-targeted creature-sacrifice interaction. Each resolution has every opponent split their current creatures into piles, then you choose a pile for each to sacrifice. Anhelo’s copy resolves before the original and the second set of piles is made from what remains. | Cuts a strong copied token spell that can make four flying Drakes. The deck still has Army of the Damned and multiple other token/fodder engines, but Make an Example is less useful against creature-light boards. |
