## Accepted changes

- **Force of Will:** free protection for Tetsuo's attack/combat turns.
- **Withering Torment:** instant-speed creature/enchantment interaction.
- **Leyline Axe:** MV4 raises Tetsuo's casting/damage ceiling while supplying double strike and trample; can begin on the battlefield from the opening hand.
- **Swiftfoot Boots:** tutor-able haste/hexproof makes commander recasts immediately functional.
- **Oathkeeper, Takeno's Daisho:** Tetsuo is a Samurai, so the death-return trigger directly protects the commander from ordinary destruction.
- **Savage Beating:** combat-timing-compatible extra combat; Tetsuo can cast it from hand during his attack trigger when the Equipment ceiling is at least five.
- **World at War:** combat-time casting still schedules a later extra combat after the second main phase; rebound is available because Tetsuo casts it from hand.
- **Volcanic Island / Mana Confluence / Flooded Strand / Marsh Flats / Misty Rainforest:** premium untapped fixing while keeping 35 lands.

## Rejected material challengers

- **Sword of Wealth and Power:** real power and current optimized usage, but protection from instants/sorceries can make equipped Tetsuo an illegal target for Chandra's Ignition. The accepted Equipment package improves reliability without creating that tension.
- **Temporal Manipulation / Time Warp:** raw power is high, but extra-combat replacements better preserve Tetsuo's own combat identity.
- **Shadowspear:** overlaps Basilisk Collar and existing trample sources.
- **Komainu Battle Armor:** does not beat the existing extra-combat/high-MV/protection slots.
- **Jhoira, Ageless Innovator:** slower setup than existing artifact engines.
- **Generic cEDH conversion:** rejected as commander-identity loss.

## Historical rules correction

Relentless Assault and Seize the Day were previously counted as Tetsuo free-cast extra-combat payoffs. Both phrase their extra combat as occurring after "this main phase" and current rulings confirm that when they resolve outside a main phase they do not create that combat. Tetsuo's attack trigger resolves during combat, so these two slots are replaced rather than preserved on a false interaction premise.
