## Accepted and implemented
| Candidate | Paired cut | Decision basis |
|---|---|---|
| Court of Ambition | Loyal Subordinate | Recurring upkeep life loss resolves before first main phase, helping Abaddon’s same-turn cascade threshold; monarch status can add card flow. Trade-offs: four-mana investment; opponents can discard; effect repeats only on later upkeeps. |
| Warstorm Surge | Rakdos, Lord of Riots | Converts each 3/3 Spawn created by The Lost and the Damned after an exile-cast into 3 damage; other creature entries also become targeted damage. Trade-off: loses Rakdos’s 6/6 flying/trample body and creature-spell discounts. |

## Rejected for this revision
| Candidate | Reason |
|---|---|
| Blood for the Blood God! | Strong possible finisher and refill, but MV 11, hand discard, and efficiency dependence on multiple creature deaths make it higher-variance than the chosen package. Revisit if game evidence shows a missing finisher. |
| Poxwalkers | Recurs from the graveyard on exile-casts, but the deck has no dedicated self-mill/sacrifice package to ensure it is in the graveyard at the relevant time. |
| Descent into Avernus | Symmetrically gives opponents Treasure and can accelerate their mana as much as or more than this deck. |
| Dark Apostle | The four-mana body plus a three-mana tap activation is slow and telegraphed; Abaddon already grants cascade to qualifying hand-cast spells. |
| Heralds of Tzeentch | Mostly a flying body plus one cascade; lower global impact than the accepted threshold and payoff engines. |
| Let the Galaxy Burn | Overlaps the existing board-control/cascade package without improving persistent payoff as much as the accepted changes. |
| Pink Horror | On-theme spell-ping candidate, but no clearer net gain than Court and Warstorm in this revision. |
| Mutalith Vortex Beast | Coin-flip ETB value is less dependable than existing draw/cascade engines. |
| Bloodthirster | Combat-damage dependent, less central to the cast-from-exile burn plan. |
| Spear Spewer | Symmetrical self-damage and one damage per activation provide a lower-ceiling threshold setup. |
| Cryptolith Fragment // Aurora of Emrakul | Enters tapped at three mana and has symmetrical life-loss; the Court slot is stronger for the commander plan. |
| Kill! Maim! Burn! | Playable flexible removal, but the review found no cut with a clear net gain over the chosen changes. |

## Explicitly inconclusive
**Aurora Phoenix over Mind’s Desire:** the previous bounded 3+3 pilot test cast neither candidate, had unmatched starting libraries, and included one timeout. It is not performance evidence; Mind’s Desire remains. The same weak comparison was not rerun.
