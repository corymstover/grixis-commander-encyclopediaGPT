## Meaningful outside-ranked challengers

- **ADD — Force of Will.** Replaces Arcane Denial; free protection materially improves tap-out activation/payload turns and is currently Commander-legal.
- **ADD — Pemmin's Aura.** Final adversarial correction over Aphetto Alchemist. Same three-mana deployment class as several untap engines, but `{U}: untap enchanted creature` is repeatable in the same turn and `{U}` can also grant shroud. This is more commander-specific than a summoning-sick creature untapper.
- **ADD — Swiftfoot Boots.** Replaces Lightning Greaves because hexproof permits your own Minamo/Patriar's Seal/Pemmin's Aura interactions while shroud from Greaves would block targeted untap effects.
- **ADD — City of Brass, Marsh Flats, Misty Rainforest, Flooded Strand, Volcanic Island, Arid Mesa, Verdant Catacombs, Wooded Foothills.** No-budget mana-base red-team: remove tapped/check/pain sources where a true dual/fetch provides broader sequencing and Brainstorm/fetch utility without weakening the 34-land count.
- **REJECT — Freed from the Real.** Genuine commander-specific candidate, but Pemmin's Aura has the same mana value and same `{U}` repeatable untap while adding on-demand shroud/flying. The extra blue pip is supportable by the finalized mana base.
- **REJECT — Aphetto Alchemist.** Initially selected over Ioreth, then displaced during red-team by Pemmin's Aura because Alchemist must survive summoning sickness and supplies only one natural untap per turn.
- **REJECT — Rings of Brighthearth.** Commander-specific but adds `{2}` to each copied activation; Illusionist's Bracers gives the same core payoff without repeated mana tax.
- **REJECT — Thousand-Year Elixir / Magewright's Stone / Sting, the Glinting Dagger.** Untap redundancy is real, but these are slower or more timing-limited than the finalized Training Grounds + Omen Hawker + Bracers + Pemmin's Aura + Patriar's Seal + Minamo package.
- **REJECT — Thassa's Oracle / Demonic Consultation / Tainted Pact / Underworld Breach generic cEDH package.** A small Zevlor cEDH sample demonstrates the shell can be used, but these wins do not require Zevlor and would homogenize the deck rather than maximize the commander's natural target-copy strategy.
- **REJECT — additional free counters/fast mana.** Force of Will is the one material protection upgrade. More generic cEDH density did not beat target payloads or commander activation infrastructure at Zevlor's Bracket-4 natural ceiling.

## Final adversarial closeout

Red-team corrections made before closure:
1. Lightning Greaves -> Swiftfoot Boots because Greaves' shroud shuts off the deck's own targeted untap effects.
2. Stormcarved Coast -> Flooded Strand as part of the fetch/dual mana red-team.
3. Aphetto Alchemist -> Pemmin's Aura after repeatable same-turn untap and protection were verified.
4. Shivan Reef -> Arid Mesa; Sulfurous Springs -> Verdant Catacombs; Underground River -> Wooded Foothills to complete the usable no-budget fetchland suite.

After those corrections, another full role-by-role and candidate pass produced **ZERO NEW MATERIAL IMPROVEMENTS**.
