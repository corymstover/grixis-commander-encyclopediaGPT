# Admiral Beckett Brass — Candidate Decision Summary

**Cycle:** 2026-10-13 | **Final status:** APPROVED / LOCKED

## Implemented
- Add **The Indomitable**; cut **Bident of Thassa**. Similar combat-damage draw role; the Vehicle has trample and can be cast from graveyard with three tapped Pirates/Vehicles. Trade-off: loses optional draw and force-attacks mode, while The Indomitable draws mandatorily.
- Add **Zephyr Singer**; cut **Daring Saboteur**. Zephyr adds flying/vigilance and permanent flying counters only to creatures that convoked it. Trade-off: raises printed mana value from two to four, taps convokers (they typically cannot attack that turn), and loses Daring Saboteur’s repeatable single-creature unblockability/filtering.
