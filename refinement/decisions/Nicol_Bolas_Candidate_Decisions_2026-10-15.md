## Why the candidate changed direction

The original commander's current ruling says his discard ability triggers whenever **Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent for any reason**, not only combat damage. That makes commander-sourced damage the highest-value axis:

- **Ingris Stingerquill:** Nicol attacking causes Nicol to deal 1 to every opponent.
- **Kediss:** one combat hit causes Nicol to deal that much to every other opponent.
- **Dragon Tempest:** Nicol's Dragon ETB causes Nicol himself to deal damage and gives haste.
- **Chandra's Ignition:** Nicol damages every opponent and each other creature.
- **Pain for All:** Nicol deals power-based damage on Aura entry and can reflect damage to every opponent.
- **Quicksilver Dagger / Viridian Longbow:** Nicol can directly ping an opponent and empty that hand without combat.

## Candidate exhaustion — notable rejected alternatives

- **Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker / Nicol Bolas, God-Pharaoh / Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God — REJECT here.** They are legitimate high-ranked/popular Bolas cards, but deck #42 is Nicol Bolas, the Ravager, whose transform/planeswalker identity much more naturally supports Superfriends. Importing the whole package here would homogenize adjacent commanders.
- **Cruel Ultimatum / Dark Intimations — REJECT at final cut stage.** Flavorful and strong, but five-to-seven-mana one-opponent value does not outperform direct commander-source damage engines or retained discard payoffs.
- **Pandemonium — REJECT.** It can make Nicol deal 7 on entry, but it also gives every opponent's entering creatures power-based damage opportunities. Dragon Tempest supplies Nicol-source entry damage plus haste without that symmetrical liability.
- **Electropotence — REJECT.** Valid commander-source damage, but requires both a three-mana enchantment and an additional `{2}{R}` after Nicol enters. Pain for All and Dragon Tempest are more efficient.
- **Hermetic Study / Fire Whip / Psionic Gift — REJECT.** Valid tap-ping effects, but Quicksilver Dagger adds card draw and Viridian Longbow supplies non-Aura redundancy.
- **Soul's Fire / Surestrike Trident — REJECT.** Can make Nicol deal damage, but Chandra's Ignition scales the same concept to every opponent while sweeping other creatures.
- **Samut, Hazoret's Champion — REJECT as redundant.** Dragon Tempest, Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Hall of the Bandit Lord, and Arena of Glory provide sufficient haste access.
- **Karlach, Fury of Avernus — REJECT as redundant.** Aggravated Assault remains and already pairs with Sword of Feast and Famine for repeat combats; commander-source damage is a more distinctive use of slots.
- **Bloodchief Ascension — REJECT at final slot stage.** Excellent once online, but requires three quest counters; Sangromancer and Tinybones pay off the first successful hand wipe immediately.
- **Painful Quandary / Quest for the Nihil Stone / Shrieking Affliction — REJECT.** They punish empty hands but do not help Nicol create the decisive damage event; existing aftermath payoff density is sufficient.
- **Additional infect — REJECT.** Infect shortcuts are powerful but bypass the commander's defining hand-destruction strategy.
- **Wheel package — REJECT.** Refill-and-discard play is more naturally Nekusar/Gwendlyn territory; original Nicol wants emptied hands to stay empty.
- **Generic Oracle/Consultation or unrelated deterministic Breach wins — REJECT.** They increase abstract power by making Nicol less necessary.

## Interaction corrections / retained synergies

- **Curiosity remains.** The refined list greatly increases the number of Nicol-sourced damage events, so Curiosity now converts those events into optional cards rather than relying only on ordinary combat.
- **Tergrid + Tinybones, Bauble Burglar:** both can trigger from a discarded permanent, but the card can only end up in one destination. The controller chooses trigger order; resolving one first can make the other fail to find the card in the graveyard.
- **Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine remains.** A connected Sword hit untaps lands; in the following main phase five usable mana can fund another Assault combat. Continued connections can repeat the sequence.
- **Sword of Fire and Ice leaves.** Besides being generic, protection from red/blue conflicts with Pain for All and Quicksilver Dagger targeting/remaining attached to Nicol.
