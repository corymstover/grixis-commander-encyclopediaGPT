## Decisions

### ACCEPT — Patriar's Seal
The best new commander-specific mana piece. It taps for any color and its `{1}, {T}` ability untaps Tetsuo, directly converting one artifact slot into both colored support and another activation.

### ACCEPT — Puppeteer
At three mana with `{U}, {T}:` tap or untap target creature, it can either create a legal Tetsuo target or untap Tetsuo. It is a cleaner role fit than Maeve's expensive repeatable goad.

### ACCEPT — Flood
Current Oracle text is `{U}{U}: Tap target creature without flying.` It is repeatable, does not tap itself, and can manufacture targets at instant speed. The flying restriction is real but acceptable given the remaining goad/forced-combat package.

### ACCEPT — Blustersquall
One blue mana creates one legal target; overload `{3}{U}` taps every creature the caster does not control. This is both an emergency interaction spell and a mass Tetsuo-enabler.

### ACCEPT — Retreat to Coralhelm
Every landfall can tap or untap a creature. The revised fetchland package creates additional landfall events, allowing Retreat to tap an opposing threat or reset Tetsuo after an activation. The scry option prevents dead triggers.

### ACCEPT — Force of Will
Tetsuo repeatedly consumes U/B/B/R during opponents' turns, so zero-mana stack interaction is materially more valuable than Arcane Denial's two-mana hold-up and opponent card draw. Final verification must confirm a healthy blue pitch pool.

### ACCEPT — Withering Torment
Instant-speed destroy target creature or enchantment at a fixed two-life cost is a clean efficiency upgrade over Feed the Swarm.

### ACCEPT — premium dual/fetch package
Badlands, Underground Sea, Flooded Strand, Marsh Flats, and Misty Rainforest improve untapped U/B/R sequencing and specifically help assemble the commander's U/B/B/R activation.

### ACCEPT — Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs
Converts one Island into a land-capable interactive spell while preserving 36 functional land-capable slots. The spell side can return an opposing spell or nonland permanent, including a spell that cannot be countered.

## Material challengers rejected

- **Mana Vault** — fast in the abstract but cannot pay any part of Tetsuo's first U/B/R cast or U/B/B/R activation; Patriar's Seal is materially more commander-native.
- **War's Toll** — forced-combat redundancy plus generic land-tapping stax; below the identity/slot threshold.
- **Thornbite Staff** — too much setup mana relative to the existing untap package.
- **Tamiyo, the Moon Sage** — five-mana setup loses to lower-cost tap engines.
- **Ghoulish Impetus / Parasitic Impetus / additional Impetus Auras** — redundant one-target goad density.
- **No Mercy** — defensive deterrent rather than proactive target creation.
- **Opposition** — powerful with a larger creature board, but this build should not require tapping multiple friendly creatures to do what Flood/Puppeteer/Retreat accomplish more cleanly.
- **Generic cEDH packages** — rejected as identity-erasing.
