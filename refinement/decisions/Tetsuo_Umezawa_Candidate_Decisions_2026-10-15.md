## Accepted commander-engine upgrades

### Sting, the Glinting Dagger
Equipped creature gets +1/+1 and haste and untaps at the beginning of each combat. On Tetsuo this is a repeated commander reset across multiplayer combat steps. It replaces generic Opposition Agent while Lightning Greaves and Swiftfoot Boots remain for protection/haste redundancy.

### Patriar's Seal
Taps for any color and can pay `{1}, {T}` to untap target legendary creature you control. It directly supports Tetsuo's colored activation and repeat-use engine.

### Puppeteer
`{U}, {T}:` tap or untap target creature. It can manufacture a legal Tetsuo target or untap Tetsuo.

### Flood
`{U}{U}:` tap target creature without flying. Repeatable target manufacturing at instant speed without tapping itself.

### Blustersquall
One blue mana taps one opposing creature; overload `{3}{U}` taps every creature not controlled by the caster.

### Retreat to Coralhelm
Every landfall can tap or untap a creature. Fetchlands can create two triggers, allowing repeated target creation or Tetsuo resets.

### Force of Will
Free stack protection is valuable because Tetsuo repeatedly consumes U/B/B/R. Final verification must confirm a healthy blue pitch pool.

### Withering Torment
Instant-speed destroy target creature or enchantment at fixed two-life cost.

### Premium dual/fetch package
Badlands, Underground Sea, Flooded Strand, Marsh Flats, and Misty Rainforest improve untapped U/B/R sequencing and specifically help assemble U/B/B/R.

### Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs
Turns one Island into a land-capable interactive spell while preserving 36 functional land-capable slots.

## Material challengers rejected

- **Mana Vault** — cannot pay any part of Tetsuo's first U/B/R cast or U/B/B/R activation; Patriar's Seal is much more commander-native.
- **War's Toll** — forced-combat redundancy plus generic land-tapping stax.
- **Thornbite Staff** — too much setup mana relative to the existing untap package.
- **Tamiyo, the Moon Sage** — five-mana setup loses to lower-cost tap engines.
- **Ghoulish Impetus / Parasitic Impetus** — redundant one-target goad density.
- **No Mercy** — defensive deterrent rather than proactive target creation.
- **Opposition** — creature-board dependent and less direct than Flood/Puppeteer/Retreat.
- **Norritt** — can untap blue Tetsuo, but Puppeteer is cheaper and broader.
- **Generic cEDH packages** — rejected as identity-erasing.
