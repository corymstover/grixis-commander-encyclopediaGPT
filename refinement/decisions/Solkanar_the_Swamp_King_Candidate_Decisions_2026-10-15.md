## Preserved engines

- **Sol'kanar + Demon's Horn:** separate triggers from each black spell create repeated lifegain events for downstream payoffs.
- **Painter's Servant naming black:** makes all spells black in addition to their other colors, so every player's spell can trigger Sol'kanar and Demon's Horn; Painter remains paired with Grindstone as a compact secondary finish.
- **Blanket of Night / Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth:** global Swamp conversion enables Sol'kanar's swampwalk and substantially expands Coffers/Crypt Ghast/Dread Presence/Consuming Corruption value.
- **Exquisite Blood / Bloodthirsty Conqueror + Vito / Enduring Tenacity / Marauding Blight-Priest / Starscape Cleric:** redundant life-gain/life-loss loops are preserved.
- **Aetherflux Reservoir, Bolas's Citadel, K'rrik:** lifegain is converted into spell velocity and a direct finisher.
- **Wheel of Fortune / Windfall + Sheoldred / Underworld Dreams:** secondary draw-punishment pressure remains intact.

## Material challengers rejected

- **Staff of the Death Magus:** one mana more than Demon's Horn and redundant once Painter's Servant is active; Swamp-entry rider is not enough to displace an established slot.
- **Evil Presence / Contaminated Ground / Tainted Well / Nightcreep:** lower-impact redundancy behind Blanket of Night and Urborg.
- **Diamond Mare / Prism Ring:** only the controller's spells trigger them, making them materially worse than Demon's Horn in a Painter's Servant deck.
- **Epicure of Blood / Defiant Bloodlord:** expensive versions of already-present gain-to-loss payoffs.
- **Veinwitch Coven:** useful recursion, but Witch of the Moors already turns routine commander lifegain into card return plus opposing sacrifice pressure without additional mana per trigger.
- **Defile:** excellent rate, but Consuming Corruption is the more commander-specific Swamp-scaling removal spell because it also gains life; Withering Torment fills the missing enchantment/instant role.
- **Countersquall / Undermine:** black identity is attractive, but losing Counterspell's ability to answer creature spells or adding mana is not a clean upgrade.
- **Witch's Cottage / Leechridden Swamp:** actual Swamps but insufficient fixing/tempo compared with the final typed-surveil land package.
- **Pristine Talisman:** lifegain on tap does not justify moving a two-mana rock to three mana.
- **Boggart Trawler // Boggart Bog:** flexible, but Bojuka Bog remains immediate zero-spell-cost graveyard hate while Rakdos Charm already supplies instant-speed redundancy.
- **Additional fetchlands:** strong, but after adding the two typed surveil Swamps and retaining six premium fetches, no additional land cut improves the total package enough to justify more churn.
- **Force of Will:** the exact current list does not have a reliable enough blue nonland pitch pool; the deterministic Counterspell slot stays.
- **Generic Thassa's Oracle / Underworld Breach conversion:** rejected as homogenizing and not necessary to maximize Sol'kanar's natural Bracket 4 ceiling.
