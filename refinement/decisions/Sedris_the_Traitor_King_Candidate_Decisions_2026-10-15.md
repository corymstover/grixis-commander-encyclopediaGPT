## Preserved engines

- Sedris + **Convergence of Dominion**: commander-granted unearth activations become {B}, and cannot be reduced below one mana.
- **Obeka, Brute Chronologist / Sundial of the Infinite**: ending the turn after an unearth delayed exile trigger is on the stack removes that trigger from the stack.
- **Teferi's Veil**: an attacking unearthed creature phases out at end of combat, making it unavailable to the beginning-of-end-step exile trigger; lethal combat damage still occurs before the phase-out trigger.
- **Deadeye Navigator**: exile-and-return can reset an unearthed creature as a new object; the unearth replacement effect does not replace an event that is already exiling the card.
- **River Kelpie**: unearthed creatures enter from a graveyard and trigger its draw ability.
- **Twilight Diviner**: an eligible creature entering from a graveyard can create a lasting token copy, subject to its once-each-turn trigger limit.
- **Deadeye Navigator + Peregrine Drake**: retained as the existing creature-based mana engine/combo package.
- **Panharmonicon**: retained because the deck's tutor, reanimation, removal and value creature ETBs are central to the Sedris plan.

## Material challengers rejected

- **Embalmer's Tools:** redundant with Convergence's one-mana unearth floor.
- **Phyrexian Delver:** excellent when unearthed, but no clean creature cut; Dread Return remains self-mill compatible and can function from the graveyard without Sedris.
- **Bladewing, Deathless Tyrant:** strong payoff but must connect in combat and asks for a larger stocked graveyard; does not beat the immediate ETB targets already present.
- **Diluvian Primordial / Ancient Brass Dragon:** powerful multiplayer haymakers, but opponent-resource/combat dependence loses to the existing deterministic ETB suite.
- **Gravebreaker Lamia:** self-tutor is strong, but its cost-reduction text affects spells cast from the graveyard rather than Sedris's activated unearth ability.
- **Conjurer's Closet / Thassa, Deep-Dwelling / Ghostly Flicker:** all can rescue unearthed creatures, but Obeka/Sundial/Veil/Deadeye already provide sufficient dedicated rescue density. Ripples solves the more valuable setup bottleneck instead.
- **Wonder:** useful graveyard evasion, but the deck is primarily ETB/reanimation value rather than combat-connect reanimator.
- **Ripples of Undeath versus other setup spells:** Ripples wins the slot because it is cheap, repeatable, mills three every first main phase and lets the controller buy one of those cards into hand rather than being a one-shot loot spell.
- **Curse-Marred Demon:** current and Commander-legal, but its random discard after tutoring is less deterministic than the existing tutor creatures.
- **Hela, Mistress of Darkness:** excluded from this Sedris refinement because it is a future-release card and Sedris has no project-authorized pre-release exception.
- **Generic Thassa's Oracle / Underworld Breach conversion:** rejected; higher generic Grixis combo power would dilute Sedris's unearth identity.
