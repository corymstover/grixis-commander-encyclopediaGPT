## Commander-first blueprint

- **Primary plan:** build a dense suite of artifact and creature activated abilities that sacrifice a permanent as part of their cost. Ashnod copies each qualifying non-mana ability once when she is on the battlefield, converting one paid sacrifice into repeated value.
- **Secondary plan:** artifact-token production, recursion and death/entry payoffs turn repeated activations into cards, mana, bodies, drain, and board control.
- **Win routes:** grind through repeated artifact value and token engines; convert artifact sacrifices into opponent life loss through Marionette Apprentice, Disciple of the Vault, Mayhem Devil, Reckless Fireweaver and the new Oni-Cult Anvil; build a wide artifact-creature board with Sai and Retrofitter Foundry; or leverage established artifact/activated-ability combos when their pieces and costs are assembled.
- **Core packages:** Ashnod's Altar / Krark-Clan Ironworks and sacrifice outlets; artifact death/ETB value; artifact recursion (Emry, Goblin Welder, Scrap Trawler, Scrap Welder, Myr Retriever, Nim Deathmantle); artifact cost reduction; repeatable activated abilities that sacrifice permanents.
- **Essential coverage:** fast artifact ramp and colored fixing, redundant sacrifice outlets, card draw, recursion, interaction/protection, and multiple noncombat drain/board-building routes.
- **Constraints / natural ceiling:** optimized artifact-sacrifice combo/value deck, approximately Bracket 4 under the project’s scale. Its commander costs five mana and must survive; activated engines often need mana/tap setup and are vulnerable to removal or graveyard hate. Do not replace its identity with generic Grixis spellslinger or a standalone artifact-storm shell.
- **Exceptions:** none recorded for this deck.

## Implemented swaps

1. **Add Oni-Cult Anvil → cut Commander's Sphere.** Anvil’s {T}, sacrifice-an-artifact activation is copied by Ashnod. One activation resolves twice, dealing 2 damage to each opponent and gaining 2 life, while the artifact leaving during your turn can also make a Construct once that turn. The trade-off is losing a three-mana fixer; the deck retains a high density of efficient ramp and premium colored lands.
2. **Add Stormclaw Rager → cut Dockside Chef.** Rager’s {1}, sacrifice-another-creature-or-artifact activation is copied by Ashnod, so one sacrificed permanent yields two cards and two +1/+1 counters. Its activation is sorcery-only and Rager costs three mana to deploy, while Chef has instant-speed flexibility; the deck retains multiple other instant-speed sacrifice outlets and gains a cheaper activation plus a scalable threat.
