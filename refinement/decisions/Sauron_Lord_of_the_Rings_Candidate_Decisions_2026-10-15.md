## Material challengers rejected

- **Shelob, Dread Weaver / Wake the Dragon / Treasure Nabber:** useful high-cost theft/value effects, but the current deck already has a dense premium reanimation target suite and these do not solve Sauron's casting or setup bottlenecks.
- **Worn Powerstone:** acceleration is welcome, but entering tapped and costing three loses materially to Mana Vault/Grim Monolith.
- **Lord of the Nazgûl:** thematic but asks for a spell-density subtheme this reanimator build does not support strongly enough.
- **Fact or Fiction / Deep Analysis:** good graveyard-friendly draw, but the deck already has cheaper loot/self-mill and sustained premium draw; no stronger cut remains.
- **Incarnation Technique / Dread Return:** playable reanimation redundancy, but the selected suite already spans one- and two-mana recursion, Victimize, Necromancy, Living Death, Too Greedily, Too Deep, and Sauron's own cast trigger.
- **Intuition / Unmarked Grave:** strong setup tools, but current commander-specific evidence is small and the existing Entomb/Buried Alive/Forbidden Alchemy/loot package is already dense.
- **Avacyn, Angel of Horror:** a legitimate new 2026 reanimation target, but it has no immediate ETB and does not displace Archon, Valgavoth, Jin-Gitaxias, Sheoldred, Ancient Brass Dragon, Cavern-Hoard Dragon, or the new Fanatic chain.
- **The Soul Stone:** thematic recursion engine but far too slow relative to the selected fast mana and cheap reanimation.
- **Geode Golem:** commander-centric in theory, but requires connecting in combat before it casts Sauron; pure acceleration and Command Beacon are more reliable.
- **Heartless Summoning:** reduces Sauron's cost but creates material nonbos by shrinking the creature/Army support package, including killing 1-toughness creatures such as Orcish Bowmasters.
- **Generic Oracle/Breach/turbo-combo conversion:** rejected. Raising raw Grixis combo power at the cost of Sauron's cast-reanimation identity violates the encyclopedia's no-forced-homogenization rule.

## Rules-critical conclusions

- Sauron's cast ability triggers when Sauron is **cast**, not when he enters. Once triggered, it exists independently of the commander spell; countering Sauron does not remove the already-created triggered ability.
- The trigger resolves in order: amass Orcs 5, mill five cards, then return a creature card from the controller's graveyard to the battlefield. The five cards milled by the same trigger can therefore become eligible for the final reanimation instruction.
- Command Beacon puts Sauron from the command zone into its owner's hand. Commander tax applies to casts from the command zone, so a later hand cast avoids that additional cost while still being a cast of the commander and still triggering Sauron's ability.
- Metamorphosis Fanatic returned by Sauron's trigger enters the battlefield and can trigger its own reanimation ability, allowing a second creature to return from the graveyard.
- Dance of the Dead is an Aura reanimation spell with its own tapped/untap restriction; it is not the same rules object as Reanimate/Animate Dead and must be sequenced with its Oracle text.
- Phyrexian Tower's sacrifice activation is a mana ability and can place a creature into the graveyard while generating {B}{B}; this is relevant before Living Death or a subsequent recursion effect.
- Nazgûl's deck-construction text permits up to nine cards named Nazgûl. Four copies therefore do not violate Commander deck construction.
- Anger requires a Mountain controlled while Anger is in the graveyard; Wonder analogously requires an Island. The revised mana base retains multiple lands with those basic land types.
- Living Death exiles all creature cards from graveyards, then players sacrifice all creatures they control, then put the exiled creatures onto the battlefield; the sequence matters for setup and sacrifice decisions.
- Sneak Attack's delayed sacrifice naturally places cheated creatures in the graveyard for later reanimation.
- The Mouth of Sauron can target its controller, intentionally milling three before its amass calculation and feeding Sauron's graveyard plan.
