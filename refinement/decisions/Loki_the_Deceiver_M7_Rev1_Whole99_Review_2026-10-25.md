## Commander-first objective

Loki ({1}{U}{B}{R}) creates a tapped-and-attacking nonlegendary Illusion copy of another target Villain on attack, with a delayed next-end-step sacrifice. Each Loki object draws when one or more Villains deal combat damage to each player. Copies entering attacking trigger ETB, static and combat-damage text, but not the copied creature's "attacks" triggers. Preserve cheap Villain targets, high-impact repeat ETBs, haste/protection, the Villain connive/draw engine, and token-saving Master/Sundial effects. Bracket 4 — Optimized, not generic turbo-combo.

## Seven exact swaps and costs

1. +Sakashima of a Thousand Faces / -Thunderbolts Conspiracy. Four-mana permanent Loki copy gets a second independent attack trigger and combat-damage draw engine on later combat, with legend-rule suppression. Thunderbolts can recover nontoken dying Villains with finality, but not a dying temporary token; Master preventing token sacrifice also reduces its value. Loss: sweep-recovery of nontoken Villains.
2. +Elektra, Daughter of the Hand / -Kang Prime. Four-mana Villain ETB destroys one opposing creature of power 3 or less, repeated on each Loki clone. Sneak supports another ETB. Kang Prime's entering suspend value takes two upkeeps to cash in. Loss: late free spell and flyer.
3. +Ultimo, Civilization's End / -Doctor Octopus, Master Planner. Six-mana Villain enters and forces each opponent to sacrifice a creature, repeated on every copy. Seven-mana Octopus offers +2/+2 and end-step refill to eight, but costs more and duplicates the ample draw engine. Loss: anthem and max-hand/refill.
4. +M.O.D.O.K., Evil Intellect / -Venom, Eddie Brock. Five-mana flying Villain edicts one target opponent's nontoken creature on the second card drawn each turn, naturally fueled by Loki/connive. Venom grew and drew from Villain deaths, but Master may prevent the token deaths it wants. Loss: death-card engine.
5. +Living Lies of Loki / -Kang, Temporal Tyrant. Two-mana Villain adds an early copy target, gains power from Illusions and draws on death, including a naturally sacrificed Loki copy. Kang's attack connive never triggers from entering-attacking tokens; its second-draw drain is smaller. Loss: ping/drain and real Kang attacks.
6. +Kid Loki / -Dark Ritual. One-mana Villain raises early legal-target access; connived counters grant hexproof only to your creatures given counters that turn. Dark Ritual's explosive one-off BBB is real but leaves no board or repeatable commander synergy. Loss: ritual tempo in nut draws.
7. +City of Brass / -Path of Ancestry. Untapped UBR source versus entering tapped; improve Loki deployment and double-spell turns at 34 lands. Loss: Villain-cast scry. City deals one damage when tapped.

The cut choices deliberately retain Crimson Cowl, Master of Evil and Killmonger, Ruthless Usurper. The older two-swap draft cut these three-drops; that harms early target access. Cowl creates a 2/1 Villain whenever original nontoken Loki attacks, while a Killmonger token's combat-damage artifact sacrifice/Treasure still works even though its attack pump does not.

## Whole 99-card audit and red-team

All 94 distinct noncommander names (99 physical cards) appear in the classified slot ledger below; every slot was compared against its actual role. The seven substitutions were selected from this full list, without a fixed swap quota.

- Land/fixing: preserve full UBR untapped fetch/dual/rainbow coverage, eight basics as fetch targets, Villainous Hideout, Cavern of Souls, and Hall of Echoes' copy-Loki identity. Path of Ancestry loses because its enters-tapped cost is material at 34 lands. Urborg fixes black in other specialty lands. Ancient Tomb improves colorless burst but can fail colored Loki pips; Three Tree City is high-ceiling only after a broad board; Secluded Courtyard does not cast noncreature colored interaction.
- Early Villains: Baron Strucker and Tombstone discount creature casts; Prowler and Ultron convert entry/connive into board; Cowl and Killmonger are cheap actual targets; new Kid Loki and Living Lies increase count of printed Villain targets with MV at most three from 10 to 12.
- ETB bodies: Black Cat's opponent exile, Carnage's low-MV reanimation, Doctor Doom's two Doombots, Mysterio's mass Villain copies, Elektra's targeted kill, Ultimo's each-opponent edict. Kang Prime's suspend was good but delayed; Octopus was potent but costlier than selected edict.
- Combat/connive payoffs: Iron Monger, Leader, M.O.D.O.K., Red Ghost, Stilt-Man, Crossbones, Madame Hydra, Norman/Green Goblin, Doctor Doom King, The Squadron Sinister and Titan all support draw, counters, evasion or finishing. M.O.D.O.K. and Evil Intellect are separate abilities, so retaining both is intentional.
- Multipliers/preservation: Chameleon, Taskmaster, Spark Double, Sakashima, Loki Lord of Misrule, Roaming Throne, The Master Multiplied and Sundial are central. The Master stops triggered sacrifice/exile of tokens, not voluntary costs or state-based deaths; Sundial needs the delayed sacrifice trigger already on stack to save tokens permanently.
- Mana/value/protection: the mana-support package with Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, three Talismans, The Soul Stone, Loki's Scepter, Patchwork Banner and Urza's Incubator for tribal reduction. Lightning Greaves and Swiftfoot Boots grant haste/protection to Loki. Greaves shroud only blocks targeting of its wearer, not Loki targeting another Villain.
- Interaction: An Offer, Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Deadly Rollick, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act, Vandalblast cover spells, permanents, sweepers and artifacts. Elektra/Ultimo/Evil Intellect add repeatable board interaction. Reanimate and Demonic Tutor supply recovery/selection; Jeska's Will is commander-dependent mana/card velocity.
- Enchantments: Avengers Under Siege, Doom Reigns Supreme, Cover of Darkness, Kindred Discovery, Black Market Connections and Rhystic Study each yield Villain bodies, Villain ETBs, combat connection or cards. Thunderbolts is the least necessary recursion spell versus the true copy engine.

Alternatives fully considered:
- REJECT old cut pairings (+Ultimo/-Crimson Cowl, +Elektra/-Killmonger); cheap-target role matters.
- RETAIN ALTERNATIVE Strionic Resonator; old limited goldfish made its two-mana activation payable on an attack by turn 6 in only 0.0393 modeled hands, not a win rate. New one-mana Kid is more consistently available early.
- REJECT Seize the Day; old model found a postcombat castable turn-six hand only 0.0219 of the time. No current Rev1 gameplay simulation asserted.
- REJECT Kindred Charge, Molten Echoes, Helm of the Host, Rite of Replication; non-Loki copies of most legendary Villains are killed by the legend rule unless Master is present, and the upper-curve slot cost is excessive.
- RETAIN ALTERNATIVE Auton Soldier: strong nonlegend myriad copy, but six-mana non-Villain with a later attack requirement. Its entering-attacking myriad tokens themselves do not get attack triggers.
- REJECT Kingpin Wilson Fisk, Skullclamp, Doom's Time Platform, Typhoid Mary, Lady Loki, Puppet Master, Vulture, Raise the Palisade and Kindred Dominance on repeatability, Master dependency, timing, curve or poorer specific role tradeoffs.
- REJECT generic Oracle/Consult/Breach and Vampiric Tutor package convergence. EXCLUDE unreleased Hela/Mirror Universe Intrusion and banned Dockside Extortionist/Jeweled Lotus.

Printed noncommander Villains MV 3 or less: 10 -> 12. Mean nonland library MV: 3.3846 -> 3.3077. Game Changers unchanged at five: Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Jeska's Will and Rhystic Study.

Rules: Loki tokens have no copied "attacked" event; ETBs and combat damage can trigger. Multiple Loki objects draw separately once per damaged player, not per connecting Villain. Sakashima needs haste or a later turn to attack; its copy of Loki obeys the self-reference rule. Hall becoming a creature this turn generally cannot attack without haste. Ultimo edicts every opponent; Evil Intellect edicts only the target opponent's nontoken creature; Elektra only destroys one target opponent creature with power <=3; Kid's counter hexproof only lasts the turn counters were placed. The Master may prevent Living Lies from dying; death-draw is not counted in that scenario. Keep Sundial timing precise.
