## Accepted and implemented

| Addition | Cut | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Power Conduit | Dire Fleet Daredevil | Removes the finality counter from a Pirate returned by the commander as an activation cost. The +1/+1 mode can make that returned creature a 5/5 and, more importantly, let it go to the graveyard instead of being exiled if it later dies. |
| Nesting Grounds | Crumbling Necropolis | Untapped utility land for moving a finality counter during a main phase. It complements Power Conduit by offering a postcombat timing window, including when Roaming Throne doubles the commander’s combat trigger. It sacrifices an always-tapped UBR source for colorless mana; 34 lands remain. |
| Kitesail Larcenist | Amphin Mutineer | A three-mana flying/ward Pirate whose ETB can temporarily disable up to one artifact or creature per player. It costs less and offers broader table reach; its effect ends when Larcenist leaves and opponents can sacrifice the transformed Treasure artifacts. |
