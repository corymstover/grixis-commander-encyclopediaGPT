## Trading Post for Commander's Sphere

- **Candidate rank:** #2 in the project's Ashnod top-100 candidate ranking.
- **Project data:** 52.3212% EDHREC inclusion; 45.331325 lift; 3,188-deck sample snapshot.
- **Add:** Trading Post — a four-mana colorless artifact with activated abilities to gain life, make a Goat token, sacrifice a creature to return an artifact card from graveyard to hand, or sacrifice an artifact to draw a card.
- **Cut:** Commander's Sphere — a three-mana mana rock that can be sacrificed to draw a card.
- **Commander-specific hypothesis:** Trading Post's sacrifice modes are unusually relevant to Ashnod. Sacrificing a nontoken creature or artifact to activate Trading Post can trigger Ashnod, and if it is the first Ashnod trigger that turn, the activated ability is copied. The copy resolves without paying the costs again, so the creature/artifact is sacrificed once but the artifact-recursion or card-draw effect occurs twice.
- **Expected benefit:** Repeatable artifact recursion and card draw, plus creature/artifact conversion utility; preserves the artifact-sacrifice identity.
- **Tradeoff:** Trading Post costs four mana, requires tapping and additional mana for its utility modes, and is not ramp. Cutting Commander's Sphere reduces mana-rock count and removes a cheap sacrifice-for-card-draw outlet. The baseline already has substantial fast mana, cost reduction, and artifact-based ramp.
- **Oracle / color identity / legality:** Trading Post has colorless identity; the white Goat token mentioned in its text does not add white color identity. No Commander legality issue identified.
