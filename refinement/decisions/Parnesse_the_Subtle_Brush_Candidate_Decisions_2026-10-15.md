## High-impact challengers rejected

- **Twincast / Twinferno / Teach by Example / See Double:** efficient and valid Parnesse cards, but the current list already has enough low-cost spell-copy density. Adding another one would displace a card-flow, protection, recursion, payoff, or acceleration slot without materially changing the deck's ceiling.
- **Storm King's Thunder:** powerful scaling finisher, but red-intensive and mana-hungry; the retained Thousand-Year Storm, Prismari, Bonus Round, Twinning Staff, and copy suite already cover the explosive-copy role with less dependence on one large setup turn.
- **Radiant Performer:** spectacular ceiling with the correct single-target spell, but five mana and highly board/target dependent.
- **Transforming Flourish:** demonstrate is commander-native, but the removal effect compensates destroyed permanents with free nonland spells, making it less reliable as optimized interaction than the retained answers.
- **Exterminator Magmarch:** direct spell-copy text, but only works with a narrow subset of targeted removal and requires a legal target controlled by a different opponent.
- **Dual Casting:** repeatable but asks for an Aura plus tapping a creature; Errant, Pyromancer's Goggles, Primal Wellspring, Double Vision, and the instant-copy suite are cleaner.
- **Resonating Lute:** extremely high spell-mana ceiling, but four mana and does not help cast Parnesse or the creature/enchantment engines; existing acceleration is already deep.
- **Chandra, Hope's Beacon:** direct copy trigger and strong value, but six mana, once-per-turn copy restriction, and vulnerable permanent type; does not displace the cheaper/repeatable current engines.
- **Hive Mind / Curse of Echoes:** high political spectacle but cede too much copy control to opponents and can improve opposing interaction/combo turns independently of Parnesse.
- **Venser, Fervent Forger:** new and genuinely synergistic when copying an opponent's instant/sorcery twice, but six mana/reactive and does not beat the retained five-to-seven-mana engines for a slot.
- **Mercurial Spelldancer:** repeatable in theory, but requires two noncreature casts plus combat connection before the copy payoff.
- **Generic Oracle/Consultation/Breach packages:** rejected under the project identity standard; they raise abstract Grixis power while making Parnesse less necessary.

## Rules-critical decisions

- **Parnesse cares about copying spells, not copying cards.** Under CR 707.10, copying a spell puts a spell copy on the stack; under CR 707.12, copying an object/card and then casting that copy is a separate process. This is the central reason Arcane Bombardment is cut.
- **Mizzix's Mastery is retained as recursion, not misclassified.** Its copies are card/object copies that are cast. The card remains because the overload ceiling rebuilds a graveyard full of the deck's actual copy spells and triggers cast/copy payoffs through subsequent effects.
- **Tempt with Mayhem:** each spell copy created under our control causes Parnesse to trigger. Copies opponents create are controlled by those opponents and do not themselves satisfy 'whenever you copy a spell' for us.
- **Wizard's Staff / Harmonic Prodigy / Veyran:** each can cause Parnesse's triggered ability to trigger an additional time under its own condition. Parnesse is a Vampire Wizard, so Wizard's Staff uses equip Wizard {1}.
- **Twinning Staff:** an additional spell copy produced by its replacement effect is another spell copy event for Parnesse.
- **Prismari, the Inspiration:** storm creates spell copies on the stack; those copies are not cast, but each copy is still a spell copy and feeds Parnesse. Multiple instances of storm trigger separately.
- **Sunken Palace:** when its special mana is spent to cast a spell, its delayed trigger copies that spell and feeds Parnesse; copying an activated ability instead does not trigger Parnesse.
- **Replication Technique / demonstrate:** our demonstrate copy feeds Parnesse; the chosen opponent's demonstrate copy does not count as a copy made by us.
