## Commander-first blueprint

The Rani is an optimized forced-combat / Clue commander. Her defining engine is not merely “goad matters”: every enter-the-battlefield or attack trigger creates a Mark of the Rani Aura token, and every goaded creature that connects with one of our opponents investigates. The strongest realistic build therefore wants repeatable Rani ETBs/attacks, reliable goad, ways to make goaded creatures connect, and artifact/Clue conversion that turns the combat engine into mana, cards and extra-turn pressure without replacing the commander with a generic Grixis combo shell.

Current optimized EDHREC evidence continues to center the commander on Forced Combat, with high commander-specific representation for Bothersome Quasit, Academy Manufactor, Vengeful Ancestor, Ghoulish Impetus, Karazikar, Mocking Doppelganger, Kardur, Entity Tracker, Firkraag, Forensic Gadgeteer and Thassa, Deep-Dwelling. The project candidate rankings independently place The Master, Mesmerist at #1 and Incriminating Impetus at #4 for The Rani.

## Accepted changes

- **The Master, Mesmerist:** repeatable targeted goad plus skulk makes the chosen creature more likely to connect, which can simultaneously trigger The Rani's investigate ability and The Master's draw/counter trigger. A Mark of the Rani may legally be attached to The Master; the +2/+2 raises his power from 3 to 5 and therefore increases the range of creatures his activated ability can target.
- **Thassa, Deep-Dwelling:** repeatably blinks The Rani at each end step. The Rani returns as a new object and immediately triggers her ETB ability, producing another Mark without needing another attack. Thassa is also indestructible and her activated tap ability can remove a blocker to help a goaded creature connect.
- **Force of Will:** protects the four-mana commander and high-leverage forced-combat turns while tapped out; the candidate retains a healthy blue nonland pitch pool.
- **Incriminating Impetus:** Aura density matters because Entity Tracker and Hateful Eidolon already reward enchantments. It both goads and suspects the enchanted creature; menace and 'can't block' make the forced attacker more likely to deal combat damage to another opponent and produce a Clue.
- **Baeloth Barrityl, Entertainer:** a Mark of the Rani can be attached to Baeloth, making him 4/7. His static ability then goads opposing creatures with power less than 4, while deaths of goaded attacking/blocking creatures produce Treasures. The Mark's own goad requirement on Baeloth does not turn off his static ability.
- **City of Brass / Mana Confluence / Flooded Strand:** replace conditional checklands with unconditional or fetch-based U/B/R access while preserving the 33-land count.

## Cuts

- **Agitator Ant:** opponents may simply decline the +1/+1 counters, so its goad output is opponent-controlled. The Master provides deterministic targeted goad and an additional combat-damage payoff.
- **Insurrection:** powerful but eight mana and largely a generic one-shot theft finisher. The deck already has Hot Pursuit as a late mass-control payoff; Thassa advances The Rani's own Mark engine every turn for half the mana.
- **Counterspell / Arcane Denial:** the list retains Mana Drain, Swan Song, Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will, Deflecting Swat, Deadly Rollick, Chaos Warp and Cyclonic Rift. Two generic counters are the cleanest slots for free protection and another commander-native goad Aura.
- **Orcish Bowmasters:** high raw power but no meaningful Mark, goad or Clue interaction. Baeloth is a substantially stronger commander-identity slot and converts The Rani's own Mark pump into a wider static goad threshold.
- **Dragonskull Summit / Drowned Catacomb / Sulfur Falls:** conditional lands lose to unconditional premium fixing/fetch access in a no-budget optimized list.

## Key retained engines

- **Academy Manufactor + investigate:** each Clue creation becomes one Clue, one Food and one Treasure, greatly increasing artifact-token output.
- **Erdwal Illuminator:** the first investigate each turn causes an additional investigate, multiplying the commander's damage payoff.
- **Academy Manufactor + Erdwal Illuminator:** the first investigate in a turn can generate two modified investigate events, producing six artifact tokens total (two Clues, two Foods and two Treasures) if both permanents remain available.
- **Time Sieve:** converts excess Clue/Food/Treasure production into extra turns; it is retained as an artifact-token payoff rather than presented as a deterministic infinite combo.
- **Displacer Kitten:** noncreature spells can blink The Rani and create additional Marks.
- **Bjorna, Nightfall Alchemist:** Clues and other excess artifacts become targeted goad plus damage.
- **Five Hundred Year Diary / Forensic Gadgeteer / Inspiring Statuary / Urza, Lord High Artificer / Tangletrove Kelp:** retained because they turn the investigate output into mana, cheaper clue conversion, or a combat board.
- **Ghoulish / Psychic / Shiny / Coercive Impetus and Eye of Nidhogg:** repeatable or value-generating goad Auras remain the thematic removal/pressure package.
- **Disrupt Decorum / Spectacular Showdown / Maestros Confluence / Kardur:** retained mass-goad effects give the deck sufficient whole-table forced-combat coverage.

## Material challengers rejected

- **Martha Jones:** creates a Clue and can make creatures unblockable after a Clue is sacrificed, but the list already has dense Clue conversion and does not need Rani to connect for her attack trigger to happen. She does not beat the accepted deterministic goad/blink engines.
- **Renegade Silent:** resilient repeatable goad, but the end-step-only cadence and self-phasing make it slower than The Master, Baeloth or the retained two- to three-mana goad pieces.
- **Parasitic Impetus:** perfectly on-theme, but Coercive Impetus, Shiny Impetus, Psychic Impetus, Ghoulish Impetus, Eye of Nidhogg and Incriminating Impetus each provide a stronger secondary payoff or evasion/removal function.
- **Conjurer's Closet:** repeats The Rani ETB but costs five mana and is more vulnerable than indestructible four-mana Thassa; only the stronger repeatable blink engine is added.
- **Propaganda / No Mercy:** strong defensive cards, but successful goad already directs legal attackers elsewhere and the deck has Bothersome Quasit plus interaction. They do not advance the investigate engine enough to displace a proactive slot.
- **Mana Vault / Chrome Mox:** raw acceleration is real, but The Rani requires U/B/R colored mana and the current rock/fixing suite is already dense. No commander-specific slot is weak enough to justify further fast-mana homogenization.
- **Generic Oracle/Breach conversion:** rejected as an identity-erasing attempt to force a higher bracket than The Rani's natural optimized forced-combat ceiling.
