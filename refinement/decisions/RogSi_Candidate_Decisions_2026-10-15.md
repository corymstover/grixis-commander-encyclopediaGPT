## High-impact challengers rejected

- **Last Chance:** functionally duplicates Warrior's Oath as a two-mana sorcery extra turn with the same delayed loss condition. A name-only exchange is not a material improvement, and a third effect did not beat the retained interaction/value slots.
- **Snapback:** current results are attractive, but Rograkh makes Deadly Rollick's free mode nearly automatic and exile is stronger against permanent hatebears. Chain of Vapor and Into the Flood Maw already cover bounce.
- **Chromatic Star:** inherited candidate rank #1, but the current shell does not need another low-impact cantrip artifact merely to increase artifact count; fresh tournament evidence also does not justify forcing it in.
- **Starting Town / Gemstone Mine:** viable mana, but neither materially beats the selected 24-land configuration after fetch/typed-land modernization.
- **Fellwar Stone:** good acceleration, but the shell already has exceptional fast-mana and ritual density; another two-mana rock loses to tutor/interaction quality.
- **Commandeer / Misdirection:** powerful zero-mana interaction with high card disadvantage; the retained counter suite plus Samut, Hexing Squelcher, Defense Grid, and Vexing Bauble provide a better protection profile for this list.
- **Full artifact/Monolith conversion:** legitimate current RogSi branch, but not a sufficiently clear improvement over the classic turbo-naus/Breach shell to justify rewriting this encyclopedia deck's established competitive identity.

## Rules-critical decisions

- **Rograkh as engine:** zero mana makes Mox Amber, Springleaf Drum, Paradise Mantle, Culling the Weak, Infernal Plunge, Diabolic Intent, Deflecting Swat, Fierce Guardianship, Deadly Rollick, Jeska's Will, and Flare of Duplication materially more efficient than in generic Grixis shells.
- **Silas Renn:** retained as the blue-black partner and secondary artifact-recursion engine. His combat-damage trigger gives a permission to cast the targeted artifact from the graveyard that turn; it does not waive normal timing restrictions.
- **Vexing Bauble:** symmetrical. It can counter our own zero-mana/no-mana-spent spells and must be sequenced or sacrificed accordingly. Hexing Squelcher makes our spells uncounterable, so Bauble cannot successfully counter them while Squelcher is active.
- **Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun:** gives our instant and sorcery spells split second. Split second restricts casting spells and activating nonmana abilities while the spell is on the stack; it does not stop triggered abilities such as Vexing Bauble.
- **Final Fortune / Warrior's Oath:** each creates an extra turn and a delayed lose-the-game trigger at the beginning of that extra turn's end step. The deck must win or otherwise handle the trigger before it resolves.
- **Demonic Counsel:** before delirium it can search only for a Demon card; this candidate contains no Demon card, so it is effectively an unrestricted tutor only once delirium is active.
- **Mnemonic Betrayal:** uses opponents' graveyards, does not grant land plays, follows normal timing for cards cast from exile, returns uncast cards at the next end step, and exiles itself.
- **Underworld Breach / LED / Brain Freeze:** retained primary graveyard combo. Grinding Station plus zero-mana artifacts supplies an alternate self-mill/Breach engine.
- **Tainted Pact:** candidate has 100 distinct names, preserving the compact Oracle/Pact win requirement.
