## Commander-first plan

Ashad gives the first nonlegendary artifact spell cast each turn casualty 2; paying that cost copies the spell and an artifact spell copy becomes a token. Whenever another creature is sacrificed, Ashad receives a +1/+1 counter. The list’s defining engine is therefore cheap artifact casting, repeatable qualifying sacrifice bodies, copied artifact spells/tokens, artifact ETB/death payoffs, and recursion—not generic Cyberman tribal alone.

## Decision

**Add:** Ultron, Artificial Malevolence.  
**Cut:** Dalek Drone.

Ultron costs three mana and can pay 2 whenever another nontoken artifact enters under your control to make a token copy. It gives the deck a repeatable artifact-copy engine that stacks with Ashad’s casualty copy, Iron Man, Bleeding Edge, Sculpting Steel, Phyrexian Metamorph, Auton Soldier, Esoteric Duplicator, and token-multiplication effects. Ultron’s own trigger will not loop from its generated token: the trigger requires a nontoken artifact to enter.

Dalek Drone is an excellent but expensive five-mana removal body: flying, menace, and an ETB that destroys an opponent creature and makes that player lose 3 life. The cut gives up that immediate removal/drain effect. The baseline retains several independent removal answers, including Cyber Conversion, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Noxious Gearhulk, Portal to Phyrexia, Their Name Is Death, and Delete. This is a considered engine-density trade, not a claim that Ultron wins every board-state comparison.
