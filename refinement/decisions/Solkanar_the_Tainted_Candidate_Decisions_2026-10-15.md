## Rules/strategy closeout targets

The final gate must independently verify:

- Sol'Kanar's mode history belongs to the battlefield object; exile-and-return creates a new object and resets the set of chosen modes.
- Thassa and Conjurer's Closet can be stacked with Sol'Kanar's beginning-of-end-step trigger so a useful mode resolves before the blink reset.
- Planar Incision returns the targeted artifact/creature under its owner's control, so it can recover an opponent-controlled Sol'Kanar as well as reset mode history.
- Homeward Path changes control but does not itself create a new object; if it retrieves a Sol'Kanar that was transferred by the fourth mode, the fourth mode's exile/return already created the fresh object.
- Immortal Coil's empty-graveyard ability is controlled by the current controller of Coil. Bojuka Bog or Rakdos Charm can create the empty-graveyard condition after Coil is transferred.
- Demonic Pact's chosen modes remain associated with the same permanent through control changes; transferring it after safe modes can leave the losing-game mode for the recipient.
- Captive Audience enters under an opponent's control and its controller chooses its remaining modes at that player's upkeeps.
- Conjurer's Closet does not undo the deck's donated creatures en masse, unlike Alicia Masters.
- Force of Will retains an adequate blue nonland pitch pool after the 13-card revision.

## Rejected material challengers

- **Modify Memory:** five-mana sorcery overlap with cheaper/more flexible exchange effects.
- **Abyssal Persecutor:** strong giftable lock, but not as direct a closer as Immortal Coil and can protect an opponent from losing until someone removes it.
- **Wishclaw Talisman:** efficient tutor, but handing an opponent a tutor is not the kind of liability this deck wants to create; Demonic/Vampiric already cover premium tutoring.
- **Alicia Masters, Skilled Sculptor:** her ownership reset would undermine Xantcha, Kain, Khârn, Jon Irenicus and other intentional creature gifts.
- **Sanctum of Eternity:** slower/colorless redundancy behind Homeward Path, Planar Incision and Coveted Falcon.
- **Ghostly Flicker / Essence Flux / Blur:** one-shot reset redundancy loses to repeatable Conjurer's Closet.
- **Bilbo, Luckwearer // Burglar's Plot:** five-mana exchange is redundant; creature half is generic looting/evasion.
- **Deadpool, Trading Card:** exchanges text instead of preserving/amplifying Sol'Kanar's own text.
- **Venser, Fervent Forger:** high-quality new card but six-mana reactive copying does not improve the commander's defining bottleneck.
- **Turbulent Crater / Turbulent Wetlands:** legal current typed duals, but not superior to the adopted original-dual/fetch configuration.
- **Generic Thassa's Oracle / Underworld Breach conversion:** rejected as homogenizing.
