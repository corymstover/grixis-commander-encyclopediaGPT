## Rules conclusions

- Beckett requires combat damage by three distinct Pirates to the same player during the turn.
- Daring Piracy's token may contribute to that history even though it is exiled at the next end step.
- Roaming Throne choosing Pirate doubles qualifying triggered abilities of other Pirate creatures, including Beckett's end-step trigger.
- Fiery Cannonade deals 2 damage to each non-Pirate creature and is intentionally asymmetric in this tribal shell.
- Kitesail Larcenist can temporarily remove blockers/threats by turning them into Treasure artifacts, with the acknowledged drawback of giving their controllers mana access.
- Open into Wonder at X=3 directly creates a Beckett connection window while also converting successful hits into cards.
- Zephyr Singer's convoke can delay current-turn attackers; its flying counters are primarily a persistent subsequent-combat benefit.

## Commander necessity / identity

Removing Beckett removes the deck's primary conversion mechanism: successful three-Pirate combat connections no longer become recurring nonland-permanent theft. The final build is therefore not merely Grixis Pirate goodstuff; its deployment, evasion, blocker manipulation, combat draw, and protection packages are specifically shaped around repeatedly satisfying Beckett's trigger.
