## Recommendation
Retain the historical one-slot proposal after fresh review: Talrand's Invocation OUT, Cryptic Pursuit IN. No additional mutation is justified.

Cryptic Pursuit costs the same four mana but changes the slot from a one-shot two-Drake sorcery into a repeatable engine. Each instant or sorcery cast from hand manifests the top card as a 2/2 creature, satisfying Anhelo's casualty 2 threshold on a later spell. If a face-down creature dies and the underlying card is an instant or sorcery, Pursuit can exile it and allow it to be cast until the end of the next turn. A casualty copy is not cast and therefore does not trigger Pursuit again.

The tradeoff is meaningful: Talrand's Invocation produces two evasive 2/2s immediately and can produce four when copied. Pursuit produces no body on resolution and requires later hand-cast spells. The existing list already contains numerous token/fodder engines, making the repeatable commander-specific engine the stronger identity fit.
