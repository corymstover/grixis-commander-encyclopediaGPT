# Admiral Brass, Unsinkable — Deck Quality Gates v3 Review

**Deck:** #3/62. **Cycle:** global-restart-2026-10-13. **Current status:** UNAPPROVED / UNLOCKED. Historical pre-reset approval is evidence only.

## Recommendation

Retain the historically proposed one-for-one refinement: **Revel in Riches OUT → Power Conduit IN**. No additional mutation is justified.

Power Conduit is unusually commander-specific. Admiral Brass returns a Pirate with a finality counter. Once that trigger has resolved, Conduit can remove that counter; if the Pirate later dies without a finality counter, the finality replacement effect no longer exiles it, allowing it to become a future graveyard target. Because Conduit is a noncreature artifact, its tap ability can be used on the turn it enters.

The cost is real: Revel in Riches is an independent Treasure engine and alternate win condition, while Power Conduit is narrow without a counter-bearing permanent. Commander-first optimization favors the narrower card because it directly improves Brass's defining recursion loop.

## Red-team review

Material objections considered and resolved: Power Conduit can be situational; Revel's independent win is lost; graveyard hate remains a structural vulnerability; Conduit must wait for Brass's reanimation trigger to resolve before removing the new finality counter; and generic reanimation effects could otherwise dilute commander identity. None justifies reversing the swap. The broad reanimation package remains complementary recovery rather than the deck's defining engine.

## Commander necessity

Removing Admiral Brass removes the repeatable beginning-of-combat Pirate reanimation engine and the finality-counter interaction that justifies Power Conduit. The deck would become materially closer to generic Grixis Pirate/reanimator. **PASS.**
