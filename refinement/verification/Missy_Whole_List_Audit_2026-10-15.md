## Closure observations

- The baseline’s strongest commander-specific axis is nonartifact-creature death -> face-down Cyberman conversion -> turn-face-up recycling. The refined candidate concentrates more slots on that axis.
- The largest structural liabilities were the Dalek subpackage’s high mana cost, duplicated five-mana sacrifice punishment, three Signets versus Talismans, slow/tapped mana, and Ixidron self-disabling Missy.
- Generic high-power staples were retained only where they solve a real optimized-deck requirement; commander-native interaction and combo pieces won close comparisons against generic alternatives.
- A second adversarial pass retained The Valeyard and Ugin’s Mastery after exact rules review rather than cutting them.

**Whole-list audit: PASS.**
