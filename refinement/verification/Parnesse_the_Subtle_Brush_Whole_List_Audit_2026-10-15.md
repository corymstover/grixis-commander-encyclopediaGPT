### KEEP rationale groups

- **True spell-copy engines:** Alania, Bonus Round, Chef's Kiss, Display of Power, Double Vision, Dualcaster Mage, Errant, Galvanic Iteration, Increasing Vengeance, Narset's Reversal, Primal Wellspring, Prismari, Pyromancer's Goggles, Reiterate, Replication Technique, Return the Favor, Reverberate, Thousand-Year Storm, Thunderclap Drake, Twinning Staff, Wandering Archaic, and Zevlor all create or materially multiply actual spell copies.
- **Parnesse-trigger multipliers:** Harmonic Prodigy, Veyran, Voice of Duality, and Wizard's Staff increase the number of Parnesse triggers generated by a copy event. Parnesse is a Wizard, so Wizard's Staff uses equip Wizard {1}.
- **Copy-volume payoffs:** Archmage Emeritus, Storm-Kiln Artist, Sedgemoor Witch, Ral, Storm Conduit, Professor Onyx, and Grapeshot convert casting/copying into cards, mana, bodies, or damage.
- **Protection/interaction:** Parnesse already taxes targeted opposing interaction by life; Hexing Squelcher, Deflecting Swat, Return the Favor, Chef's Kiss, An Offer You Can't Refuse, Counterspell, Mana Drain, Chaos Warp, Feed the Swarm, Narset's Reversal, and Blasphemous Act provide the complementary stack/board coverage needed by a five-mana commander.
- **Recursion/resilience:** Kess, Cormela, and Mizzix's Mastery preserve access to key instants and sorceries after interaction. Mizzix's Mastery copies cards and casts those copies rather than directly copying spells on the stack; it is retained for recursion/overload ceiling, not misclassified as a direct Parnesse trigger engine.
- **Mana/card-flow infrastructure:** the retained rocks, rituals, cost reducers, cantrips, Rhystic Study, Windfall, Frantic Search, Big Score, and 35-land floor support a five-mana commander plus several high-impact copy engines.

## Candidate-exhaustion / red-team conclusions

The final serious pass compared the candidate against the inherited top-100 research, current Parnesse EDHREC evidence, current/recent card releases, and the stale-membership blind spots caused by the inherited per-card audit. In particular, Tempt with Mayhem and Twincast required explicit reconsideration because they were present in older membership snapshots and therefore were not reliably surfaced as missing-card candidates.

Challengers including Twincast, Twinferno, Teach by Example, Storm King's Thunder, Radiant Performer, Transforming Flourish, Exterminator Magmarch, Dual Casting, Resonating Lute, Chandra, Hope's Beacon, Hive Mind, Curse of Echoes, Venser, Fervent Forger, Mercurial Spelldancer, See Double, and generic cEDH packages were tested against current slots. The deck already has high true-copy density; adding more redundant copy effects at the expense of draw, recursion, protection, payoff density, or acceleration did not produce a material net gain. Tempt with Mayhem was the exception because it is simultaneously efficient, scalable, interactive, and natively political.

The land red team preserved **35 lands** because Parnesse costs five and the deck retains multiple five-to-seven-mana engines. No land-count reduction survived review.
