# Ashad, the Lone Cyberman — Current-Cycle Whole-List Audit

**Cycle:** global-restart-2026-10-13
**State:** WORKING / UNAPPROVED / UNLOCKED
**Baseline:** frozen 2026-10-12 export plus prior-cycle evidence; historical lock does not carry through the reset.

## Commander-first target

Ashad wants the first nonlegendary artifact spell on each turn to be worth copying, reliable power-2 casualty fuel, sacrifice outlets/value, artifact recursion, and enough flash/instant-speed artifact access to exploit the per-turn wording. Generic artifact goodstuff is secondary.

## Structural review

The 99 noncommander slots were challenged by package: mana, cost reduction, draw, tutors, artifact recursion, sacrifice outlets, casualty-eligible bodies, copy targets, artifact-token payoffs, protection, interaction, and finishers.

### Mana / acceleration
Retain Academy Ruins, artifact lands, fetch/dual package, Command Tower, Xander's Lounge, Inventors' Fair, Urza's Saga, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Rakdos Signet and three Talismans. Etherium Sculptor, Foundry Inspector, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Urza and Mox Opal are high-value artifact acceleration. No additional land cut is justified.

### Artifact-copy targets / bodies
Solemn Simulacrum, Wurmcoil Engine, Noxious Gearhulk, Phyrexian Metamorph, Sculpting Steel, Cursed Mirror, Auton Soldier, Cybermen Squadron, Simulacrum Synthesizer, Worldwalker Helm and Portal to Phyrexia all create materially valuable copied permanents or artifact-token boards. Solemn remains superior to Jadar here because it is itself a qualifying artifact spell, a 2/2 casualty body, ramp and death draw.

### Sacrifice package
Ashnod's Altar, Krark-Clan Ironworks and Phyrexian Altar remain premium outlets. Esoteric Duplicator, Scrap Trawler, Goblin Welder, Emry, Disciple of the Vault, Mayhem Devil, Pitiless Plunderer, Marionette Master, Reckless Fireweaver and Ingenious Artillerist reward the artifact/sacrifice loop from different axes.

### Draw / engine
Jhoira, Thought Monitor, Thoughtcast, Mystic Forge, Thopter Spy Network, Uthros Research Craft and Baleful Strix are retained. **Canoptek Spyder is cut after re-audit:** five mana is expensive, it only sees another nontoken artifact creature/Vehicle entering, and notably does not reward Ashad's token copies. **Canoptek Scarab Swarm** is tested instead: when it enters, it exiles a graveyard and creates a tapped 1/1 flying Insect artifact token for each artifact/land card exiled. Copying the Swarm with Ashad produces another ETB and another graveyard target; it is simultaneously graveyard interaction and an artifact-token finisher. Its 4/4 body can later pay casualty.

### Fodder production
**Sai, Master Thopterist** replaces Poetic Ingenuity. Sai creates a 1/1 flying Thopter artifact token whenever an artifact spell is cast, and can sacrifice two artifacts to draw. Individual Thopters do not meet casualty 2, but the deck's token multipliers, artifact count and sacrifice payoffs make Sai substantially more central to the artifact engine than Poetic Ingenuity's narrower historic-triggered 3/3 Dinosaur/Treasure plan.

### Low-impact artifact slot
**Dalek Drone → Ichor Wellspring** is a working change. Drone is a 3/3 artifact creature with a small opponent-life-loss ETB and menace; copying it doubles a modest ETB. Wellspring is only two mana, draws on ETB and again when put into a graveyard from battlefield. If it is the first nonlegendary artifact spell, Ashad can casualty-copy it for two ETB draws; the token copy can then be sacrificed to KCI or other effects for another draw. It is a cleaner low-curve copy target and sacrifice resource.

## Candidate challenges / rejects

- **Jadar:** recurring 2/2 but nonartifact and end-step constrained; Solemn better fits the artifact-copy axis.
- **Canoptek Spyder:** respectable nontoken-artifact draw engine, but does not trigger from Ashad token copies and is five mana.
- **Twinning Staff:** text applies to copying instant/sorcery spells, not Ashad's artifact-spell copies.
- **Poetic Ingenuity:** flavorful artifact/token support but less concentrated than Sai's artifact-cast engine.
- **Dalek Drone:** on-theme but low ceiling compared with a cheap artifact that replaces itself multiple times through sacrifice.
- **Biotransference:** retained. Six mana is expensive, but making all creatures artifacts and creating 2/2 Necron artifact creature tokens on artifact casts is unusually aligned with both casualty eligibility and artifact payoffs.
- **Portal to Phyrexia:** retained despite nine mana; a copied Portal is an exceptional Ashad payoff and artifact recursion/reanimation target.
- **Cybermen Squadron:** retained despite six mana; Myriad plus artifact-creature typing creates a legitimate combat finisher and copy target.
- **Unwinding Clock:** retained because artifact-mana untaps and Shimmer Myr enable using Ashad's 'first ... each turn' wording beyond the controller's turn.
- **Padeem:** retained as protection/card advantage for a permanent-heavy artifact engine.
- **Brudiclad / Cyberdrive Awakener:** retained as token-board conversion finishers.
