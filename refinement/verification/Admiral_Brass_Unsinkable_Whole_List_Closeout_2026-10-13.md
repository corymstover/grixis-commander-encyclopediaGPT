## Objective

Make Admiral Brass's five-mana, beginning-of-combat Pirate reanimation with finality counters the primary engine. All 99 noncommander slots reviewed for synergy, opportunity cost, and commander necessity, rather than limiting review to the earlier one-card swap.

## Proposed changes vs frozen baseline

1. **Revel in Riches → Power Conduit.** Directly removes finality counters after reanimation, enabling reuse after a later death. Does not remove the finality counter before Brass's trigger resolves.
2. **Windfall → Brass's Tunnel-Grinder.** Selective graveyard seeding without refilling opponents' hands; discard any number then draw that many plus one. Can transform after three qualifying end steps, not instantly. Reverse face Tecutlan discovers on permanent spells cast with its mana.
3. **Ghost of Ramirez DePietro → Mary Read and Anne Bonny.** Replaces conditional postcombat card retrieval with hasty Pirate looting and Treasure generation from discarding Pirates/Islands. Better immediate graveyard setup for Brass; still vulnerable to removal and requires tapping.

## Rejected candidates and tradeoffs

- **Nesting Grounds:** can move finality counters to another permanent, but *only at sorcery speed*. It cannot rescue a newly returned Pirate during combat, and a colorless-producing land burdens UBR requirements. Power Conduit is better for immediate post-trigger removal.
- **Soul Diviner:** removes counters and draws, but a freshly cast creature cannot normally tap immediately; non-Pirate creature slot. Power Conduit is faster.
- **The Grim Captain's Locker:** four-mana surveil/escape engine competes with Brass's own reanimation and consumes graveyard cards for escape.
- **Storm Fleet Negotiator:** flying Pirate and attack-based Maps, but symmetrical card draw and no discard on entry; lower priority than immediate looting.
- **Kitesail Larcenist:** good removal Pirate, but ETB makes opponent's permanent a Treasure and can accelerate opponent; existing interactive Pirate package is adequate.
- **Arm-Mounted Anchor:** requires equip and successful attack to filter; slower than Tunnel-Grinder and Mary Read.
- **Back in Town:** mass outlaw recursion duplicates Living Death and Patriarch's Bidding, less efficient than existing options.
- **Warkite Marauder:** cheap evasive Pirate but modest graveyard/ETB value as a 4/4 reanimation target.
- **Dire Fleet Ravager:** burst damage ETB but competes with existing Angrath's Marauders, Port Razer and other stronger top-end Pirate targets.
- **Port of Karfell / Nephalia Drownyard:** expensive graveyard utility and colored-source opportunity costs; insufficient upside over present land suite.
- **Revel in Riches:** Treasure/alternate win is powerful but a generic five-mana parallel strategy, displaced by commander-specific counter management.
- **Windfall:** strongest when hand is low, but gives opponents new hands and cannot choose how many cards to discard; Brass's Tunnel-Grinder better at placing selected Pirates in graveyard.
- **Ghost of Ramirez DePietro:** reanimation yields a 4/4 with narrow evasion and requires combat damage plus a card discarded/milled *that turn*; Mary Read advances graveyard seeding proactively.
