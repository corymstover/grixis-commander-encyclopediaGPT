## #1 Abaddon the Despoiler — RULES PASS

Current Abaddon text grants cascade only **during your turn** to spells **cast from your hand** whose mana value is at most the total life opponents have lost that turn.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- The threshold uses life actually lost, not attempted/prevented damage. Damage that causes life loss can build the threshold; effects that directly cause life loss also do so.
- A spell cast from exile by cascade is not cast from hand and does not receive Abaddon's grant merely because it was generated by a qualifying spell.
- Cascade's own mana-value restriction still applies to the card hit by cascade.
- Copying a spell is not casting it, so copies do not independently satisfy cast triggers.
- `Underworld Breach` currently gives nonland cards in the graveyard escape for their mana cost plus exiling three other cards. `Lion's Eye Diamond` currently discards the hand and sacrifices itself for three mana as a mana ability. `Brain Freeze` currently mills three and has storm. The documented Breach / LED / Brain Freeze line remains rules-coherent when the stated graveyard, mana, and spell-count prerequisites are present; it is not represented as an unconditional infinite or as interaction-proof.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/metadata_audit/ABADDON_CLOSEOUT_DECISION_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #2 Admiral Beckett Brass — RULES PASS

Current Beckett text checks at the beginning of your end step for a player who was dealt combat damage by **three or more Pirates** that turn, then targets a nonland permanent that player controls.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- Three qualifying Pirate sources must have dealt combat damage to the same player during the turn; the permanents need not still be on the battlefield at the end step for the turn-history condition to have happened.
- `Roaming Throne` choosing Pirate causes a triggered ability of another Pirate creature you control to trigger an additional time; this can duplicate Beckett's qualifying end-step trigger.
- Evasion / blocker-manipulation effects in the approved list support the combat-history requirement but do not waive it.
- `Open into Wonder` may create an unblockable connection window for the selected creatures; the resulting Beckett condition still depends on actual combat damage.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/verification/Admiral_Beckett_Brass_Approval_Ready_Closeout_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #3 Admiral Brass, Unsinkable — RULES PASS

Current Admiral Brass text mills four on entry and, at beginning of combat on your turn, may return a Pirate creature card from your graveyard with a finality counter, base P/T 4/4, and haste until end of turn.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- A finality counter creates the relevant replacement condition only while that counter remains on the permanent. `Power Conduit` can remove a counter from a permanent you control as an activation cost; removing the finality counter before the creature would be put into a graveyard means that counter no longer supplies the exile replacement for that later event.
- Power Conduit does not need to remove the counter before Brass's trigger resolves; the creature must first be on the battlefield to have its counter removed.
- `Roaming Throne` choosing Pirate causes Brass's beginning-of-combat triggered ability to trigger an additional time because Brass is a Pirate creature and Throne applies to another creature of the chosen type.
- The 4/4 base-setting effect and the until-end-of-turn haste granted by Brass are distinct from the finality counter; removing the counter does not itself erase those other effects.
- `Brass's Tunnel-Grinder` and `Mary Read and Anne Bonny` remain graveyard-seeding engines under their current text; they do not bypass Brass's requirement that the reanimation target be a Pirate creature card.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/verification/Admiral_Brass_Unsinkable_Whole_List_Closeout_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #4 Anhelo, the Painter — RULES PASS

Current Anhelo text gives the first instant or sorcery spell you cast each turn casualty 2.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- Casualty is paid while casting by sacrificing a creature with the required power; the sacrifice is part of the casting process and is already paid before opponents can respond to the resulting spell / casualty trigger.
- The casualty-created spell object is a **copy**, not a cast spell.
- `Deekah, Fractal Theorist` currently triggers whenever you **cast or copy** an instant or sorcery; an Anhelo casualty sequence therefore supplies a trigger for the original cast and another for the casualty copy.
- `Startle` copies resolve independently, so each resolving copy performs its own instructions, including its own draw and token creation.
- `Dualcaster Mage` + `Twinflame` remains a deterministic repeated-copy line when Twinflame legally targets a creature you control and Dualcaster's ETB copies Twinflame targeting the newest Dualcaster token; the produced copies have haste and can be converted to combat damage. The core loop does not require paying Anhelo casualty.
- Magecraft / copy payoffs such as `Storm-Kiln Artist` and `Ral, Storm Conduit` distinguish correctly between casting and copying according to their own current text.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/verification/Anhelo_the_Painter_Approval_Ready_Closeout_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #5 Ashad, the Lone Cyberman — RULES PASS

Current Ashad text gives the first nonlegendary artifact spell you cast each turn casualty 2, explicitly noting that a copy of an artifact spell becomes a token as it resolves.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- Casualty's sacrifice is an additional casting cost; the copied permanent spell is not cast and becomes a token as it resolves.
- `Twinning Staff` currently says that if you would copy **a spell** one or more times, you instead copy it that many times plus one. That replacement effect is not limited to instants and sorceries; therefore it applies to Ashad copying a nonlegendary artifact permanent spell. Staff's separate activated ability is the part limited to an instant or sorcery spell.
- A copied `Solemn Simulacrum` spell resolves as its own token permanent and supplies its own enter-the-battlefield trigger.
- `Ichor Wellspring` copies resolve as token artifacts and each token is a distinct permanent for its own enter / graveyard-from-battlefield triggers.
- Flash-granting artifact infrastructure can exploit Ashad's “first ... each turn” wording on other players' turns when normal timing restrictions are legitimately changed.
- Ashad's +1/+1 trigger is for sacrificing **another** creature; sacrificing Ashad itself does not satisfy that particular trigger.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/verification/Ashad_the_Lone_Cyberman_Approval_Ready_Closeout_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #6 Ashnod the Uncaring — RULES PASS WITH DOCUMENTATION CORRECTION

Current Ashnod text is:

> Whenever you activate an ability of an artifact or creature that isn't a mana ability, if one or more permanents were sacrificed to activate it, you may copy that ability. You may choose new targets for the copy. (Sacrificing an artifact for mana to activate an ability doesn't count.)

This fresh text exposed a stale historical interpretation. Current Ashnod has **no once-per-turn restriction** and does **not** require the sacrificed permanent to be nontoken.

Material conclusions under current text:
- `Oni-Cult Anvil`, `Arcbound Ravager`, `Trading Post` sacrifice modes, `Repurposing Bay`, `Time Sieve`, and other nonmana artifact/creature activated abilities qualify when one or more permanents are sacrificed as an activation cost.
- The copied ability does not require paying the activation cost again.
- `Ashnod's Altar` and `Krark-Clan Ironworks` are mana abilities, so Ashnod does not copy those activations.
- A sacrifice that occurs only on resolution and was not paid to **activate** the ability does not satisfy Ashnod; the prior `Goblin Welder` noninteraction conclusion therefore remains correct.
- Multiple separate qualifying activations in the same turn can each trigger Ashnod under the current text, subject to the individual permanents' tap costs, timing restrictions, and available resources.

The stale wording is explicitly repaired in `refinement/verification/retrospective_1_to_8/Ashnod_Oracle_Correction_2026-10-14.md`. This correction strengthens the approved engine and does not require a card-list change.

**Result: PASS.**

## #7 Be'lakor, the Dark Master — RULES PASS

Current Be'lakor text draws / loses X on its own entry based on Demons controlled, and whenever **another** Demon enters, that entering Demon deals damage equal to its power to any target.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- Be'lakor entering does not trigger its own Lord of Torment because that ability requires another Demon.
- `Spark Double` may enter as a copy of Be'lakor you control; its modification makes the copy nonlegendary and gives it an additional +1/+1 counter. It therefore avoids the legend rule, enters as a Demon, triggers its copied Prince of Chaos ability, and is seen by the original Be'lakor as another Demon entering. The original Lord trigger uses the entering copied Demon's power when it resolves.
- `Roaming Throne` choosing Demon causes triggered abilities of another Demon creature you control to trigger an additional time when its condition occurs.
- `Liliana's Contract` currently requires four or more Demons **with different names** at the beginning of upkeep; token / copy quantity alone does not bypass the different-name condition.
- `Heartless Summoning` power reduction is a real interaction cost because Be'lakor's Lord trigger uses the entering Demon's power; it can also eliminate sufficiently small creatures through toughness reduction.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/evidence/Be_lakor_the_Dark_Master_Approval_Gate_Dossier_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS.**

## #8 Bjorna // Cecily + Lutri — RULES PASS / PROJECT EXCEPTIONS PRESERVED

Current Bjorna text uses `{T}, Sacrifice an artifact` as an activation cost, targets a **creature**, deals 1 to it and goads it. Current Cecily text draws one and loses one when she attacks, then checks whether you have eleven or more cards in hand and may cast an instant or sorcery from hand without paying its mana cost.

Material conclusions rechecked:
- Bjorna's artifact sacrifice is paid before the ability can be responded to. Bjorna cannot target a player with that ability.
- Cecily performs the draw / life-loss instruction before checking the eleven-card threshold, so the drawn card can make the condition true for that same trigger.
- `Robe of the Archmagi` currently triggers on **combat damage** and draws that many cards. Its draw happens after Cecily's attack trigger has already resolved, so Robe cannot raise the hand to eleven for that same attack; it supports later attacks. Cecily is a Wizard, so the Robe's Wizard equip cost applies.
- A spell Cecily casts with her trigger is still cast **from hand** even though its mana cost is not paid. Additional costs remain relevant; an alternative cost cannot also be chosen in place of the already supplied “without paying its mana cost” alternative.
- `Harmonic Prodigy` can cause Cecily's triggered ability to trigger an additional time because Cecily is a Wizard; each trigger resolves separately and checks the hand condition during its own resolution.
- `Lutri, the Spellchaser` currently requires each nonland card in the starting deck to have a different name. The machine gate independently passes that companion condition. Lutri remains outside the 100-card deck, can be put into hand through the companion special action, then is cast normally. Its ETB copies a target instant or sorcery spell you control only if Lutri itself was cast.

Official / project legality remains deliberately split:
- `Lutri, the Spellchaser`: officially `banned_as_companion`; project-authorized companion exception.
- `Trade Secrets`: officially banned; project-authorized main-deck exception.
- `Yawgmoth's Bargain`: officially banned; project-authorized main-deck exception.

The deck is therefore **project-valid under the registered exceptions, not officially Commander-legal without qualification**.

Historical detailed rules record retained: `refinement/evidence/Cecily__Bjorna__Lutri_Approval_Gate_Dossier_2026-10-13.md`.

**Result: PASS / PROJECT_EXCEPTION.**
