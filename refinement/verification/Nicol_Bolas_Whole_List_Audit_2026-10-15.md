### KEEP rationale groups

- **Commander acceleration:** Ancient Tomb, Arcane Signet, Cabal Ritual, Dark Ritual, Dimir/Izzet/Rakdos Signet, Fellwar Stone, Jeska's Will, Mana Vault, Nightscape Familiar, Seething Song, Sol Ring, and the three Talismans remain because an eight-mana `UUBBRR` commander with a recurring `UBR` upkeep requires both speed and colored precision.
- **Protection / interaction:** Bedevil, Chaos Warp, Counterspell, Cyclonic Rift, Force of Will, Mana Drain, Sheoldred's Edict, Terminate, Toxic Deluge, Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Brotherhood Regalia, and No Mercy survive because deploying Nicol without protection or blocker control is too large a tempo risk.
- **Discard conversion:** Court of Ambition, Geth's Grimoire, Hypnotic Specter, Liliana's Caress, Oppression, Raiders' Wake, Tergrid, Tinybones, Trinket Thief, Torment of Hailfire, and Waste Not all convert the commander's hand wipe into pressure, cards, mana, stolen permanents, or an empty-hand payoff.
- **Card advantage / control:** Curiosity, Mystic Remora, Rhystic Study, and Notion Thief remain. Curiosity becomes stronger after refinement because the candidate creates substantially more Nicol-sourced damage events.
- **Extra-combat package:** Aggravated Assault and Sword of Feast and Famine remain together. A successful Sword hit untaps lands; if at least five usable mana is available, the following main phase can fund another Assault activation and another combat.
- **Flavor without homogenization:** Nicol Bolas, the Ravager and The Eldest Reborn remain as strong Bolas-flavored support, but the deck is not converted into the planeswalker/Superfriends shell that belongs more naturally to the next encyclopedia commander.
- **Retained lands:** Blood Crypt, Bloodstained Mire, Bojuka Bog, City of Brass, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Hall of the Bandit Lord, Island x2, Luxury Suite, Morphic Pool, Mountain, Otawara, Polluted Delta, Scalding Tarn, Steam Vents, Swamp, Takenuma, Training Center, Watery Grave, and Xander's Lounge all provide premium fixing, fetchability, basics, or unique utility that survives the mana-base comparison.

## Audit closure

- Every one of the **99 baseline noncommander copies** is explicitly present above as KEEP or CUT.
- **17 of the 29 cuts are land replacements.** The strategic spell package changes only 12 baseline nonland slots.
- The baseline's exact land count is **39**, correcting an earlier verbal misstatement. The candidate stops at **37**, rather than forcing a low-land cEDH configuration.
- Generic Bolas planeswalker piles, wheel packages, infect shortcuts, generic Oracle/Consultation, and unrelated deterministic Breach wins were not forced into the list.
- The central upgrade criterion is whether a card makes **Nicol Bolas himself deal damage**, lets that damage happen immediately/safely, or materially converts the resulting opponent discards into advantage.
- Final serious strategic pass after the 29-card delta: **ZERO NEW MATERIAL IMPROVEMENTS**.
