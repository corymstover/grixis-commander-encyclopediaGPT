### KEEP rationale groups

- **Commander acceleration / transform mana:** Ancient Tomb, Arcane Signet, the Signets/Talismans, Fellwar Stone, Chrome Mox, Lotus Petal, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Mox Opal, Sol Ring, and Jeska's Will survive because Ravager wants to be cast on curve and then produce seven mana for the transform activation.
- **Planeswalker core:** Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God; Nicol Bolas, God-Pharaoh; Nicol Bolas, the Deceiver; Liliana, Dreadhorde General; Narset; both Teferis; Vraska; The Chain Veil; Ichormoon Gauntlet; Deepglow Skate; Flux Channeler; Inexorable Tide; Tekuthal; Spark Double; Jaya's Phoenix; Liliana's Talent; Tezzeret's Gambit; Karn's Bastion; and The Elderspell all directly multiply, protect, copy, accelerate, or capitalize on loyalty abilities.
- **Bolas / graveyard identity:** Dark Intimations and The Eldest Reborn remain because both connect discard/removal to creature-or-planeswalker recursion, which supports the Arisen's -4 rather than replacing it.
- **Commander-created discard payoff:** Tergrid and Tinybones remain because Ravager naturally makes every opponent discard on ETB and the refined Displacer Kitten package can repeat that ETB without requiring a wheel subtheme.
- **Premium control:** An Offer, Cyclonic Rift, Damnation, Deadly Rollick, Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will, Mana Drain, Swan Song, Toxic Deluge, Demonic Tutor, Mystical Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Gamble, Mystic Remora, Rhystic Study, and Bolas's Citadel remain appropriate at the deck's Bracket-4 ceiling.
- **Mana base:** retained lands provide typed-dual fetchability, premium fixing, channel utility, multiplayer fixing, or proliferate utility. Karn's Bastion is specifically protected as a commander-native loyalty engine.

## 16 CUT copies

| Card | Decision basis |
|---|---|
| Dark Deal | Wheel/discard package is being reduced; one-shot mass hand cycling is less important than directly accelerating the commander's transform ability. |
| Liliana of the Veil | The lowest-impact retained-style planeswalker in multiplayer; The Theorist, Jace Beleren supplies much stronger multiplayer card advantage and board interaction at the same strategic layer. |
| Nicol Bolas | Original Nicol's damage-to-full-hand-discard identity now belongs to locked deck #41; retaining it here unnecessarily homogenizes adjacent encyclopedia entries. |
| Notion Thief | Its strongest purpose here is the generic wheel lock, which is deliberately being removed; planeswalker-specific ability multiplication is a better use of the slot. |
| Opposition Agent | Powerful generic Grixis stax but not meaningfully connected to Ravager's ETB, transform, planeswalker face, or proliferate plan. |
| Orcish Bowmasters | Efficient generic interaction, but Dreamtide Whale is a much more commander-specific multiplayer proliferate engine. |
| Sheoldred, the Apocalypse | Premium generic value but not necessary to the Superfriends/transform plan; Tezzeret the Seeker tutors and untaps the artifact engines that actually drive the deck. |
| The One Ring | Generic draw engine; Rowan's Talent directly doubles the loyalty abilities of the transformed commander or another key planeswalker. |
| Waste Not | Its ceiling depends on preserving a larger wheel/discard shell; Displacer Kitten more directly repeats Ravager ETBs and reuses Deepglow Skate while remaining optional after transformation. |
| Wheel of Fortune | Wheel density overlaps Nekusar/Gwendlyn and refills opponents after discard; Deliver Unto Evil instead rewards successfully establishing a Bolas planeswalker and recovers up to four graveyard cards. |
| Windfall | Same identity concern as Wheel of Fortune; Ripples of Potential is cheaper, proliferates, and can phase out loyalty-bearing permanents for protection. |
| Bojuka Bog | Entering tapped and exiling a graveyard can conflict with the Arisen's -4; Cavern of Souls better protects the commander cast while preserving land count. |
| Shipwreck Marsh | Conditional slowland loses to Reflecting Pool in this premium three-color mana base. |
| Sulfurous Springs | Painland becomes Wooded Foothills for typed-dual access and cleaner three-color sequencing. |
