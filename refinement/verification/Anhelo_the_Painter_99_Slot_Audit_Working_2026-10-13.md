## Working changes from frozen baseline

- Talrand's Invocation → Cryptic Pursuit (historical proposal retained for independent current-cycle consideration).
- Fury Storm → Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia (new working proposal; **not approved**). Fury Storm copies a target instant/sorcery spell once for each time the commander has been cast from command zone, making it inconsistent early; Jadar supplies a 2/2 decayed casualty body each end step if none currently exists. Jadar's end-step condition and inability to make an immediate body on casting are real costs.

## Fresh research

Current EDHREC Anhelo commander data shows Jadar in roughly 63% of 6.33k decks and Cryptic Pursuit in roughly 47%; this is discovery evidence, not a justification by popularity alone. Deekah, Fractal Theorist and Metallurgic Summonings are legitimate high-cost alternatives that make creature tokens from casting spells; Deekah also triggers on copied spells. Their 5-mana investment competes with current copy engines, and token sizes can be below 2 for cheap spells, so neither is automatically accepted.

## Remaining closure tests

- Recheck whether Flawless Forgery, Maestros Ascendancy, and other 4–5-mana engines are worth their setup and fodder costs.
- Compare Jadar against 2-power recursion bodies, Sedgemoor Witch (Pests only 1/1), and instant-speed 2/2 makers.
- Verify exact current Oracle text and color identity of all newly accepted cards; audit baseline against current Scryfall overlay.
- Complete final 100-card/singleton recount, mana base review, combo validation, and zero-new-material-improvement pass before approval.
