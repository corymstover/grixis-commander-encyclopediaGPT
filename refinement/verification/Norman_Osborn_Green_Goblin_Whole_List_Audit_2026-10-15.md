### KEEP rationale groups

- **Compact cEDH wins:** Thassa's Oracle, Demonic Consultation, Tainted Pact, Underworld Breach, Brain Freeze, and Lion's Eye Diamond remain because this commander has a demonstrated Bracket-5 ceiling and can support compact Grixis wins without becoming commander-irrelevant.
- **Commander-native discard / Mayhem:** Big Score, Dark Deal, Careful Study, Faithless Looting, Frantic Search, Gamble, Psychic Frog, Shadow of the Goblin, Unstable Experiment, Wheel of Fortune, Will of the Jeskai, and Windfall all either discard cards directly or turn hand resources into graveyard resources Green Goblin can cast. Their value is evaluated after the commander's `{2}` generic graveyard-cast reduction, not only by printed rate.
- **Fast mana:** Ancient Tomb, Arcane Signet, Chrome Mox, Culling the Weak, Dark Ritual, Fellwar Stone, Jeska's Will, LED, Lotus Petal, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Mox Opal, Rite of Flame, Sol Ring, and all three Talismans support fast Norman deployment, transformation, and compact win turns.
- **Tutors:** Beseech the Mirror, Demonic Counsel, Demonic Tutor, Gamble, Mystical Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, and Wishclaw Talisman preserve cEDH consistency.
- **Protection / stack interaction:** Chain of Vapor, Deflecting Swat, Fierce Guardianship, Flusterstorm, Force of Will, Hexing Squelcher, Mental Misstep, Mindbreak Trap, Pact of Negation, Red Elemental Blast, and Swan Song keep the deck capable of forcing through or stopping compact wins.
- **Card advantage:** Mystic Remora, Rhystic Study, Ragavan, Orcish Bowmasters, and Birgi/Harnfel remain high-impact cEDH engines that coexist with the commander-native package.
- **Retained lands:** Badlands, Blood Crypt, Bloodstained Mire, City of Brass, Command Tower, Mana Confluence, Otawara, Polluted Delta, Scalding Tarn, Steam Vents, Underground Sea, and Watery Grave are premium fixing, fetches, or unique interaction lands.
