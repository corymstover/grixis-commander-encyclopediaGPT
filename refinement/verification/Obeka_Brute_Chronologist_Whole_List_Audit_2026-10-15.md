### KEEP rationale groups

- **Core risky-turn package:** Final Fortune, Last Chance, Warrior's Oath, Alchemist's Gambit, Glorious End, Discontinuity, Sundial of the Infinite, and the tutor suite remain because ending the turn is the deck's defining rules engine.
- **Commander-native temporary-value package:** Flameshadow Conjuring, Delina, Jaxis, Rionya, Saheeli, Feldon, Puppeteer Clique, Sneak Attack, Purphoros, Ilharg, Kiki-Jiki, Twinflame, and reanimation spells all generate temporary creatures/tokens or access creatures whose liabilities Obeka can exploit.
- **Hellkite Courser:** retained after adversarial review. It cheats Obeka through commander tax, gives her haste, and its delayed return trigger can be removed with Obeka. Current optimized Obeka data treats it as a high-lift core card.
- **Identity draw engines:** Ideas Unbound, Avaricious Dragon, and Psychic Vortex are retained because their drawbacks are unusually compatible with Obeka's end-turn ability.
- **Interaction/protection:** the retained counter/removal package is appropriate for Bracket 4 and protects a four-mana commander whose tap activation is often the safety valve for risky lines.
- **Mana:** all retained lands/rocks either provide premium fixing, speed, channel utility, or enough basic access to support the final 34-land structure.
