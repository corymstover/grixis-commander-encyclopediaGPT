## Commander blueprint

The deck is an exile-theft / opponent-library resource engine. Jester turns library exile caused by your spells and abilities into playable cards through the end of your next turn, while the attack trigger supplies its own three-opponent burst. The natural optimized ceiling is cEDH because the commander converts unusually cheap exile effects into card-selection/tutor-like access while Grixis supports compact Breach and Oracle finishes.

## Commander-first effects of the changes

- The planeswalker swap reduces the exile-engine cost from five to three mana and makes library exile immediate rather than token/combat mediated.
- Dauthi Voidwalker converts qualifying opponent-library-to-graveyard events caused by your spells/abilities into exile, creating additional Jester triggers while providing efficient graveyard hate.
- Lightning Greaves makes the four-mana 3/2 commander immediately attack-capable and protects it without interfering with its non-targeting triggers.
- Demonic Consultation and Tainted Pact complete the already-present Thassa's Oracle finish, converting an otherwise incomplete cEDH win package into a compact one without removing the commander-specific exile core.
- Removing Brainstealer Dragon, Etali and Thief of Sanity sharply reduces reliance on expensive or combat-damage-dependent theft bodies.
