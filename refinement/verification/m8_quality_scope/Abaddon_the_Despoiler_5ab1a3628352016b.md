**October 28, 2026 — KEEP EXACT APPROVED REV3 100; no cuts/adds.** Separate Cory exact-SHA approval on October 23 supersedes Rev2; the research candidate's historical approval-wait wording does not override the subsequent owner lock. Natural bracket 4 Optimized; zero exceptions, 34 actual lands, 95 distinct names; basic Island ×3, Mountain ×2, Swamp ×3. **Commander-first axis:** On our turn an actual spell cast **from hand** with mana value at most the *total* life all opponents have lost this turn acquires Abaddon cascade. Damage before casting the qualifying hand spell establishes the threshold; damage caused by that spell after casting cannot retroactively grant it cascade. Casting from exile/graveyard does not qualify, but printed native cascade and nonhand-cast Warhammer payoffs remain real. Multiple live Abaddon-like triggers from Sakashima can stack, but each independent cascade checks its own legal hit and casting.

**Mana and defense:** 34 lands including paid black/red Mount Doom, eight basic lands, three Talismans, Sol Ring/Mana Vault/Chrome Mox/LED and rituals make a tuned five-mana colored commander possible, not guaranteed on turn three. Mount Doom activation costs 1BR and tap to ping three opponents; Sanctum triggers on upkeep after surviving a turn. Pact/Force/Fierce/Swat/Pyroblast/Flusterstorm and real removal protect only when legitimately payable/legal. Rule of Law or Drannith Magistrate can stop hits from exile, and graveyard hate stops Breach.

**Win counterfactuals:** With Birgi, Nightscape Familiar, Grinning Ignus and red to activate, each return/recast can create net generic mana and an Impact Tremors enter-ping, but requires payable repeated actions, alive permanents and no Rule of Law. Neheb+Aggravated Assault requires actual prior combat life loss and 3RR per extra combat; Loyal Subordinate, Solphim, Sheoldred plus Wheel, and printed Call Forth/Noise Marine/Nalfeshnee create different pressures, not guaranteed kills. Direct alternative substitutions Ojer over Solphim, Veyran over Familiar, Lightning Greaves over Pact, Keeper of Secrets over Sakashima and Warhammer seven-swap stress all lose meaningful protected functionality.

**Historic I/J boundary:** Abaddon's own Stage20 92/92 engineering CI, M7 independent Rev3 review and 95-name Oracle reconciliation are archived evidence for this approved hash, but those trials do not demonstrate independently sampled multiplayer wins or all opponent response trees. No M6 `test_blueprints/Abaddon...json` exists among the four neighboring original design-only blueprints, so the labeled cases here are a **new bounded review**, not forged original group IDs.
