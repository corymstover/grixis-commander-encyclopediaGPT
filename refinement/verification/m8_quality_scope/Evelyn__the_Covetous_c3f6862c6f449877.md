## Commander-specific evaluation, mana/protection and adverse win lines

- **Commander first:** Evelyn has flash. Each Evelyn or other Vampire entering exiles one top card of each player's library with collection counter. While a live Evelyn permission applies, a card exiled by an ability we controlled can be played from exile **once per turn for that Evelyn's permission**, subject to actual land-play limits, mandatory costs/targets and any-color *spending for spell casting*. Prior M6 K1's absolute 'one across all Evelyn copies' is not an independently proven global rule here: distinct copied Evelyn objects require separate permission adjudication, so never report an automatic global one or unlimited plays. Insolent Neonate is the one added cheap Vampire/discard outlet, not an unrequested tribal rebuild.

- **Competitive mana and safety:** Only 26 lands; 19 game changers and many fast mana pieces (LED, Mox Diamond/Opal, Petal, Chrome Mox, Rituals). Marsh Flats and Verdant Catacombs replace tapland and Verge to improve black dual fetches/Brainstorm shuffle but pay life, can be stopped and reduce unconditional black source. Burnt Offering sacrifices a controlled creature for B/R mana using mana value, a real cost rather than unconditional early ritual; this and Neonate trade off Spellskite and Redirect Lightning target-protection. Multiple hard counters, Hexing Squelcher, Defense Grid and Stifle remain; Vexing Bauble may also counter our own no-mana-spent Forces/Pact/Petal.

- **Worldgorger/Oracle/Breach lines:** Animate Dead/Dance/Necromancy on Worldgorger can recur battlefield exiles/returns and tap lands for mana; if triggers cannot be ended by an actual legal exit, mandatory loop may draw the game. Insolent Neonate can provide an instant-speed discard outlet only with a real card to discard. Evelyn entries may exile our own finite library; they do not provide limitless free casts. Oracle + Consultation/Pact and Breach + LED + Brain Freeze are separate real win lines but need actual stack, library, graveyard and opponent-interaction windows; no sampled cEDH win-rate claim.

- **Spell-control friction and owner lock:** Vexing Bauble must be removed/sacrificed at the correct time before zero-mana alternative-cost spells; own free counters and Gitaxian Probe can fail with Bauble active. The owner's preserved Ripples of Undeath, Saw in Half, Windfall/Notion Thief, Leyline and Mistrise remain; explicit previously rejected Kitten, extra generic sweepers and Vampire package remain excluded. No legitimate reason to displace combo protection for marginal Vampire volume.

## Whole-100 counterfactual challenge / why stop here

**Exact approved swaps:** Marsh Flats ← Thundering Falls; Insolent Neonate ← Spellskite; Brainstorm ← Redirect Lightning; Burnt Offering ← Wan Shi Tong, Librarian; Verdant Catacombs ← Blazemire Verge. Exactly five survived independent adversarial challenge; no made-up sixth change.

Whole-100 challenges considered keeping Spellskite or Redirect for target protection, Spell Pierce over explicit owner-kept Ripples, Grim Monolith over Burnt Offering for colorless acceleration, Intuition over Praetor's Grasp, additional Vampire Hexmage/Harvester, Daze around self-Bauble. Each loses a uniquely useful color-protection/Worldgorger-exit/owner-retained axis without provable better cEDH execution. Status bounded; combo lines not playtested.

**M8 scope disposition:** Keep exact October23 approved 100, not a new swap proposal. Historic I/J cannot be silently rebranded as current independently passed; overall M8 release requires remaining decks and genuine P1 dispositions/owner acceptance.
