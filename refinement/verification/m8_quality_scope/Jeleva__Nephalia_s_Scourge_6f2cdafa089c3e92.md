# M8 exact-current 99-library quality audit — #24 Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge

**October 28, 2026 — KEEP EXACT OWNER-APPROVED REV1 100.** Cory explicitly waived five-swap minimum **for Jeleva only** following an independent 23-configuration full-100 challenge, approving precisely Cavern of Souls ← City of Traitors, Fire Covenant ← Toxic Deluge. 100 singleton names, 27 actual lands, 45 instants/sorceries, Power Bracket 5 fringe cEDH, no project exceptions.

**Commander-first cast vs entry:** Jeleva is a Vampire Wizard, flying, and on entering exiles top X of **each** player's library where X is *actual mana spent to cast this Jeleva object*, including commander tax paid, excluding mana costs reduced/unpaid. If cheated into play without being cast X=0. An attack trigger lets one spell instant or sorcery actually exiled **by that specific Jeleva object** be cast without paying mana cost during trigger resolution, subject to additional costs, targeting and legal casting restrictions. Each new Jeleva object cannot inherit exiles linked to an older object, and if she dies before declaring attack no attack trigger happens.

**Harmonic Prodigy boundary:** Because Jeleva is a Wizard, live Prodigy adds one instance of her ETB and attack triggered abilities. Two ETBs exile separate X groups; two attack triggers can cast two distinct eligible linked exiled spells, not twice the same removed card and not any spell previously exiled by older Jeleva incarnation. Actual spells cast off attacks interact with storm and commander-only timing independently; their mana value still matters for actual costs. Top-staging Scroll Rack/Top/Brainstorm/Long-Term Plans can prepare a spell but the library may change before entry.

**Mana and defenses:** The 27-land turbo configuration has rocks/rituals and haste tools (Torch Courier, Greaves, Arena of Glory etc.) but cannot assume early UBR or a successful attack. Cavern chosen **Wizard** contributes colored mana on Wizard creature casts and makes those creature spells uncounterable only if its restricted mana actually paid that cost; it does not protect Jeleva's ETB/attack abilities nor cast spells from exile in the absence of colored mana. Losing City of Traitors removes two-generic burst and some tax capacity. Hexing Squelcher, counters and protection depend on paid timing; equipped Greaves' shroud conflicts with targeted Torch Courier haste.

**Fire Covenant and win choice:** Jeleva freecasting Fire Covenant waives only mana cost: its **X life additional cost** still must be paid, and total damage divided among legal targets; the selective instant can leave Jeleva alive but misses hexproof/indestructible and needs BR when hand-cast. Toxic Deluge would kill our own commander but can shrink untargetable creatures. The alternative stack-reactive Mindbreak Trap, opponent-yard Mnemonic Betrayal and Mind's Desire storm payoff remain because swaps to Flame of Anor, Spellseeker or Savage Beating sacrificed distinct high-velocity value. Breach/LED/Brain Freeze, Oracle/Pact/Consultation, Doomsday, Mind's Desire and Yawgmoth's Will all require actual zones, timing and opponent interaction. No observed multiplayer win-rate claim.

Engineering PR #699 has 14 focused proxy tests, 23 exact physical alternative configurations and the owner Jeleva-only exception.
