## Current 100 commander-first, mana, protection, win lines and negative cases

- **Commander first:** This 4/4 artifact Dalek triggers from being **declared an attacker**: the four choices are rolled separately for each trigger—Thay adds a counter to artifact creatures, Caan draws two, Sec creates one 3/3 menace artifact Dalek, Jast drains each opponent for four. Roaming Throne creates an extra trigger with its own roll; copying an already rolled trigger with Strionic Resonator or Scientist Supreme copies its chosen mode. No trigger requires combat damage, and tokens that enter already attacking do not get an attack-declaration trigger.

- **Mana, cohesion and interaction:** The 34-land/eight-plus-one-rock tempo supports a four-mana UBR artifact commander, but RR for Hammer and extra combat, UU for Reverse the Polarity/Mana Drain, metalcraft for Mox Opal and life/conditional Ancient-style speed must be paid accurately. Academy Ruins / Inventors' Fair enable artifact utility, while artifact creature wipes can both remove the board and disable Cult. Fierce Guardianship and Deflecting Swat are commander-dependent, and Greaves shroud can prevent one's own targeted equipment/trigger selection.

- **Copy/haste chain:** Retaining The Master, Multiplied is essential: Shaun's leave-battlefield triggered token exile and myriad delayed exile are preventable **only while The Master survives at resolution**; not state-based actions or opposing exile. Shaun and Karlach can create additional combats, but a newly created tapped-and-attacking Synth cannot legally be declared for Karlach's extra combat without haste (Hammer or other). Helm/Spark/Sakashima/Auton establish persistent Cult attackers only under each card's proper timing.

- **Finite finish and counterplay:** Sword of Feast and Famine + Aggravated Assault can make repeating combat loops only after combat damage actually connects, Sword untaps enough lands to pay {3}{R}{R}, red pips are present, and a sorcery-speed activation is available in a main phase. Jast is a one-in-four randomized result per fresh trigger, not assured lethal; other choices create cards/tokens/counters, with library and opponent interaction limits. A destroyed Sword/equipment recipient or missed combat connection stops the line.

## Full-source alternatives, actual owner-retained cut choices, stop reason

**Nine approved source changes:** Karlach ← Cybermen Squadron; Kindred Discovery ← Canoptek Spyder; Hammer of Purphoros ← Adaptive Omnitool; Shaun ← Thopter Spy Network; Quantum Misalignment ← Anrakyr the Traveller; Skorpekh Lord ← Baleful Strix; Reverse the Polarity ← Desynchronization; Otawara ← Island; Takenuma ← Swamp. **Retain** Etherium Sculptor and The Master against the Grok first-pass alternative.

Counterfactual exact-list branches were explicitly judged: restore Cybermen Squadron/Anrakyr for higher mana-value myriad/cheat but lose immediate reliable Karlach combat and permanent Cult duplication; restore Thopter Network/Spyder for slow Thopters and artifact draw but lose Shaun/Kindred's direct trigger scaling; replace Etherium Sculptor with Baleful Strix to regain defense/draw but reduce early Cult tempo; retain a basic over each channel land for greater fetch/nonbasic resilience but lose flexible bounce/recursion. No alternative beats the approved coherent attack plan sufficiently to propose an unapproved cut.

**Disposition:** Keep exact current 100 with its documented limits. No new user-unapproved cuts/adds; no altered project exceptions; no assertion of independent Oct28 full game simulation. M8 overall acceptance remains pending remaining decks, original P1 evidence and explicit owner release approval.
