## Current 100 commander-first, mana, protection, win lines and negative cases

- **Commander first:** Davros is a legendary **artifact Alien Scientist, not Dalek**. His beginning-of-end-step trigger **always triggers**, checks if any opponent lost ≥3 life **during resolution**, then creates one Dalek if true and offers a villainous choice only to each opponent who met 3. Merely having a Dalek or dealing two is not sufficient; zero qualifiers means no token/choice. The Valeyard doubles the *choice* not the token.

- **Engine and protected roles:** Boltwave, Maestros Charm, Flame Rift (4 self-damage), Fiery Confluence's repeated two-to-all modes, Loyal Subordinate and Court of Ambition are current-turn qualification; Tergrid/Waste Not/Tinybones convert genuine discards into value, and Sycorax plus Entropic Battlecruiser retain villainous-choice flavor. Seven rocks and 34 lands supply UBR but Metalcraft Mox Opal conditional, and no cloned Davros bypasses color/timing costs. Swan Song, Vandalblast, Toxic Deluge and Fierce/Swat support the engine, with limitations in response to end step.

- **Copies and choice ordering:** Spark Double, Irenicus's Vile Duplication, Quantum Misalignment and Auton make permanent nonlegendary Davros copies with end-step triggers. Myriad copies that exile at end of combat do not endure till end step. Purphoros's Dalek-entry damage triggers only **after** a Davros has qualified and made a Dalek: the ping cannot retroactively qualify players for that same Davros resolution, but can qualify later stacked Davros triggers. Mechanized Warfare makes Purphoros 2 become 3 each opponent, Bloodletter on our turn makes 2 life-loss damage 4; without either and with just a first ping of two, new players fail threshold.

- **Legality and friction:** Mechanized Warfare amplifies damage from red/artifact sources to opponents/permanents, not nongame life loss from Wound Reflection or our own Flame Rift damage. Fiery Confluence's 2/2/2 opponent modes do not target, but a targeted artifact mode can be countered by all-target illegality. Dauthi Voidwalker may exile cards Tergrid needed in grave; the opponent can choose an empty-hand discard with no effect. No deterministic infinite or simulated win rate is claimed.
