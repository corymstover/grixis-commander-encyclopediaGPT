## Commander-specific evaluation, mana/protection and adverse win lines

- **Commander first:** Azula has firebending 2 on attack, giving {R}{R} lasting only through combat. **While she is attacking**, every spell we **cast** is copied and its targets may change. The copy goes on stack rather than being cast, so it does not add another cast/storm trigger. Copied permanents become token permanents on resolution, not a new card cast and not an effect saying create. Casting normal sorceries in combat demands actual timing permission (Borne Upon a Wind, Quicken, Valley Floodcaller, High Fae Trickster, Emergence Zone); having firebending RR alone does not allow it.

- **Electrodominance bounded engine:** At X=2, {R}{R} firebending can pay both red pips but two additional generic are still due. If an attacking Azula copies Electrodominance, both copy and original can each deal X to legal target and permit a **new actual cast** of an eligible mana-value≤X card from hand during resolution without paying its cost. Azula can copy the newly cast spells only while still attacking. **Either Electro instance with no legal target on resolution fails to resolve**, granting no free cast; no forced infinite, and extra card costs/targets/X=0 for free X spell still apply. The conditional Turnabout/Narset's Reversal/bounce line needs live stack and adequate untap/mana, not a guaranteed unbounded play.

- **Mana, haste, protection and owner costs:** The 26 land-face cEDH manabase gives up often-rainbow Exotic Orchard for Sink's blue land back face, which enters tapped unless 3 life paid and increases Ad Nauseam MV as a front spell. Samut, Hazoret's Champion provides two-mana global haste to attack same turn; it replaces Vivi's repeatable counters/mana. The approved revision also removes previously owner-kept split-second Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun and Redirect Lightning: genuine lost response-denial and retarget protection. Retained Swan Song, Gemstone Caverns, Hexing Squelcher, Cavern, Force/Fluster and other counters mitigate, not replicate, split second. Mox Opal requires metalcraft, and no combat attack means no copy.

- **Win and failure audit:** Breach + LED/Petal/Brain Freeze, Oracle with Consultation/Pact, Beseech Bargain copy and Big Score double resolution are separate conditional combos with actual graveyard, stack and spell availability. Copy of Big Score inherits its paid discard cost status but does not require a *second* discard: resolving original and copy draws total four, makes four Treasures if both resolve. Flashback grants paid alternate casting later from graveyard and then exile, not free recursion. Priority, opponents, mana and haste constrain the lines; prior finite cEDH castability estimates are not observed wins.

## Whole-100 counterfactual challenge / why stop here

**Exact approved swaps:** Turnabout ← Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun; Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs ← Exotic Orchard; Samut, Hazoret's Champion ← Vivi Ornitier; Flashback ← Repeal; Electrodominance ← Redirect Lightning. These five are the exact owner-approved Oct24 Rev2—even though two older owner preferences were re-challenged.

Five full-100 alternatives considered: Grok #667 loses Swan Song/Gemstone Caverns (rejected); keeping split-second Samut trades Narset-Reversal/Turnabout coherence and copy-on-resolution sequencing; Unexpected Windfall over Vivi leaves higher {2}{R}{R} costs outside firebending; Mistrise Village over a fetch loses three-color early tempo in a 26-land shell; protect Redirect Lightning instead of Electro loses high-impact two distinct free-cast instances. Selected current five improve attacked-window spell density and conserve premium interactive resources with real disclosed costs.

**M8 scope disposition:** Keep exact October24 approved 100, not a new swap proposal. Historic I/J cannot be silently rebranded as current independently passed; overall M8 release requires remaining decks and genuine P1 dispositions/owner acceptance.
