## Current 100 commander-first, mana, protection, win lines and negative cases

- **Commander first:** Whenever we discard **one or more land cards**, Doom drains **2 life per opponent per discard event**, not per discarded land in a wheel. Beginning of combat, Doom targets a **Villain we control**, gives it menace and makes it connive; with no legal Villain target, there is no connive. Doom or another eligible Villain may be the target; Chameleon clone name/legend restrictions are source-specific. This deck retains land discard as its central engine, not generic wheels for wheels' sake.

- **Discern distinct card-versus-event payoffs:** Glint-Horn Buccaneer pings per discarded *card*, unlike Doom's event count; Seismic Assault (free discard per activation) and Molten Vortex ({R} each) make repeated events only with cards and mana. Lightning Axe's discarded land as an additional cost is a live event if Doom is already on battlefield; a prior turn Mox Diamond land discard to accelerate into Doom cannot retroactively trigger him. Multiple lands discarded simultaneously by Windfall/Change of Fortune cause one Doom drain. Red Ghost's second-draw turn-local ability depends on actually drawing again.

- **Mana and land recursion:** Thirty-eight lands include tapped Coastal Peak/Tolaria West/Dakmor Salvage, real fetch/shock color sources, plus seven rocks. Mox Diamond must discard a land to enter; The Soul Stone/Villainous Hideout may have restricted mana not universally spendable on Vortex, Assault and non-Villain abilities. Tolaria transmute {1}{U}{U} as sorcery can discard Tolaria (one Doom trigger) to search a mana-value-zero land or Mox Diamond, not a nonzero-Vortex. Villainous Hideout's restrictions are respected. Swiftfoot Boots hexproof protects Doom and permits owner targeting, whereas Greaves shroud would stop the connive targeting.

- **Loop accounting and failure modes:** Dakmor dredge 2 + Tectonic Reformation cycling + Surly Badgersaur Treasure can recycle a cycling land with available R and sufficient library; dredge/replacement and graveyard hate impose real finite limits, no guaranteed infinite, and no separate infinite-mana claim. Trade Routes repeatedly discards lands only while B/U activation/resource/return mana and actual lands exist. Withering Torment retains targeted enchantment removal at instant speed; original Feed the Swarm/Big Score changes are real interaction/mana-speed tradeoffs.

## Full-source alternatives, actual owner-retained cut choices, stop reason

**Approved source changes:** Lightning Axe ← Big Score; Glint-Horn Buccaneer ← Green Goblin, Revenant; Red Ghost ← Loki; Coastal Peak ← Shipwreck Marsh; Swiftfoot Boots ← Unexpected Windfall; Mox Diamond ← Fellwar Stone; Withering Torment ← Feed the Swarm; Molten Vortex ← Kang; Tolaria West ← Geier Reach Sanitarium. Change of Fortune, Crucible, Dakmor, Reformation and Badgersaur remain; no phantom old six-swap J.

Whole-100 challenges include (1) restore Fellwar Stone over Mox for no land-cost reliable post-Doom mana but lose zero-mana accel and commander-live land discard; (2) Bitter Triumph over Lightning Axe for bigger kill but lose the one-mana land discard tempo; (3) Skirge Familiar over Change of Fortune adds repeated discards and {B} but destroys a high-lift refill and raises curve; (4) keep Shipwreck instead of Coastal Peak for untapped UB with two other lands but sacrifice a cyclable land. The exact nine owner-approved cards offer superior land-discard focus with known tapped/mana costs.

**Disposition:** Keep exact current 100 with its documented limits. No new user-unapproved cuts/adds; no altered project exceptions; no assertion of independent Oct28 full game simulation. M8 overall acceptance remains pending remaining decks, original P1 evidence and explicit owner release approval.
