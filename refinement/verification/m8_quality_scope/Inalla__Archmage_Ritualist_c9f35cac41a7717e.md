# M8 exact-current 99-library quality audit — #23 Inalla, Archmage Ritualist

**October 28, 2026 — KEEP EXACT OWNER-APPROVED REV1 100.** The October24 five approved changes Flashback, Corpse Dance, Gifts Ungiven, Forbidden Orchard and Starting Town were source-SHA approved after an independent 6/6 Gifts-pile check. 100 singleton names, only 27 land sources, Bracket 5 cEDH, no project exception; a lean turbo shell, not a casual 35-land mana base.

**Commander-first mechanics:** Inalla's Eminence fires when another **nontoken Wizard** controlled by us enters and Inalla is in command zone/battlefield, optional payment {1} gives one hasty token copy, exiled at next end step. The copied Wizard is a token and does NOT generate an infinite chain of new Eminence triggers. The intervening if is also checked at resolution; no automatic copy if Inalla is in neither eligible zone. Tapping five untapped Wizards for seven life loss does **not** use a tap symbol and so can use summoning-sick untapped Wizards. One paid trigger per applicable nontoken entry; actual activated life-loss is finite by untapped five-Wizard groups.

**Original Spellseeker route carefully bounded:** Spellseeker tutoring <=2 MV instants/sorceries, the optional paid Inalla copy and real mana supply can unlock the established complex 1-card chain, but original M6 K4 explicitly called the deterministic line uncertain; no fictional uninterruptible deterministic win is inferred here. Gifts Ungiven's exact four [Spellseeker, Step Through, Reanimate, Unearth] offer six possible 2/2 splits: all give Spellseeker in hand OR Spellseeker in yard plus a hand reanimation spell; that proves **access**, not actual win or payment. All four must genuinely remain in library when resolving Gifts.

**Mana and protection:** City of Traitors and Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault, Grim, LED/Mox/Petal, rituals and 27 lands supply explosive starts but lack routine long-game colored resilience. Starting Town helps rainbow during turns 1–3 when its cost is paid and enters tapped after; Forbidden Orchard grants enemy creatures when tapped for colors, potentially a blocker. Existing Force/Pact/Swan/Fluster/REB, Chain and death/yard spells are conditional answers, not automatic protection. Turn-one commander cast is unnecessary because Eminence works from command zone.

**Combo and opportunity-cost audit:** Bloodline Necromancer+Ruthless Technomancer and Scholar of the Ages buyback Corpse Dance or Saw in Half routes need actual Wizard entry, mana, grave order, death/reanimation steps and legal targets. Scholar clones retain printed mana value 7 after Saw in Half while P/T are halved. Flashback lets a chosen spell from graveyard be cast by paying normal costs and exiles it afterward; loses Kefka Wizard value. Corpse Dance over Final Fortune sacrifices an emergency extra turn; Gifts over Wheel loses fast seven-card draw and Breach refuel. This whole-100 selection beats explored Buried Alive/Daze/Pyroblast/Snapback/extra land filler by the more coherent Spellseeker source access, with real speed costs.

**Historical M6 design case boundary:** 5 P0 and 9 P1 original cases below have exact-current positive/adverse premises. Inalla M6 K4, F2 and F3 must not be promoted to confirmed instant-speed wins: stack, zone changes and split-second rules are real. Research PR #698 passed bounded 6/6 pile/source tests, not historical J replay, full stack certification or real measured competitive win rate.
