## Commander-specific evaluation, mana/protection and adverse win lines

- **Commander first:** Edea is a five-mana 2/5 ward {2}. At beginning of combat on our turn, the targeted creature of an opponent is borrowed, untapped and hastened through end of turn. If a creature we control but don't own dies, Edea returns that object to **its owner** and we draw a card. The target must exist and be legal; borrowing alone is temporary. Extra combat phases each have their own beginning-of-combat Edea theft. The normal borrowed death/return is a **draw plus return to its owner**, never a free permanent steal by itself.

- **Sacrifice coherence and kept slots:** Source retains owner-kept repeatable outlets Evereth, High Market, Ayara, Viscera Seer, Warren Soultrader, Altars, Phyrexian Tower, Goblin Bombardment and Illuminor Szeras. If a borrowed creature dies, owner-return and draw happen on the same trigger; alternate death-trigger order for Kaya's Ghostform or Chaos Shrine's Black Crystal can return/exile the actual same object first so that Edea's later trigger still draws but cannot relocate an absent graveyard card. If a replacement prevents death there is no Edea death trigger. Thassa blinking a temporarily stolen creature returns it under our control separately with normal ETB effects.

- **Mana and protection:** 35 lands include **Hall of Echoes** as a colorless utility land, losing Shipwreck Marsh U/B fixing; Hall's activation costs {5} and temporarily makes a controlled creature copy, with legend suppression, so no free persistent clone. Six rocks and 3× basic each help colored UBR but cannot erase the two-color-source loss. Ward {2}, Fierce/Swat, Mana Drain, Boots and other counters protect conditional attack/borrow windows. Hall becomes targetable as a creature during its effect; High Market remains instant-speed sacrifice but taps colorless.

- **Finishing:** Aggravated Assault demands real {3}{R}{R} and a main-phase sorcery-speed activation; Combat Celebrant must attack and exert, untapping only other creatures, and it normally skips its next untap. Multiple steals plus Mayhem Devil/Disciple/Pitiless, strong altars, Grave Pact and retained mass theft Mob Rule/Insurrection create real aristocrat/alpha lines. Mass Mutiny was removed by the approved revision; do not claim it remains. Creature-light opponents, instant removal and enemy ETB replay are major failure modes.

## Whole-100 counterfactual challenge / why stop here

**Exact approved swaps:** Aggravated Assault ← Mass Mutiny; Kaya's Ghostform ← Zara, Renegade Recruiter; Hall of Echoes ← Shipwreck Marsh; Geyadrone Dihada ← Village Rites; Combat Celebrant ← Seize the Spotlight. Crucially retained Evereth, High Market and Ayara, unlike earlier Grok first-pass candidate.

Four whole-100 challenged alternatives: restore Mass Mutiny for immediate three-opponent theft but lose repeated Edea combats; restore Village Rites for one-B sacrifice/draw but lose independent planeswalker permanent steal; restore Shipwreck Marsh for stronger UB mana at the cost of Hall's multi-trigger ceiling; re-add Zara over Ghostform for hand theft but sacrifice permanent acquisition on Edea-controlled bodies. The chosen five preserve the highest-leverage repeatable sacrifice infrastructure; no new owner-unapproved changes.

**M8 scope disposition:** Keep exact October23 approved 100, not a new swap proposal. Historic I/J cannot be silently rebranded as current independently passed; overall M8 release requires remaining decks and genuine P1 dispositions/owner acceptance.
