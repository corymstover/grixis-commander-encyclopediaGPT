## Commander-specific evaluation, mana/protection and adverse win lines

- **Commander first:** Garza Zol is a seven-mana flying haste 5/5. Any creature Garza deals damage to **this turn** that later dies gives Garza a +1/+1 counter, including Garza-sourced instant/mass damage (Giantfall, Chandra's Ignition, Nova Flame); damage to player from Garza in **combat** optionally draws a card. Deathtouch from Basilisk Collar/Quietus Spike makes a real point of damage lethal but not if prevented or redirected. If Garza kills creatures by deathtouch later in the turn, her own triggered +1 counters grow; not every global board death counts absent Garza damage.

- **Voltron math:** With unmodified Garza base 5 power, Two-Handed Axe attack doubles power to 10 until end of turn. Lizard Blades double strike gives **two separate 10-damage combat steps = 20** to one unblocked opponent, *one short of lethal 21 commander combat damage*. A subsequent valid combat with another Axe attack trigger can increase that turn's power further for lethal. Two-Handed Axe // Sweeping Cleave is **one physical card** with Adventure spell and equipment front; don't count two slots. Grafted Exoskeleton adds +2/+2 and infect: 7 power before Axe, 14 with Axe; connecting once deals 14 poison. Kediss can deal Garza-sourced noncombat infect damage to other opponents, but it does **not** count as commander combat damage to them.

- **Protection/tempo:** Thirty-seven conventional land sources plus tap-only Malakir Mire back face equal 38 possible land drops, not 38 untapped lands. Malakir Rebirth at {B} and two life can return a Garza that would die **only if she remains in graveyard instead of choosing command zone**; she returns tapped as a new object, loses counters and attached Equipment. Replacing untapped Takenuma channel recursion costs early black tempo. Conqueror's Flail equipped to a Garza that guarantees UBR can give +3/+3 and prevents opponents **casting spells** on our turn, not their activated abilities or spells cast before equip resolves; current counters are still important. Loss of Rapid Hybridization, Sheoldred's Edict and Robe/Scythe is a real reactive/draw cost.

- **Finite finish and weaknesses:** Extra combat via Seize the Day/flashback and Aggravated Assault are paid/targeted, not automatically infinite; Sword of Feast and Famine and Reaver Cleaver need actual hits and sufficient {3}{R}{R}. The Ozolith gets equivalent +1 counters when Garza leaves, but does not preserve equipment attachment or commander-tax status. Jeska, Thrice Reborn starts with 0 loyalty if Garza has *not* been cast from command zone (e.g. only from hand after Command Beacon); no unconditional threefold combat. A single seven-mana threat remains vulnerable to edicts, early tax, shroud/equipment timing and blockers.

## Whole-100 counterfactual challenge / why stop here

**Exact approved swaps:** Lizard Blades ← Scythe of the Wretched; Two-Handed Axe // Sweeping Cleave ← Inquisitor's Flail; Conqueror's Flail ← Robe of the Archmagi; Giantfall ← Rapid Hybridization; Seize the Day ← Sheoldred's Edict; Temur Battle Rage ← Psychotic Fury; Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire ← Takenuma, Abandoned Mire. Keep 11 rocks and 20 equipment; no owner-unapproved added staple.

Four complete current-100 alternatives challenged: Grok's Lizard-over-Fellwar makes seven-mana Garza less castable; retain Scythe over Lizard favors one-time theft/attachment move rather than commander double strike; keep Robe instead of Flail preserves large draw at equip {4} but sacrifices +3/+3 and cast-shielding; retain Takenuma/Fell the Profane or replace Orchard with blue-only MDFC degrades early colored tempo/cheap protection relative to Malakir. The chosen seven are real specialization changes, not arbitrary forced extra swaps.

**M8 scope disposition:** Keep exact October24 approved 100, not a new swap proposal. Historic I/J cannot be silently rebranded as current independently passed; overall M8 release requires remaining decks and genuine P1 dispositions/owner acceptance.
