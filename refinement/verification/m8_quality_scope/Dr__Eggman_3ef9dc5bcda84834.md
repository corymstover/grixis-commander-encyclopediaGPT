## Current 100 commander-first, mana, protection, win lines and negative cases

- **Commander first:** Five-mana flying Eggman **at beginning of our end step** first draws one card, then each opponent in turn order faces a villainous choice: discard one card or allow a **Construct, Robot or Vehicle card in our hand to be put** onto the battlefield. An opponent with empty hand can choose to discard nothing—so there is no guaranteed free payload, no forced discard and no Caress damage from that branch. Putting from hand is not casting; gearhulk/Vehicle ETB triggers work, **Cityscape Leveler cast triggers do not**, and normal cast restrictions do not become new cast events.

- **Eligible payloads and retention:** Source keeps 31 eligible names including six-drop Behemoth of Vault 0, Possession Engine, Skysovereign, Interceptor Mechan, Myr Battlesphere, Cityscape Leveler, Gearhulks and Synthesizer tools. The new Vehicles enter as noncreature artifacts until crewed; Eggman's power three can crew Skysovereign/Possession Engine's Crew 3 if available to tap. The Master Transmuter/Forge/Recombiner plus Unwinding Clock create another recurring *put/activated ability* path on opposing untap steps, not extra Eggman end steps.

- **Mana and interaction:** 32 lands vs prior 31 improve five-mana UBR castability but still expose bad early colored starts; Gloomlake Verge is always U, supplies B only with Island/Swamp. Seven rocks include conditional Mox Opal; Sol Ring doesn't pay UBR and one multicolor source cannot pay all three pips by itself. The prior Grok 160k draw-access proxy is not a full legal mana-sequencing or win-rate model; do not advertise 40.9%/50.6% as a playtest success probability. Keep Foundry Inspector and Unwinding Clock; losing Cryptothrall reduces protection versus Padeem overlap, losing Withering Torment loses a unique enchantment answer. Real Chaos Warp, Rift, Toxic and Their Name Is Death are narrower backup.

- **Villainous choice payoffs and constraints:** The Valeyard makes each opponent face two independent choices, but it cannot force them to deploy when discard is cheaper; Y'shtola Rhul permits an additional end step only under its own real trigger/phase conditions. Waste Not, Liliana's Caress and Geth's Grimoire react to actual card discards, not to an empty-hand choice. A 31-object eligible payload count does not mean 31 payloads in hand, and an artifact wipe/removal before end step interrupts the engine.

## Full-source alternatives, actual owner-retained cut choices, stop reason

**Approved source changes:** Possession Engine ← Blasphemous Act; Skysovereign ← Cybermen Squadron; Interceptor Mechan ← Cryptothrall; Behemoth of Vault 0 ← Weapons Manufacturing; Gloomlake Verge ← Withering Torment. Foundry Inspector and Unwinding Clock retained; owner separately approved **this exact Oct23 Rev2 hash** notwithstanding the earlier Oct14 retention preference for Blasphemous Act.

Full-100 genuine alternatives: return Blasphemous Act over Possession Engine for a cheap wipe but lose direct choice-eligible control payload; restore Cryptothrall instead of Interceptor to gain artifact-creature protection but lose eligible aerial recursion; restore Weapons Manufacturing over Behemoth for repeated artifact fodder but lose a high-impact direct put payload; replace Gloomlake Verge with Torment for full enchantment answer but regress land count to 31. The approved five picks favor reliable named Eggman output and early land access; not a guarantee that every multiplayer hand performs.

**Disposition:** Keep exact current 100 with its documented limits. No new user-unapproved cuts/adds; no altered project exceptions; no assertion of independent Oct28 full game simulation.
