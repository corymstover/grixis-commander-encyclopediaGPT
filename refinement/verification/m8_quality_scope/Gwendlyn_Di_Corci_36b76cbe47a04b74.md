# M8 exact-current 99-library quality audit — #22 Gwendlyn Di Corci

**October 28, 2026 — KEEP EXACT APPROVED REV1 100.** Six approved adjustments (Hymn/Rings/Sting/Misty/Marsh/Shivan) retain 35 lands, 2 Island, 1 Mountain, 5 Swamp, no project exception and Power Bracket 4 Optimized. Engineering PR #697 original 9 focused checks plus two complete counterfactuals support it.

**Commander first:** Gwendlyn taps for *one random discard* targeting a player, only during our own turn. Summoning sickness prevents tap immediately unless true haste/Thousand-Year exception; opponent empty hand can make ability impotent. Rings of Brighthearth (pay 2) and Illusionist's Bracers independently copy an activation: original + Bracers copy + paid Rings copy = **three**, not four, and targets can differ. Sting's start-of-combat untap can give a second own-turn activation; it does not permit opponent-turn Gwendlyn activations. Pemmin's Aura has own target/untap rules; shroud on commander blocks *other* target spells and equipping more gear.

**Mana and defense:** UBBR colored commander needs 35 physical lands/dual/fetch, 2-cast 2-equip Sting and multiple extra untap payments; two fetches trade painless tapped tri/check lands for pay life and possible shocks, Shivan trades a basic Mountain for UB? Shivan provides **U/R** at life cost and never B. Fast mana Chrome/Mox/Petal/LED and two Talismans are not all durable rocks. Force suite, Swan and removal help Gwendlyn survive but shroud from Greaves conflicts with attached targeted untappers.

**Counterplay and finish:** Waste Not, Grimoire, Liliana Caress, Tinybones and Tergrid need actual discards; Caress is per card, Tinybones varies by card wording and trigger window. Chains of Mephistopheles can punish our OWN wheel draw attempts, especially with empty hand. Underworld Breach+LED+Wheel is a distinct combo with graveyard/mana limits; Bloodchief Ascension **does not** automatically loop with absent Mindcrank. Bottomless Pit and Oppression hurt our casting/discard too; once all opponents empty their hands the commander’s repeat ability can be blank. Retaining Mind Twist, Geth, Bracers/Pemmin/Seal and wheels is strategic identity.

**Tradeoff stop:** The authorized six improve targeted Gwendlyn taps and untapped colors but lose Dauthi grave hate/stolen free spell, Underworld Dreams wheel punishment, Raven Man flyers and no-life fixed tri-land. The full-100 Freed from the Real vs Sting and five-swap no-Shivan controls are legitimate but weaker for this specific haste-and-tap plan. Fourteen original M6 design groups below have current positive/adverse witnesses; no prior I/J is relabeled.
