## Current 100 commander-first, mana, protection, win lines and negative cases

- **Commander first:** Don's continuous +2/+2, menace, deathtouch and Pirate type apply to creatures we **control but do not own**. Cards we create as token copies are ordinarily owned by us and not buffed. Don's separate trigger creates **two tapped Treasures** each time we actually **cast** a noncreature spell **we do not own**; stealing a creature onto battlefield, copying a spell on stack, or casting our own exile spell does not satisfy that trigger. Tap state means those two Treasures do **not** pay for the triggering cast.

- **Theft and secondary win engines:** Fallen Shinobi ninjutsu must return an unblocked attacker and its exiled spells are cast without cost; exiled lands cannot simply be played during combat damage resolution if normal land-play timing is unavailable. Gríma casts actual opponent-owned instants/sorceries from exile on combat damage; Treachery steals a permanent creature only if the Aura successfully enters and untaps up to five lands upon entrance. Mind's Dilation fires on each opponent's first spell *each turn* and can freely cast one revealed nonland; such casts trigger Don when opponent-owned and noncreature. Passionate Archaeologist can be played as normal Background in library 99 and grants Don exile-cast damage to target opponent while Don remains.

- **Mana and defense:** Thirty-five lands/nine rocks with two untapped Verge lands deliberately balance early colors against eight-mana RRR Insurrection finish. Gloomlake Verge unconditional U but conditional B by controlling Island/Swamp; Blazemire Verge unconditional B but conditional R by Swamp/Mountain. The cut Shipwreck Marsh actually untapped with any two OTHER lands, not only basics, and is a serious alternative for black by turn three; cut Smoldering Marsh requires two basics. Life/tapped/fetch restrictions and real interaction (Fierce, Swat, Toxic, Rift) matter. Loss of Blasphemous Act sacrifices cheap wipe; losing Face-Breaker delays independent Treasures.

- **Actual win lines:** Opponent-owned stolen creatures are buffed Pirates and enable Admiral Beckett Brass/Kindred/Coercive Recruiter; Insurrection at eight mana with RRR steals a board temporarily and can swing only with legal attackers and sufficient damage. Roaming Throne Pirate doubles triggered Treasures (four **tapped**) but not Don's continuous stolen-creature buff. Thieving Amalgam manifests opponent-owned **nontoken cards** and benefits from Don, whereas Dauthi Voidwalker replacement exile prevents some grave/death-trigger payoffs. Opponent departure removes their cards; theft depends on actual opposing pool.

## Full-source alternatives, actual owner-retained cut choices, stop reason

**Approved source changes:** Gríma ← Professional Face-Breaker; Fallen Shinobi ← Seize the Spotlight; Treachery ← Blasphemous Act; Gloomlake Verge ← Shipwreck Marsh; Blazemire Verge ← Smoldering Marsh; Mind's Dilation ← Stolen Strategy; Passionate Archaeologist ← Breeches, Eager Pillager. Four complete-list tradeoffs: keep Shipwreck for guaranteed turn-3 UB (but early tapped vs conditional Verge); keep Act for one-red sweeper (but lose permanent theft and potential land untap); Mass Mutiny over Mind's Dilation gets cheaper immediate one-shot theft (but loses free repeated opponent-first-spell casts); restore Face-Breaker over Gríma for self-impulse/early Treasures (but reduce actual casts of opponent spells and Background damage). No new unapproved cuts warranted.
