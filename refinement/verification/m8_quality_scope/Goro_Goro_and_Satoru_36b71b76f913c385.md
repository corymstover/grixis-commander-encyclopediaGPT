# M8 exact-current 99-library quality audit — #21 Goro-Goro and Satoru

**October 28, 2026 — KEEP EXACT APPROVED REV1 100.** Cory approved the exact October24 five swaps, no substitutions now. Bracket 4 Optimized, 33 lands, Island ×2 only repeated, no project exceptions. Engineering PR #696 nine focused checks and five-swap alternate research are original-source support, not live-game win percentages.

**Commander first:** Per damaged **player per combat-damage step**, whenever one or more of our creatures that entered this turn connects, one 5/5 Dragon is created. A Human-chosen Roaming Throne adds one copy of that triggered ability (two Dragons per qualifying player/step), not one per fresh attacking token. Newly created Dragons after combat damage are not automatically attacking and require haste to attack in a subsequent combat. Goro's 1R haste activated effect only affects creatures already controlled as it resolves; Samut and Dragon Tempest can solve the gap.

**Resource and disruption:** 33 lands, three Talismans, Fellwar, Sol Ring, Signet, Ancient Tomb and actual UBR dual/fetch base pay 1UBR commander plus 1R for team haste; fetches Misty/Marsh can shock or fetch tapped triome. 1/1 new Hanweir attackers are blockable unless genuine evasion (Tetsuko, flying, etc.) applies. Sweepers, fog, stax and damaged-player blockers deny Dragons; counter/removal and Goro tax constrain restart.

**Finite win pathways:** Breath of Fury is not a free extra combat: the enchanted creature must deal combat damage to a player, be sacrificed, the Aura must successfully move to an appropriate creature, and the new attacker must be able to attack in the granted combat. Dragons created by the damage step may become Aura hosts but cannot attack then without haste. Loyal Apprentice/Urabrask's Forge/Daring Piracy/Determined Iteration and Hanweir generate fresh attackers conditionally; Terror/Tempest burn depends on actual Dragons. Aggravated Assault 3RR, Karlach and Great Train Heist are independent finite paths.

**Whole-99 decision:** Keep Bident combat card access, Ninja draw/ninjutsu/Yuriko, Breath and every extra-combat engine. Owner's selected Bitterblossom, Human Throne and Hanweir outperform tested Chandra or Kaito alternatives on repeated combat output but cost life, require actual attacks and lose Kaito card selection/Grim Hireling Treasurer; fifth/sixth filler not justified. Historic M6 blueprint 5 P0+9 P1 below reconciled as bounded current-list cases, not replayed I/J or full stack simulation.
