## Commander identity and current 99 physical card-role ledger

Original Nicol Bolas costs `{2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}`, requires a **real `{U}{B}{R}` upkeep payment** to stay, flies and makes *each opponent damaged by Nicol Bolas itself* discard their whole hand. Unlike Ravager's transform/Superfriends deck #42, this #41 must actually create and protect Bolas-sourced damage events, convert discarded cards to cards/mana/pressure, or attack for commander damage. Equipment-granted tap abilities and Chandra's Ignition can use Bolas as damage source; generic Sword damage is **not** Bolas damage. The 99 current physical library copies are assigned to one primary role below, without hiding duplicates or substituting past-list cards.

Dragon Tempest and Ingris require real entered Dragon/source predicates. Seven-power commander can be stopped by a blocker, fog or removal before damage.

## Post-approval opportunity challenge: no automatic acceptance of ten swaps

The ten owner-approved add/cut decisions are re-examined for role loss: Sarkhan's separate-named copy versus Hypnotic Specter; Rivaz's Dragon-restricted paid U/B/R versus Familiar's generic discount; Seal over symmetrical Oppression; Ophidian Eye over Raiders' Wake; Coat over No Mercy; Reanimate over the five-mana Eldest Reborn saga; Coalition Relic over Seething Song burst; Minamo over Shizo fear; Plaza over graveyard-answer Bog; Dream Halls over Viridian Longbow's narrower ping. Every cut has a real cost. The current simultaneous 100 nevertheless gives more direct commander-sourced damage, draw, protection and colored casting/upkeep access without forcing generic Breach/Oracle or superfriends crossover.

| Complete legal 100 based on current exact approved source | Best adversarial argument | Why it loses here |
|---|---|---|
| **One-card alternate:** Minamo → Shizo, Death's Storehouse (revert one prior change) | Recovers black source/fear against nonblack, nonartifact defenders for reliable 7-power connection | Loses blue utility that untaps Bolas for another real Dagger damage/discard/draw event and Rivaz for mana, and weakens UUBBRR colored flexibility. Shizo is a legitimate metagame alternative, not an unconditional upgrade. |
| **Two-card alternate:** Plaza of Heroes → Bojuka Bog and Mithril Coat → No Mercy (both partial reverts) | Regains graveyard hate and retaliation, the strongest defensive critique of the current ten-swap package | Bog always enters tapped and cuts legendary UBR source/protection; No Mercy only reacts **after** a creature deals damage and loses immediate indestructible protection against commander removal. Weakens primary expensive Dragon survival more than it solves an unproven graveyard matchup. |
| **Two-card alternate:** Dream Halls → Seething Song and Minamo → Shizo, Death's Storehouse (partial reverts) | Opponents cannot exploit symmetric Dream Halls, and the Dragon regains fear with a one-shot faster cast path | One-shot Song fails to pay the recurring colored upkeep, removes the color-matching alternative cast, and Minamo's repeat damage engine disappears. Strongest actual risk is an adversarial counterspell pod exploiting Dream Halls; sequencing/source protection mitigates, does not eliminate it. |
| **One-card alternate:** Ophidian Eye → Raiders' Wake (revert) | More discard-drain punishment after a big hit; cheaper board presence | Removes flash/card draw on every Bolas damage event, weakens actual ability-to-continue-drawing rather than just winning harder with empty opposing hands. |

**Opportunity stop:** All current 99 physical copies explicitly assigned, best additional cuts and two separate *multi-card* 100-card packages compared in casting, colored upkeep, survival, noncombat trigger reliability, response windows and commander identity. The risk of symmetric Dream Halls and missing generic graveyard-hate is openly acknowledged, not resolved with invented games. The deciding argument is that there is no presently supported card that improves this deck's repeatable **Nicol-sourced hand destruction** more than the incumbent role it displaces. That is an authored local-best judgment with known uncertainties, not a universal mathematical optimum, independent M6 J replay or full M8 certification. **No new add/cut recommended.**
