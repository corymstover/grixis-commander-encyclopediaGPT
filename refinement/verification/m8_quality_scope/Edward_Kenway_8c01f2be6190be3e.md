## Commander-specific evaluation, mana/protection and adverse win lines

- **Commander first:** At beginning of our end step, Edward counts **tapped permanents** that are Assassin, Pirate and/or Vehicle **once per permanent**, not once per subtype. One Treasure for each still controlled and tapped at the trigger's relevant resolution; a creature that crewed a Vehicle is tapped but ordinarily no longer attacking; a Vehicle that attacks must have been crewed/otherwise animated. A Vehicle dealing combat damage to a player independently exiles the top card of that player's library face down and we can play it as long as it stays exiled, subject to normal land-play timing and colored mana/permission.

- **RMS Titanic original-test correction:** RMS Titanic's official Oracle has its combat-damage trigger **sacrifice Titanic and make Treasure**. Once it has been sacrificed during combat, Titanic is *not* a tapped Vehicle still controlled at our end step. The old M6 K5 blanket claim that Edward still counts Titanic after its sacrifice is **false for that game state**; only if it actually remains under our control tapped at the end step can it be counted. Its generated Treasure may fuel Time Sieve regardless, but is not an Edward end-step qualifier. This is an original-case correction, not a reason to change the approved card.

- **Mana, crew and protection:** Thirty-five lands and seven durable rocks preserve early colored UBR for Edward, unlike Grok's suggested cut Fellwar; Lotus Petal was removed, genuinely losing burst. Captain Lannery Storm remains for attacking Treasure and early deployment. Mu Yanling gives Vehicles flying, Brotherhood Vertibird flying naturally and Mirkwood Bats direct token-created/sacrificed drain; the loss of Coastal Piracy's any-creature combat draw and Vaan's thief engine is real. Fierce/Force/Swat counter interaction and creature/Vehicle wipes can still disrupt engine.

- **Finite combo and opponent interaction:** Time Sieve requires sacrificing five *actual artifacts* each extra turn; Academy Manufactor on each Treasure event can multiply Food/Clue/Treasure but does not make the sacrifice free or guarantee five tapped qualifiers on subsequent turns. Fateful Discovery's artifact-enter draw is **mandatory** and can deck us out in repeated Manufactor lines. Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator plus attacking Glint-Horn needs an actual discard activation, live Pirates, two or more opponents and finite deck resources to feed Treasure/red payments; do not imply a universal turn-one infinite.

## Whole-100 counterfactual challenge / why stop here

**Exact approved swaps:** Mu Yanling ← Breeches, Brazen Plunderer; Brotherhood Vertibird ← Imposter Mech; Mirkwood Bats ← Coastal Piracy; Spectral Sailor ← Lotus Petal; Malcolm, the Eyes ← Vaan, Street Thief. Keep Fellwar Stone, Captain Lannery Storm, Fateful Discovery, Manufactor and Time Sieve.

Full 100 alternatives considered: keep Coastal Piracy over Bats for card acquisition but lose direct Treasure/Time Sieve drain; keep Imposter Mech for cheaper best-creature clone but lose permanent evasive artifact-count Vehicle; keep Vaan over Malcolm for opponent theft but lose early hasty Pirate+Clues; keep Lotus Petal for one-shot speed over repeatable flying Sailor selection. Cosima/The Omenkeel's land-only theft and other EDHREC lift candidates didn't beat synergy-specific current roles without cutting owner-protected cards.

**M8 scope disposition:** Keep exact October23 approved 100, not a new swap proposal. Historic I/J cannot be silently rebranded as current independently passed; overall M8 release requires remaining decks and genuine P1 dispositions/owner acceptance.
