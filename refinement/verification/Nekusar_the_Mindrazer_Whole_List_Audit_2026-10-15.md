## Audit closure

- All 99 baseline noncommander copies were explicitly challenged.
- The historical `Krang, the All-Powerful → Fate Unraveler` proposal was not inherited. Fate Unraveler remains a strong independent draw punisher; the whole-list audit identifies Solphim as the superior cut because Solphim's damage replacement suppresses Ghyrson's exact-1 trigger.
- Current Nekusar-specific evidence supports Wizard's Staff, Winds of Change, and Krang as meaningful challengers, but candidate popularity/ranking was not treated as an automatic inclusion rule.
- Slow symmetrical draw pieces were compared against burst wheels; generic damage multiplication was compared against trigger multiplication; mana-base lands were compared against fetch/dual/five-color alternatives.
- Deliberately rejected packages include Narset/Notion Thief wheel locks, infect shortcuts, generic Thoracle consultation, and Bloodchief/Mindcrank mill conversion because they weaken the deck's commander-first draw-punishment identity.
- Final serious strategic pass after the 17-card delta: **ZERO NEW MATERIAL IMPROVEMENTS**.
