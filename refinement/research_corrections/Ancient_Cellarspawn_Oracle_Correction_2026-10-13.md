# Active Research Correction — Ancient Cellarspawn Oracle Text

**Recorded:** 2026-10-13  
**Scope:** Candidate research metadata correction; frozen baseline sources remain immutable.

The inherited candidate-ranking row for Ancient Cellarspawn incorrectly says its cast-for-less trigger makes **each opponent** lose life equal to the difference. Current Oracle wording is:

> Whenever you cast a spell, if the amount of mana spent to cast it was less than its mana value, target opponent loses life equal to the difference.

It targets one opponent per trigger, not each opponent. The Abaddon restart review and card matrix use this corrected wording. The trigger resolves before the spell that caused it, and mana value is unchanged by cost reductions or alternative costs.

- [MTG Salvation Oracle text](https://www.mtgsalvation.com/cards/duskmourn-house-of-horror-commander/63660-ancient-cellarspawn)
