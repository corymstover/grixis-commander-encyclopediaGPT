## Findings supported by current sources

- **Underworld Breach:** Escape lets its controller cast a card from the graveyard by paying its mana cost and exiling three other cards from that graveyard for each escape cast.
- **Lion's Eye Diamond:** Its activation sacrifices LED and discards the controller's hand as costs, then adds three mana of one chosen color. Do not assume cards discarded by this activation can be retained in hand for later use; they are in the graveyard and may be exiled to pay later escape costs.
- **Brain Freeze:** It has storm, and its spell mills a target player for three cards. Storm counts other spells cast before it that turn. Copies generated by the storm trigger are not cast and do not themselves increase storm count.
- **Abaddon:** Only spells cast from hand qualify for Abaddon's granted cascade; Breach casts from graveyard do not gain cascade from Abaddon on that basis alone.
- **Trigger distinction:** The storm copies are copies, not casts. Storm-Kiln Artist's “cast or copy” trigger is a relevant exception; cast-only permanents in this list do not trigger from storm copies.
- **Player loses from draw:** An empty library is not itself a loss. A player loses when they are required to draw more cards than remain and the relevant state-based action is processed.

Current reference set: [Official Magic Comprehensive Rules (effective September 25, 2026)](https://magic.wizards.com/en/rules), especially Storm (702.40), Escape (702.138), copy rules (707.10), and loss from drawing (104.3c). Relevant card pages: [Underworld Breach](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/161/underworld-breach), [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/307/lions-eye-diamond), [Brain Freeze](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/29/brain-freeze), and [Abaddon the Despoiler](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/171/abaddon-the-despoiler).

## What this does and does not prove

The rules sequence constraints above are verified at the rules-structure level. However, the current repository did not provide a concrete starting inventory and an iteration-by-iteration resource ledger that demonstrates an actual repeatable sequence. The general EDHREC combo entry is a candidate-line reference rather than proof that every stated starting state can execute through every resource constraint.

**Disposition: PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** This is not being labeled an unconditional infinite, deterministic kill, or a fully proved combo line. For closure at a stronger level, provide a concrete starting zone inventory and track available mana, all escape payments, storm count, the allocation of each Brain Freeze resolution, and remaining escape fuel for each iteration. No new Forge AI comparison is warranted by this work.

No card changes are authorized by this audit.
