# Sol'kanar the Swamp King — Commander Blueprint

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
Deck: #52 of 62

## Commander identity

Current Oracle identity: **Swampwalk** and **Whenever a player casts a black spell, you gain 1 life.**

The deck's commander-first plan is therefore not generic Grixis control. It is a black-spell/lifegain engine that uses Sol'kanar and Demon's Horn as parallel triggers, Painter's Servant to make essentially every spell black, Swamp-conversion effects to support swampwalk and Swamp-matters cards, and lifegain conversion pieces to turn small repeated gains into table-wide life loss or deterministic loops.

## Primary engines

1. **Black-spell triggers:** Sol'kanar, Demon's Horn, Painter's Servant, Bontu's Monument, Jet Medallion, K'rrik.
2. **Lifegain conversion:** Vito, Starscape Cleric, Marauding Blight-Priest, Enduring Tenacity, Aetherflux Reservoir, Witch of the Moors.
3. **Life-loss-to-life-gain engines:** Exquisite Blood and Bloodthirsty Conqueror.
4. **Swamp infrastructure:** Blanket of Night, Urborg, Cabal Coffers, Crypt Ghast, Dread Presence, Consuming Corruption, typed Swamp duals.
5. **Painter package:** Painter's Servant naming black turns all spells black for Sol'kanar/Demon's Horn and remains paired with Grindstone as a compact secondary finish.
6. **Wheel pressure:** Wheel of Fortune / Windfall with Sheoldred and Underworld Dreams supply a secondary life-total pressure plan without replacing the commander engine.

## Natural power ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Sol'kanar supports efficient tutors, compact two-card life loops, Painter/Grindstone, strong mana, and free interaction, but the commander itself is a five-mana incremental trigger engine rather than a cEDH-grade card-advantage/tutor/combo commander. The optimized target is therefore high-power Bracket 4 rather than forcing a generic Bracket 5 Grixis shell.

## Current-cycle refinement priorities

- Preserve black-spell density and the Painter's Servant identity bridge.
- Preserve the redundant Exquisite Blood / Bloodthirsty Conqueror life-loop package.
- Improve removal only where the replacement is itself black and more efficient.
- Improve ramp where it can itself be a black spell.
- Upgrade weak non-Swamp mana slots toward Swamp-typed or black-spell/land-modal cards.
- Do not add redundant low-impact lifegain artifacts solely because EDHREC ranks them highly.
- Do not replace the deck with generic Thoracle/Breach or unrelated Grixis combo packages.
