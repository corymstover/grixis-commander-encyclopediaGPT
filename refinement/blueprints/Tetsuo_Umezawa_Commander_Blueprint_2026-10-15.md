## Commander identity

Tetsuo Umezawa is a U/B/R 3/3 Human Archer with current Oracle text: Tetsuo can't be the target of Aura spells; `{U}{B}{B}{R}, {T}: Destroy target tapped or blocking creature.`

The two target conditions are independent. A creature may be destroyed because it is tapped, or because it is currently blocking even if it is untapped. The deck should therefore maximize: (1) cheap repeatable ways to tap opposing creatures, (2) forced-combat/goad effects that naturally tap attackers, (3) untap effects that let Tetsuo activate repeatedly, and (4) activated-ability copying.

## Primary game plan

1. Develop colored mana and deploy Tetsuo.
2. Manufacture legal targets through goad, forced attacks, tap effects, and opponents' normal combat.
3. Reuse Tetsuo with Minamo, Magewright's Stone, Thousand-Year Elixir, Aphetto Alchemist, Fatestitcher, Puppeteer, Patriar's Seal, and Retreat to Coralhelm.
4. Multiply activations with Rings of Brighthearth and Illusionist's Bracers.
5. Use Verity Circle / Borrowing 100,000 Arrows / Firkraag / Karazikar / Grenzo and the goad package to turn board manipulation into card advantage and pressure.

## Current-cycle additions

- **Patriar's Seal** — colored rock plus `{1}, {T}` untap for Tetsuo; more commander-relevant than Nightscape Familiar. Mana Vault was rejected because Tetsuo's initial mana cost and activated ability contain no generic mana.
- **Puppeteer** — repeatable `{U}, {T}` tap/untap target creature; can either create a target or reset Tetsuo.
- **Flood** — one-mana enchantment with `{U}{U}: Tap target creature without flying`; repeatable target manufacturing without tapping itself.
- **Blustersquall** — one-mana single-creature tap or four-mana overload to tap every creature not controlled by the caster.
- **Retreat to Coralhelm** — each landfall either taps/untaps a creature or scries; fetchlands can create two triggers and can reset Tetsuo after an activation.
- **Force of Will** — free stack protection while the deck spends colored mana on Tetsuo activations.
- **Withering Torment** — instant-speed creature/enchantment interaction over sorcery-speed Feed the Swarm.
- Premium dual/fetch package plus **Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs** — improves U/B/R sequencing while keeping 36 functional land-capable slots.

## Core keeps

Preserve the existing untap/copy shell: Aphetto Alchemist, Fatestitcher, Magewright's Stone, Minamo, Thousand-Year Elixir, Rings of Brighthearth, Illusionist's Bracers.

Preserve the strongest forced-combat/tapped-creature package: Disrupt Decorum, Fumiko the Lowblood, Kardur, Firkraag, Karazikar, Red Death, Bloodthirsty Blade, Shiny Impetus, Sly Instigator, Geode Rager, Bothersome Quasit, Vengeful Ancestor, Frozen Aether, Propaganda, Verity Circle, Borrowing 100,000 Arrows.

## Natural power ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Tetsuo's activation is powerful but mana-intensive and board-state dependent. The deck should maximize activation frequency and target access rather than abandon the commander for a generic Grixis cEDH shell.
