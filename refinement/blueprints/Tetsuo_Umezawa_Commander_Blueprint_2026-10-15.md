## Commander identity

Tetsuo Umezawa is a U/B/R 3/3 Human Archer with current Oracle text: Tetsuo can't be the target of Aura spells; `{U}{B}{B}{R}, {T}: Destroy target tapped or blocking creature.`

The two target conditions are independent. A creature may be destroyed because it is tapped, or because it is currently blocking even if it is untapped. The deck should maximize cheap repeatable ways to tap opposing creatures, forced-combat/goad effects that naturally tap attackers, untap effects that let Tetsuo activate repeatedly, and activated-ability copying.

## Primary game plan

1. Develop colored mana and deploy Tetsuo.
2. Manufacture legal targets through goad, forced attacks, tap effects, and normal combat.
3. Reuse Tetsuo with Minamo, Magewright's Stone, Thousand-Year Elixir, Aphetto Alchemist, Fatestitcher, Puppeteer, Patriar's Seal, Retreat to Coralhelm, and Sting, the Glinting Dagger.
4. Multiply activations with Rings of Brighthearth and Illusionist's Bracers.
5. Convert the resulting tapped-creature board state into cards and pressure with Verity Circle, Borrowing 100,000 Arrows, Firkraag, Karazikar, Grenzo, and the goad package.

## Current-cycle additions

- **Sting, the Glinting Dagger** — equipped creature gets +1/+1 and haste, and Sting untaps it at the beginning of each combat. On Tetsuo this is a commander-specific reset that can recur during each player's combat step in a multiplayer round. It replaces generic Opposition Agent rather than a haste/protection boot.
- **Patriar's Seal** — colored rock plus `{1}, {T}` untap for Tetsuo. Mana Vault was rejected because Tetsuo's initial mana cost and activated ability contain no generic mana.
- **Puppeteer** — repeatable `{U}, {T}` tap/untap target creature; either creates a target or resets Tetsuo.
- **Flood** — repeatable `{U}{U}: Tap target creature without flying`; target manufacturing without tapping itself.
- **Blustersquall** — cheap single-creature tap or overload to tap the opposing board.
- **Retreat to Coralhelm** — each landfall taps/untaps a creature or scries; fetchlands can create two triggers and reset Tetsuo after activation.
- **Force of Will** — free stack protection while colored mana is reserved for Tetsuo activations.
- **Withering Torment** — instant-speed creature/enchantment interaction.
- Premium dual/fetch package plus **Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs** — improves U/B/R sequencing while preserving 36 functional land-capable slots.

## Natural power ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Tetsuo's activation is powerful but mana-intensive and board-state dependent. Optimize activation frequency and target access rather than replace the commander with a generic Grixis cEDH shell.
