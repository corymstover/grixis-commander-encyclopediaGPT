## Commander identity

Tetsuo is an Equipment-centered attack-trigger commander. Whenever he attacks while equipped, he chooses either direct damage equal to the greatest mana value among Equipment attached to him or permission to cast an instant/sorcery from hand with mana value no greater than that Equipment ceiling without paying its mana cost.

The optimized deck should therefore:

1. keep a meaningful density of high-mana-value Equipment that can be deployed/attached efficiently;
2. preserve cheap attach-cheating and Equipment tutoring;
3. turn Tetsuo attacks into high-impact free spells, especially spells that create further combats;
4. protect Tetsuo and give him haste/evasion so the attack engine reliably starts;
5. retain Voltron damage as a real secondary win condition rather than becoming generic Grixis spellslinger/combo.

Natural ceiling: **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized**.

Proposed tags: **Equipment / Free-Cast Spells / Extra Combats**.

## Core engines

- Excalibur, Sword of Eden / Kaldra Compleat / Argentum Armor / Embercleave / Genji Glove / Hexplate Wallbreaker provide high Equipment mana values for the commander trigger.
- Brass Squire / Hammer of Nazahn / Blacksmith's Talent / Dalakos reduce or bypass attachment friction.
- Ancestral Vision / Profane Tutor / Wheel of Fate / Inevitable Betrayal are particularly efficient Tetsuo free-cast targets because his resolving ability supplies the alternate permission needed to cast them without paying their nonexistent mana costs.
- Genji Glove / Hexplate Wallbreaker / Akki Battle Squad / Godo / Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine form the commander-native extra-combat package.
- Basilisk Collar materially upgrades Tetsuo's damage mode because Tetsuo himself deals the damage and therefore benefits from deathtouch/lifelink.

## Current-cycle correction

Relentless Assault and Seize the Day were historically treated as Tetsuo attack-trigger extra-combat payoffs. Current rules review shows that both only create their additional combat when resolving during a main phase. Tetsuo casts them during combat, so that central synergy is false. They are replaced by timing-compatible extra-combat spells.
