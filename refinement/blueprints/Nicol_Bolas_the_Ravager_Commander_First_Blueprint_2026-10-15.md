## Commander identity

Front face: four-mana flying Elder Dragon whose ETB makes each opponent discard a card.  
Transform ability: `{4}{U}{B}{R}` at sorcery speed exiles Ravager and returns it transformed.  
Back face: seven-loyalty Bolas planeswalker with +2 draw two, -3 ten damage to a creature/planeswalker, -4 reanimate a creature/planeswalker from a graveyard, and -12 exile all but the bottom card of a player's library.

The deck therefore wants to maximize four connected axes rather than become generic Grixis control:

1. **Cast / ETB discard:** exploit Ravager entering, including selective blink/reset lines.
2. **Transform acceleration:** make the seven-mana activation materially easier to reach.
3. **Planeswalker multiplication:** proliferate, extra loyalty activations, copied loyalty abilities, and planeswalker protection.
4. **Graveyard conversion:** turn the commander-created discard and normal interaction into targets for the Arisen's -4 and Bolas-flavored recursion.

## Natural ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Ravager supports fast mana, premium interaction, strong planeswalker engines, deterministic multi-card combo potential through The Chain Veil infrastructure, and oppressive advantage once transformed. It is not being converted into a commander-irrelevant Oracle/Consultation or Breach cEDH shell.

## Encyclopedia differentiation

Deck #41, original Nicol Bolas, is locked as **Discard / Commander Damage / Control** and is centered on Nicol himself dealing damage to empty whole hands.

Deck #42 must instead own the **Superfriends / Transform / Proliferate** identity:

- Ravager's ETB is incremental multiplayer discard, not the full-hand-destruction endpoint.
- The Arisen is the centerpiece: copying, proliferating, protecting, and repeatedly activating planeswalker abilities is the primary engine.
- Bolas planeswalkers remain flavor-appropriate because this is the encyclopedia slot in which they are mechanically central.
- Generic wheel locks, generic draw-punish pieces, and original Nicol Bolas are lower priority because they blur adjacent-deck identities.
