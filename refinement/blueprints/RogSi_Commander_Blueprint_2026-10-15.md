# Rograkh, Son of Rohgahh // Silas Renn, Seeker Adept — Commander-First Blueprint

Deck: #47 of 62

## Identity

**Natural ceiling: Power Bracket 5 — cEDH.**

This deck is the classic **RogSi turbo-naus / Underworld Breach** archetype. The partner pair is intentionally asymmetric rather than a 50/50 build-around:

- **Rograkh** is the active engine. A zero-mana commander turns on Mox Amber, Springleaf Drum, Paradise Mantle, Culling the Weak, Infernal Plunge, Diabolic Intent, Deflecting Swat, Fierce Guardianship, Deadly Rollick, Jeska's Will, and Flare of Duplication with unusually low setup cost.
- **Silas Renn** supplies blue-black color identity and secondary value: deathtouch matters in stalled boards, and combat damage can recur high-impact artifacts such as Wishclaw Talisman, Vexing Bauble, Defense Grid, Grinding Station, or fast mana. The strongest RogSi games frequently never cast Silas; that asymmetry is part of this pair's established competitive identity, not a failure to build around a commander.

## Primary game plan

1. Convert Rograkh plus zero/one-mana artifacts and rituals into early mana advantage.
2. Resolve a major reload: Ad Nauseam, Necropotence, Necrodominance, Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, or a wheel.
3. Force a compact win through proactive protection such as Defense Grid, Hexing Squelcher, Samut, or Vexing Bauble sequencing.
4. Primary deterministic win packages:
   - Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation / Tainted Pact.
   - Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze, with Grinding Station/zero-mana artifacts providing an alternate Breach engine.
5. Final Fortune and Warrior's Oath convert end-step reloads and unsafe turbo windows into another turn to finish the game.

## 2026 metagame reconciliation

Fresh six-month RogSi tournament data on 2026-10-15 contains 1,767 parsed lists. The traditional turbo architecture remains dominant: Underworld Breach 99.9%, Ad Nauseam 97.1%, Final Fortune 98.4%, Valley Floodcaller 98.4%, Hexing Squelcher 98.0%, and Mnemonic Betrayal 92.2%.

A newer artifact/Monolith branch is real and has produced tournament wins, but it is treated as a metagame variant rather than a reason to overwrite this deck's established turbo identity. The final candidate retains Grim Monolith, Grinding Station, and the artifact fast-mana spine without converting to a Bolas's Citadel/keys/large-artifact shell.

## Commander-native cards preserved deliberately

- Culling the Weak / Infernal Plunge / Diabolic Intent: Rograkh is zero-mana sacrifice material.
- Mox Amber / Springleaf Drum / Paradise Mantle: Rograkh becomes immediate mana infrastructure.
- Deflecting Swat / Fierce Guardianship / Deadly Rollick: commander-presence conditions are nearly free to satisfy.
- Flare of Duplication: Rograkh is a red creature available from the command zone.
- Jeska's Will: both modes become available with a commander in play.
- Wishclaw Talisman / Vexing Bauble / Grinding Station: high-impact artifacts also preserve Silas's secondary recursion relevance.
