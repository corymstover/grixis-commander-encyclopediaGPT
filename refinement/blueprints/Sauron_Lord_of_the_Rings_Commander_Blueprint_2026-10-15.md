# Sauron, Lord of the Rings — Commander Blueprint

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
Deck: #49 of 62

## Commander-first identity

Sauron is an eight-mana Grixis reanimator commander whose defining value is attached to **casting Sauron**: amass Orcs 5, mill five cards, then return a creature card from the controller's graveyard to the battlefield. The deck should therefore maximize:

1. **Reliable early and repeated Sauron casts** through fast mana, efficient rocks, and commander-tax mitigation.
2. **Deliberate graveyard setup** so Sauron's cast trigger has a premium creature ready to return.
3. **High-impact creatures with immediate ETB/static value** so a single cast trigger materially changes the game.
4. **Secondary reanimation density** so the deck functions when Sauron is unavailable and can chain value after his trigger.
5. **LOTR/Ring/Orc identity** through Nazgûl, Call of the Ring, the Army package, Sauron variants, and thematic graveyard pieces.

## Natural ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

The commander is expensive and its engine is fundamentally value/reanimation rather than a compact cEDH win package. The strongest realistic build should accelerate Sauron aggressively and maximize reanimation quality without replacing the commander with a generic Grixis combo shell.

## Primary game plan

- Early turns: fix colors, accelerate, and place premium creatures or Anger/Wonder in the graveyard.
- Midgame: use cheap reanimation to establish pressure/value or cast Sauron ahead of curve.
- Sauron cast: amass 5, mill 5, and return the best legal creature in the graveyard; the trigger is valuable even if the commander spell is later countered.
- Follow-through: use repeatable/secondary reanimation, haste/evasion from Anger/Wonder, and the Orc Army/Ring package to convert the board into lethal pressure.

## Candidate priorities

- Fast mana that specifically solves the eight-mana commander bottleneck.
- Two-mana or cheaper deterministic reanimation.
- Graveyard creatures that create a second reanimation event or immediate value.
- Free protection for tap-out Sauron/reanimation turns.
- Mana-base upgrades that improve repeated commander casting without reducing land count.
- Preserve the Nazgûl/Ring package; reject unnecessary generic wheel/combo or unrelated high-cost value packages.
