## Identity

**Natural ceiling: Power Bracket 5 — cEDH.**

Primary identity: **Mayhem / Graveyard Casting / Turbo Combo**.

Norman Osborn is a two-mana unblockable setup commander. Combat damage connives, placing chosen nonlands into the graveyard while sculpting the hand. His sorcery-speed transform creates Green Goblin, whose two linked abilities define the deck:

1. spells cast from the graveyard cost `{2}` less, reducing only generic mana; and
2. every nonland card in the graveyard has mayhem with mayhem cost equal to its mana cost, allowing a card discarded that turn to be cast from the graveyard while normal timing rules still apply.

The deck therefore wants cheap or repeatable discard outlets, graveyard-cast payoffs, timing enablers, fast mana, and compact cEDH finishes. Generic cEDH cards are appropriate here because tournament evidence supports a real cEDH ceiling, but the commander must materially improve the deck's resource conversion rather than merely sit beside a generic Grixis shell.

## Commander-native engine priorities

- **Discard into Mayhem:** Careful Study, Faithless Looting, Frantic Search, Psychic Frog, Shadow of the Goblin, Unstable Experiment, Big Score, Dark Deal, Attunement, Breakthrough, Ghostly Pilferer, Tolarian Winds, Artist's Talent, Gamble, Reckless Handling, LED, and the Norman front-face connive trigger.
- **Cost compression:** Green Goblin reduces graveyard spells by `{2}` generic; Artist's Talent level 2 can reduce noncreature spells by another `{1}` generic. This makes otherwise medium-rate spells materially different after transformation.
- **Timing expansion:** Borne Upon a Wind and Valley Floodcaller let mayhem-enabled noncreature spells obey flash timing rather than being stranded by their printed card type on an opponent's turn.
- **Compact wins:** Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation/Tainted Pact; Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze; Underworld Breach + Grinding Station + Mox Amber as a self-mill/mana engine with exact resource requirements preserved in rules documentation.
- **Artifact access:** Reckless Handling, Beseech the Mirror, Wishclaw Talisman, Demonic/Vampiric/Imperial/Mystical tutors, and Gamble increase access to LED, Grinding Station, Mox Amber, fast mana, or protection.

## Identity constraints

- Do **not** restore Necropotence merely because it is a powerful Grixis cEDH staple. Whenever you discard, Necropotence triggers and attempts to exile that discarded card from the graveyard when its trigger resolves. Because mayhem still obeys timing rules, this sharply suppresses the deck's normal sorcery-speed discard-then-cast pattern; only spells that can legally be cast before the Necropotence trigger resolves can escape that tension. The card is therefore a poor fit for this commander-first build despite its generic power.
- Do **not** replace commander-native discard engines solely because their global cEDH inclusion is lower. Green Goblin changes their effective costs and graveyard utility.
- Do **not** overfill the deck with five-mana discard payoffs or combat-only value cards. The cEDH ceiling requires cheap setup and compact wins.
- Preserve a real commander-native package even while retaining Oracle/Consultation and Breach lines.
