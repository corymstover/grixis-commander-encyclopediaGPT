# Davros, Dalek Creator — Commander-First Refinement Blueprint

Date: 2026-10-13  
Baseline: frozen `GrixisFINALTXT60--2026.zip` / `Davros__Dalek_Creator.txt`

## Commander identity
Davros is a four-mana Grixis artifact creature with menace. At each end step, if an opponent lost at least 3 life that turn, Davros creates a 3/3 black Dalek artifact creature token with menace. Each opponent who lost at least 3 life that turn then chooses between allowing the controller to draw a card or discarding a card.

## Primary plan
- Cause repeatable life loss of at least 3 to turn on Davros at the end step.
- Use the resulting Dalek artifact-creature tokens to build a threatening artifact army.
- Maintain discard pressure and punishment through Bottomless Pit, Oppression, Waste Not, Liliana's Caress, Geth's Grimoire, and The Rack.
- Use artifact-copy/token engines and efficient interaction to protect tempo and convert the board into a win.

## Refinement decision
**Add The Master, Gallifrey's End; cut Steel Overseer.**

The Master converts deaths of nontoken artifact creatures into a second opponent-facing choice: lose 4 life or let the Davros deck create a token copy of the deceased card. That is commander-adjacent value from artifact-creature losses and adds resilience to removal and sweepers. It also fits the existing artifact-creature density and the deck's life-loss/discard pressure.

Steel Overseer is a real tribal payoff: tapping it grows every artifact creature by a +1/+1 counter. The cut sacrifices repeatable go-wide scaling. It is selected because the baseline already has token production and artifact support, while The Master adds a distinct value engine that can rebuild after artifact-creature deaths and create additional pressure.

## Risks and limitations
- The Master costs four mana and is not itself an artifact creature, so it does not trigger its own ability.
- The trigger requires a **nontoken artifact creature** you control to die; ordinary token deaths do not qualify.
- The deck has no dedicated sacrifice outlet in the baseline, so value is strongest with Ashad's casualty costs, opposing removal, and sweepers rather than guaranteed every turn.
- This is a role/synergy judgment, not a measured win-rate claim.
