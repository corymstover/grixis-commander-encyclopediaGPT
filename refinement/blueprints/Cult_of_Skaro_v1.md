# Cult of Skaro — Commander-First Refinement Blueprint

Date: 2026-10-13  
Baseline: frozen `GrixisFINALTXT60--2026.zip` / `Cult_of_Skaro.txt`

## Commander identity
Cult of Skaro is a four-mana 4/4 legendary artifact creature — Dalek. Whenever it attacks, one of four effects is chosen at random: put a +1/+1 counter on each artifact creature you control, draw two cards, create a 3/3 black Dalek artifact creature token with menace, or make each opponent lose 4 life.

## Primary plan
- Protect and repeatedly attack with Cult of Skaro to generate a random but consistently high-impact artifact-creature payoff.
- Develop a wide artifact-creature/Dalek/Cyberman board, then scale it with Steel Overseer, Master of Etherium, The Cyber-Controller, and the commander's counter mode.
- Leverage artifact token/copy effects, including Brudiclad, The Master, Multiplied, Irenicus's Vile Duplication, Nanogene Conversion, and the clone package.
- Maintain efficient interaction and artifact-centric draw/ramp so the deck can keep attacking without losing tempo.

## Refinement decision — CULT-001
**Add Ashad, the Lone Cyberman; cut Skorpekh Lord.**

Ashad grants casualty 2 to the first nonlegendary artifact spell cast each turn. The deck has a high density of artifact creatures and artifact token generators, so this can copy a meaningful artifact spell and turn the copy into an artifact token. The sacrifice also grows Ashad, and the deck can often offer a 2-power-or-greater creature such as a Dalek token or other artifact creature.

Skorpekh Lord is a real artifact-creature anthem, granting other artifact creatures +1/+0 and menace, with unearth for resilience. It overlaps with the deck's other board-wide payoffs (Steel Overseer, Master of Etherium, The Cyber-Controller, and Cult's own counter mode), while Ashad adds a distinct artifact-spell-copy engine and a Doctor Who Cyberman that directly reinforces the deck's identity.

## Candidates considered
- The Cyber-Controller (rank 1): already included and central to the artifact-creature plan.
- Ashad, the Lone Cyberman (rank 2): selected.
- Cybermat (rank 3): on-theme and rewards wide artifact attacks, but less broadly impactful than Ashad's artifact-spell-copy engine.
- Death in Heaven (rank 4): graveyard/exile value, but less direct to the baseline's artifact-copy plan.
- The Master, Gallifrey's End (rank 9): valuable death-trigger option, but it requires nontoken artifact creatures to die and is not itself an artifact creature; deferred rather than adding another four-mana engine.

## Limits
This is a commander-specific role and synergy judgment, not a measured win-rate claim. The casualty copy is not cast; it is created on the stack, and an artifact spell copy becomes a token as it resolves.
