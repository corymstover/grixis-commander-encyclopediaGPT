# Nathan Drake, Treasure Hunter — Commander-First Blueprint v1

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
Deck: #39 of 62

## Commander reward

Nathan Drake rewards **attacking with Nathan himself**, because each attack exiles the top card of every player's library and permits one spell among those cards to be cast during the trigger's resolution. His static ability fixes mana for spells the controller does not own and for activated abilities of permanents the controller controls but does not own.

The deck therefore wants to maximize four things:

1. **Safe and immediate Nathan attacks** — haste, protection, and low-opportunity-cost evasion keep the attack trigger available.
2. **More Nathan attack triggers** — passive trigger multiplication and additional combats turn the commander into a repeatable multi-library selection engine.
3. **Mana available inside combat / during trigger resolution** — the stolen spell must still be paid for, so mana that exists before or during combat is disproportionately valuable.
4. **Payoffs for casting cards from exile or cards not owned** — copied spells, Treasures, damage, cascade, and theft payoffs convert Nathan's one-spell permission into a larger advantage engine.

## Primary game plan

Accelerate Nathan onto the battlefield, protect him, and attack as early and repeatedly as possible. Use Nathan's trigger and the surrounding theft suite to convert opponents' libraries into cards, then use Don Andres, Passionate Archaeologist, Nalfeshnee, Wild-Magic Sorcerer, Prosper, Tasha, and related payoffs to compound each stolen cast.

The refined list emphasizes **attack-trigger scaling** rather than merely adding more generic theft effects:

- **Roaming Throne** chooses Rogue and causes Nathan's attack ability to trigger an additional time.
- **Firebender Ascension** charges from attacking-creature triggers and, once online, copies Nathan's attack trigger.
- **Karlach, Fury of Avernus** creates a second combat and untaps Nathan on the first combat each turn.
- **Fire Lord Azula** supplies combat mana and copies a spell Nathan casts while Azula is attacking.
- **Fire Nation Palace** gives Nathan firebending 4 so mana is available before Nathan's attack trigger resolves into a spell-casting decision.
- **Arena of Glory** can give a newly cast Nathan haste while contributing red mana.

## Identity distinction

This is not a generic Grixis exile-goodstuff or theft-control deck. Nathan specifically compresses three actions into one attack trigger: multi-library exile, immediate spell selection, and permission to cast. The optimized shell is built to make that particular trigger happen more often and to make the unusual **cast-during-resolution combat window** reliable.

Other Grixis theft decks can generate stolen cards without their commander. This deck is strongest when Nathan himself attacks multiple times and turns each combat into a one-spell heist from four libraries.

## Natural power ceiling

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Nathan can generate substantial card/tempo advantage and can create explosive combat turns with trigger doubling, extra combats, spell-copy payoffs, fast mana, and premium interaction. The deck does not require a generic Thassa's Oracle / Underworld Breach cEDH shell to function, and forcing that package would reduce commander identity rather than accurately represent Nathan's natural ceiling.

## Refinement constraints

- Prefer cards that multiply Nathan's attack trigger, improve the cast-during-resolution mana window, or reward stolen/exile casts.
- Do not add theft cards merely because they are popular; compare them against the existing theft density and the actual weakest slot.
- Do not over-invest in combat-damage-only evasion: Nathan triggers when declared as an attacker, not when he deals damage. Evasion matters primarily to preserve Nathan for repeated attacks.
- Preserve enough cheap interaction and protection to resolve and attack with a three-color commander.
- Preserve a fetchable three-color mana base capable of supporting UBR on curve and combat-window spell payments.
