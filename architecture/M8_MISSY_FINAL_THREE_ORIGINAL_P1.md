# M8 — Missy, final original P1 W1/W2/W3 paid death and choice traces

The exact owner-approved 100-card Missy source SHA-256 is `f3f8ffa671cef5df9612a52ab0a5003931f2b71110f6d7fef45906b4f052f83d`, not a constructed new list. This suite covers the three remaining original M6 W1–W3 designs. The prior source-bound original K4–K7, E1–E2, F2–F3 dispositions are not double-counted.

## Real mechanism and boundaries

- **W1 (Missy + free sacrifice + reveal-only morph):** One physical Ruthless Ripper, an actual black card retained in hand to pay each reveal-only morph cost, live Missy and Viscera Seer. Returned Cyberman starts **face-down artifact**. Turn it face up to recover the original **nonartifact** Ruthless Ripper and cause its targeted 2 life-loss trigger, sacrifice the face-up creature, then Missy returns that SAME card face down. Twenty tested iterations reduce a 40-life target player to zero; without the reveal card, Missy or sacrifice outlet, the loop fails. Goblin Bombardment can add one paid targeted damage per legal sacrifice. The source contains Dragon's Eye Savants and Horde Ambusher too, but their individual face-up benefits and required reveal colors differ.
- **W2 (real draw ceiling and Ixidor paid recurrence):** Phyrexian Altar can produce one mana per face-up Ripper sacrificed while Grim Haruspex sees another actual nontoken death and **mandatorily draws one**. Eight such cycles draw eight and add eight mana; a ninth draw from an eight-card starting library loses instead of being called a safe infinite engine. Separately, an Ixidor activation pays `{2}{U}` to flip a legally returned Cyberman, Ashnod's Altar gains `{C}{C}` from the now nonartifact creature death, and Pitiless Plunderer's Treasure pays the next `{U}`. A 12-cycle finite trace preserves exactly two generic plus one blue of reusable resources under the stated board and response-free conditions. Missing pieces or absent initial blue stop it. Neither line is a complete 3-card stand-alone combo; a re-flippable real original creature remains necessary.
- **W3 (The Valeyard, villainous choices):** Missy's end-step each opponent makes a real choice between damage from each controlled artifact creature **or** giving Missy's controller a draw. The Valeyard means an **additional choice**, not forced additional damage: with three artifact Cybermen and three opponents, two damage choices each are six damage to each opponent, while choosing draw both times can instead produce six mandatory draws and **zero** damage (and may deck Missy's controller). Source-locked final 100 includes Cyberman Patrol, The Cyber-Controller and The Valeyard. Original M6 W3 also named Cybermen Squadron and Ashad, the Lone Cyberman; neither remains in this approved final 100 and neither is pretended present.

## Limits and provenance

- Every finite fixture assumes relevant pieces were legally assembled and survive opponent responses. A true indefinite loop requires repeatable ability transitions plus no introduced mandatory losing trigger; finite 100-card draw effects never count as a safe infinity. No multiplayer victory rate, resource guarantee or full Oracle layer engine is inferred.
- The original M6 source is `data/grixis/m6/test_blueprints/Missy.json`, transferred with exact-current owner-locked receipt `refinement/verification/m7_certification_transfer/Missy_f3f8ffa671cef5df.json`. Executable cases live in `tests/test_m8_missy_final_three_p1_loops.py` and must be anchored to a successful exact-head GitHub Actions run before being recorded in M8.
- Wizards' [Doctor Who set first look](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/a-first-look-at-magic-the-gathering-doctor-who) confirms villainous choice is opponent-choice; Wizards' [Morph reveal-cost design explanation](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/prose-and-khans-part-2-2014-09-22) explains the non-mana reveal morph cycle, including Ruthless Ripper, Dragon's Eye Savants and Horde Ambusher. These are background official sources, not a judge adjudication for every edge case.
