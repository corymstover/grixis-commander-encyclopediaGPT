## Essential strategic and rules corrections

1. **K4 / W2 — Gary/Mikaeus/Yawgmoth is not a self-contained three-card infinite.** Yawgmoth requires sacrificing *another* creature, and Gary's first undying return bears a +1/+1 counter so cannot undie again without resetting it. The current 100 contains a reusable non-Human partner, **Mayhem Devil**, which Mikaeus also grants undying. Alternating Gary and that partner lets Yawgmoth place a -1/-1 counter on the other creature to cancel its +1/+1 counter. The positive finite trace performs **11 paid Yawgmoth activations**, draws **11 actual cards**, pays **11 life** prior to 6 Gary ETB gains, and drains three opponents starting at 40 by a conservative minimum 7 devotion each Gary return. Six ETBs bring all three to -2; this is a modeled *finite lethal* sequence, not endless draws or an infinite-library claim. A missing fourth body or a library too small to satisfy mandatory draws fails adversarially. It is a real **four-plus-piece** supported route; the original shorthand must not be sold as only three pieces.
2. **K5 — outlet-free Redcap is possible with conditions.** Redcap's own ETB can target its 2-toughness self. If **Gev** supplies at least one +1/+1 counter (because an opponent lost life earlier this turn), that counter cancels the -1/-1 from persist, so Redcap can persist again after lethal self-damage. **Blood Artist** supplies opponent life loss on each resulting death. This is no-outlet death/drain, but is **not** independent of the Gev seed or Artist. Adversarially, no earlier opponent life loss stops the second reentry; no Artist means the self-destruction loop has no modeled drain payoff.
3. **W1 — actual repeatable damage line.** With **Metallic Mimic choosing Goblin**, **Murderous Redcap** and **Goblin Bombardment**, each free sacrifice deals 1 via Bombardment and a returned 2-power Redcap deals another 2 to the opponent. Persist -1 and Mimic +1 annihilate as SBAs before Redcap's ETB trigger resolves, restoring the counterless starting state. Four cycles take an opponent at 10 to -2 with no extra mana or drawn card. Removing Mimic permits at most one such cycle; removing Bombardment prevents the activation. This is a finite executable repeatability-and-lethal trace, not an adversarial multiplayer priority engine.
4. **K6 / E1 / F2 — end-step reality.** Sneak Attack's next-end-step sacrifice occurs when that next beginning happens; a Marchesa trigger generated from that death cannot return the card until the *following* end-step beginning. Death without a +1/+1 counter or absence of Marchesa *at death* produces no Marchesa return; removing Marchesa *after* its triggered ability exists does not cancel the delayed return. A card leaving its graveyard in between also defeats that particular return.
5. **K7 / E2 / F3 — actual costs and limits.** Flare of Denial's alternative payment must sacrifice a **nontoken blue creature**; one with a +1/+1 counter can be queued for Marchesa's next-end-step return. Altar and Bombardment costs require real fodder and have different payoff units. Exiling the creature or removing its +1/+1 counter at death prevents the relevant recursion.

## Release boundary

This test implements a deterministic **subsystem trace**, not a comprehensive implementation of Magic's priority/trigger ordering, mana infrastructure, zone replacement effects, opponent removal, other life-loss triggers, or randomized gameplay. Explicit fixtures assume creatures/permanents are already on the battlefield, legal ability targets survive until resolution and mana from other costs was paid when those costs are not the subject of the test. The Yawgmoth trace conservatively ignores Mayhem Devil's *additional* damage trigger; it does not conjure extra card draw or free library cards. A deck can be commander-first and contain backup noncommander combos; these tests do not override the existing 62 whole-list strategic reviews.

Test-source Git blob, exact head commit and successful GH Actions URL must be pinned by a **subsequent separate evidence-register PR** before these cases move to `CURRENT_SHA_TESTED`. The other 84 pending original P1 cases and M8 final owner-acceptance lock are unaffected until independently dispositioned.
