## Authority and limits

- [Wizards of the Coast, Mystery Booster Commander Edition release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/mystery-booster-commander-edition-release-notes): official published Jester text states the opponent-library exile source condition, ending at the next turn, ordinary timing and costs, and spending mana of any type. The protected Jester and Dragon's Smile are project-only future-release exceptions **as of October 28, 2026**; this suite does not claim official Commander legality early.
- [Wizards of the Coast, Breach/Grinding Station/Oracle combo overview](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/metagame-mentor-modern-with-dominaria-united): independent background on paid self-mill conversion, not proof of this exact stack or match-winning rate.
- Test: `tests/test_m8_madcap_original_p1_win_and_hate.py`. It uses finite, source-SHA-locked card and zone inventories and an expressly hypothetical opponent card; it is not a general rules engine, matchup predictor, or substitute for exact-head CI and all M8 remaining cases.
