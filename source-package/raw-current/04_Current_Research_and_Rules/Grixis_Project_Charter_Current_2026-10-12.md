# Grixis Commander Encyclopedia — Project Charter & Permanent Rules

## Mission
Build a collection of 62 distinct Grixis Commander decks, one commander-first, identity-driven deck per eligible commander, not a single generic Grixis goodstuff shell.

## Permanent deckbuilding priorities
1. **Commander identity first.** Every deck showcases its commander's defining mechanics, flavor and strategic play pattern.
2. **Maximum realistic optimization.** Optimize the commander's own strategy within its natural power ceiling; do not force every commander into a higher bracket.
3. **Complete verification before approval.** Exactly 100 cards including commander, subject to documented companion treatment; verify singleton, color identity, current Oracle text, rules interactions, official legality and project-authorized exceptions.
4. **No forced homogenization.** Shared staples must improve the specific deck; don't make every deck the same combo/control shell.
5. **Preserve approved decks.** Moving on means the prior deck is approved/finished. Never reopen a locked deck unless explicitly instructed.
6. **Rankings are candidates, not commands.** Popularity, EDHREC lift, and automated rank score are evidence aids, not proof of power or automatic inclusions.

## Official legality vs project exceptions
- Lutri may be a companion in the designated Bjorna/Cecily deck by explicit project policy, despite the official companion restriction.
- Trade Secrets and Yawgmoth's Bargain are retained in the designated Bjorna/Cecily deck under explicit project exception.
- Nazgûl's copy limit follows its own card text (up to nine).
- The Madcap Jester and Dragon's Smile future-release/project planning exception is retained.
- Tresserhorn's Lord, Returned future-release/project treatment is retained.
- The Starscape Cleric BLB #116 identity override disambiguates a non-token card from a similarly named token.
- Other documented exceptions remain unchanged unless the user explicitly changes them.

## Output format
When presenting a complete decklist, use one copy-paste list: quantity prefix, one card per line, commander first labelled `[Commander]`; no categories, set codes, or role tags unless requested.

## Source-of-truth hierarchy
1. Latest supplied `GrixisFINAL` CSV/TXT exports for current list membership.
2. Latest project-approved individual deck checkpoint/update record for approval and strategic rationale.
3. Current tracker and fresh mechanical reconciliation for current list metrics.
4. Older v8.1.4 audit and candidate data as inherited research context only; never silently treat them as current for changed card lists.
5. Scryfall/current official Commander pages for fresh Oracle, identity, and legality checks when undertaken.
